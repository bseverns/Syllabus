# Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:
1. Model and modify designs for additive and subtractive fabrication.
2. Translate geometry into machine‑readable toolpaths and read basic G‑code.
3. Operate machines safely with proper setup, zeroing, and verification.
4. Combine parts into hybrid assemblies and iterate for fit and function.
5. Document your process and articulate design decisions.

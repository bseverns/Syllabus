# Final Project Guidelines

**Scope:** A small but complete object that requires both additive and subtractive steps.  
**Timeline:** 2 sessions for design, 1 for fabrication, 1 for finishing & documentation.  
**Checklist:**
- Clear intent and constraints
- Parametric model with at least two variables
- CAM/Slicer settings recorded
- Evidence of iteration
- Safe, supervised fabrication
- Reflection with photos

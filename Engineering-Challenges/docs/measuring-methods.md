# Measuring Methods
- **Torque (stall):** torque = radius (m) × mass (kg) × 9.81.
- **Speed:** count dial marks per 10 s → RPM; compare to calculated ratio.
- **Lift work:** work ≈ mass × g × height; estimate handle energy from torque × turns.
- **Repeatability:** % of trials within spec; report mean ± std dev.

# Course Outline — Engineering Challenges with 3D Printed Mechanisms (10–12 weeks)

**A/B block each session**
- **Block A (35–45 min):** Concept/lecture, live demo, planning.
- **Block B (35–45 min):** Build/print/test iterations (use rigs), documentation.

## Weeks
1. **Design for Motion** — Clearances, tolerances, fasteners; print a bearing & snap-fit coupon.
2. **Gears & Ratios** — Spur + rack; compute and test reduction/speed.
3. **Cams & Followers** — Profiles (snail, heart), motion graphs; automata base.
4. **Ratchets & Pawls** — One-way motion; torque tradeoffs; anti-backdrive.
5. **Four-Bar Linkages** — Path design; coupler curves; simple gripper.
6. **Geneva Indexing** — Stepwise rotation; dwell; exhibit pointers.
7. **Compliant Mechanisms** — Living hinges, flexures; fatigue/lifetime.
8. **Transmissions** — Belt/chain approximations; simple gear trains.
9. **Integration Sprint** — Assemble multi-mechanism demo; test height/lift/force.
10–12. **Capstone Challenge** — Student-selected mechanism solving a defined task.

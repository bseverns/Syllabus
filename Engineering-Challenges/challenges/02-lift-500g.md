# Challenge 02 — Lift 500 g to 30 cm

**Goal:** Lift a 500 g mass vertically by 30 cm with a hand crank in ≤ 15 seconds.  
**Constraints:** Use ratchet/anti-backdrive or brake; printed spool; safety shield around spool.

**Evidence:** video, torque at handle (torque rig), spool diameter, turns required.

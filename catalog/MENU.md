# Workshop Menu

Hands-on creative technology experiences, selected from the full curriculum archive. Equipment and readiness labels are for internal deployment planning; partners pick the experience.

## Grades 3–7

### Build Your Block

Build a neighborhood invention for real people with cardboard, light, motion, sound, sensing, and one chosen coding platform.

**Formats:** course  
**Screen load:** S0–S2  
**Equipment:** E0→E2 — Cardboard and simple circuits at minimum; limited shared devices for one coding platform.  
**Documentation:** GO-P — Complete delivery package; choose the site platform and complete room/material preflight.  
**Source:** [`PRIMARY/Build_Your_Block`](../PRIMARY/Build_Your_Block)

## Grades 3–5

### From Idea to 3D Print

Draw, measure, model, slice, print, and revise a useful object for someone else.

**Formats:** course  
**Screen load:** S2  
**Equipment:** E3→E3 — Device pairs, Tinkercad, an operational FDM printer queue, and facilitator-managed print operations.  
**Documentation:** GO-P — Full plans, print operations, handouts, rubrics, and safety materials; validate accounts and printer workflow locally.  
**Source:** [`PRIMARY/3D-Printing-Course-3-5`](../PRIMARY/3D-Printing-Course-3-5)

### Scratch Stories and Games

Create an animated two-level Scratch game with cutscenes, playtesting, and a clear ending.

**Formats:** course  
**Screen load:** S3  
**Equipment:** E2→E2 — Shared or individual Scratch-capable devices and printable build handouts.  
**Documentation:** GO — School-facing syllabus, minute-by-minute playbook, session plans, handouts, and starter-project recipe are present.  
**Source:** [`PRIMARY/Scratch-Game-Design-Animation_Grades3-5`](../PRIMARY/Scratch-Game-Design-Animation_Grades3-5)

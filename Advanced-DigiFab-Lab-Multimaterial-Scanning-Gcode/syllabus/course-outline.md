# Course Outline — Advanced Digital Fabrication Lab (14–16 weeks)

**A/B block per session**
- **Block A (40–45 min):** Concept, safety, live demo (ops/G‑code/scan).
- **Block B (40–45 min):** Lab shifts: print setup, maintenance, QC, documentation.

## Weeks
1. **Lab Onboarding** — Roles, safety, queue logs, calibration plan.
2. **Calibration & QC** — First‑layer, temp/retraction towers; baseline KPIs.
3. **Multi‑Material 101** — Color change via M600/pause; two‑color token.
4. **MMU/AMS Workflows** — Profiles, purge/waste, color maps, jams & recovery.
5. **Soluble Supports** — Interface layers; drying & storage; teardown.
6. **G‑code Basics** — Start/end; park positions; purge lines; intro macros.
7. **G‑code Inserts** — Conditional pauses for magnets/nuts/LEDs; jig inserts.
8. **Scanning I** — Photogrammetry planning; capture sets; image hygiene.
9. **Scanning II** — Mesh cleanup/decimation; scale & split for print.
10. **Hybrid Modeling** — Keys/bosses; scan + parametric mates.
11. **Farm Ops** — Parallel queues; uptime tactics; failure triage & incident reports.
12. **Advanced Slicing** — Modifiers/variable layer height; pressure advance notes.
13. **Capstone Proposal** — DFM, risk register, test plan, milestones.
14–16. **Capstone Build** — Print, test, iterate, and present portfolio.

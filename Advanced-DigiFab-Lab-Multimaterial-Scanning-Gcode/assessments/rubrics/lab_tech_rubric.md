# Rubric — Lab Technician (100 pts)
- **Safety & Protocol (25):** Follows checklists; PPE; clean workspace.
- **Maintenance (25):** Performs nozzle/filament changes, bed prep, dries filament.
- **Queue Ops (20):** Accurately logs jobs; triages failures; communicates ETAs.
- **Documentation (20):** Photos, settings, rationales; clear incident reports.
- **Teamwork (10):** Supports peers; hands off shifts effectively.

# Syllabus (Grades 3–5, 11 × 90min)

## Big Ideas
- **Make to learn:** knowledge emerges through building and testing.
- **Iterate kindly:** feedback is a gift; improvement is expected.
- **Design for others:** our capstone serves a real person.

## Weekly Arc (Headlines)
1. Hello, Tinkercad! + nameplate fast-win
2. Measure & scale + key tag submission
3. Tolerance + spinning top / peg toy
4. Strength & slicing basics (**Digital Factory**)
5. Tiny Museum remix (attribution)
6. Useful-in-60-mins challenge (optimize)
7. Articulated chain critter (clearances)
8. 2D→3D badge magnet (SVG import)
9. Iterate/Improve day (compare prints)
10. Capstone Part 1: interview & prototype
11. Capstone Part 2: finalize and celebrate

## Grading/Feedback (Kid-Friendly)
- **Daily green checks (✓):** on-time, safe, kind, trying.
- **Project stars (★):** planning, making, reflecting.
- **Capstone badge:** complete, thoughtful, for a real user.

## Materials
- Computers with Tinkercad accounts (teacher-managed class code)
- Calipers (5–10 shared), rulers, scrap paper
- MakerBot SKETCH printers with PLA
- Tape, post-its, markers
- Safety glasses for demo moments

# Family Letter

Dear Families,

Your student is learning 3D design and printing with Tinkercad and MakerBot SKETCH printers. We focus on safe tool use, creativity, and designing for others. Most prints are short (≤ 60 minutes) to keep things fair. Students bring home 2–4 small prints across the course, including a final capstone made for a real person.

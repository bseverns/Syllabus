# Imaging Otherwise

Images shape truth; let's warp them with care.

This folder is a **course stack** for an undergraduate studio–seminar that treats imaging as a set of technical pipelines, social agreements, and political decisions. Students will question visual defaults, build alternative imaging tools, craft speculative visuals, and share their research openly.

## What's here

- `syllabus.md` — full course description, outcomes, and week-by-week outline.
- `assignments/`
  - `process-log.md` — ongoing documentation of experiments and final project work.
  - `studies-and-experiments.md` — menu of low-stakes weekly studies.
  - `final-project-imaging-otherwise.md` — capstone brief and public share-out.
- `reading-media.md` — anchor texts, media, and tool references.
- `instructor-notes.md` — pacing notes, assessment mix, access practices.

Drop this folder into the root of the `Syllabus` repo and wire it into your catalog as another imaging / critical-design course.

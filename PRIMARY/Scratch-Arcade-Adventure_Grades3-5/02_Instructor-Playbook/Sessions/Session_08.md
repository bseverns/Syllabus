# Session 08: Juice, Feedback, and Game Feel

**Mission:** improve how the game feels without changing the whole design.

## Demo

- sound effects
- costume change on hit
- message popups
- small animation for win, lose, or power-up

## Build goals

- add at least three feedback improvements
- make the player notice important moments
- keep feedback helpful, not noisy

## Checkpoints

- hit feedback exists
- win or stage-clear feedback exists
- one polish move supports understanding

## Extensions

- screen shake imitation
- animated title
- combo or bonus text

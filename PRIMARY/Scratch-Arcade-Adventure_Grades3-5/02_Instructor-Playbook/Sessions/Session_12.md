# Session 12: Arcade Showcase + Creator Talk

**Mission:** share the final game and reflect like a designer.

## Build of the day

- no new giant features
- fix tiny bugs only
- focus on sharing clearly

## Showcase flow

1. students load published projects
2. arcade walk with partner play
3. short creator talk:
   - what is the goal?
   - what mechanic are you proud of?
   - what bug did you solve?

## Success signs

- students can explain their systems
- players understand the game
- projects feel more polished and purposeful than Week 1

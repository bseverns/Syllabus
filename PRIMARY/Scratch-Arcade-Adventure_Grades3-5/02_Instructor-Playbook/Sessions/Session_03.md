# Session 03: Enemy Patterns That Feel Fair

**Mission:** add an enemy or hazard with a readable movement pattern.

## Demo

- patrol left and right
- chase the player slowly
- spin, bounce, or move on a timer

## Build goals

- add one enemy pattern
- if the player gets hit, health changes once, not ten times
- show some feedback when the player is hit

## Checkpoints

- enemy behavior is visible and consistent
- health or setback system works
- hit feedback exists

## Extensions

- second enemy pattern
- safe zone
- moving hazard with a predictable rhythm

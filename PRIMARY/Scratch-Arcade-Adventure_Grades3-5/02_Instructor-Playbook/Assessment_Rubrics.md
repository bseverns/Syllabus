# Assessment Rubrics

## Weekly milestone lens

Ask:

- does the mechanic work?
- can the student explain it?
- can another student play it?

## Final project rubric

### Game systems

- title and start flow work
- HUD updates correctly
- stages or waves progress correctly

### Challenge design

- hazards or enemies are readable
- difficulty is fair enough to learn
- the final challenge feels different from the beginning

### Polish and feedback

- the player can tell when they got hit, won, or found a power-up
- instructions are visible and useful

### Debugging and revision

- student used playtest notes
- student fixed at least one real bug

### Creator talk

- student can explain one mechanic and one design choice clearly

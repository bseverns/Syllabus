# Classroom Routines

## Launch phrase

"Hands off controls. Eyes up. Today we are building one new system."

## Save rule

Students save:

- before every playtest
- before every reset break
- before the end of class

## Partner help rule

Students should:

1. point to the sprite
2. point to the script
3. say what they expected
4. change one thing
5. retest

## Noise supports

- visible timer
- visible noise scale
- one reset stretch between sprints
- no demo longer than 7 minutes

## Share routine

Students share:

- what mechanic they built
- what still breaks
- what they will try next

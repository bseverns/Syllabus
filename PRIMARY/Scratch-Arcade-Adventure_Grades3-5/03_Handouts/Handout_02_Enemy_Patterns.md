# Handout 02 — Enemy Pattern Recipes

## Pattern A: Patrol

- go left
- if edge, turn around
- repeat forever

## Pattern B: Chase

- point toward player
- move a little
- wait a tiny bit

## Pattern C: Pop-up hazard

- hide
- wait
- show
- become dangerous
- hide again

## Question for designers

What makes your enemy feel fair instead of random?

# Handout 03 — Power-Ups + HUD Recipes

## Good HUD ideas

- score
- health
- energy
- timer
- keys collected

## Good power-up ideas

- speed boost
- shield
- double score
- freeze enemies
- unlock a door

## Important reminder

A power-up should:

- be easy to notice
- change the rules
- turn off or be used up in a clear way

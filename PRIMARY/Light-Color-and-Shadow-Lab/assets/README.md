## Shadow-puppet photographs

Use in Session 3 to notice character shapes and poses before learners invent their own scenes. These photographs show puppets themselves, not their projected shadows or instructions to copy a tradition.

- [`yunnan-shadow-puppets.jpg`](yunnan-shadow-puppets.jpg) — “Shadown puppets - Yunnan Provincial Museum - DSC02001,” Daderot, [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shadown_puppets_-_Yunnan_Provincial_Museum_-_DSC02001.JPG); released by the photographer into the public domain; unmodified. Alt: Two colorful shadow-puppet figures displayed at the Yunnan Provincial Museum, one reaching upward with a long staff.
- [`wayang-kulit.jpg`](wayang-kulit.jpg) — “Wayang kulit 01,” Joe Mabel, [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wayang_kulit_01.jpg); [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/); unmodified. Alt: A Javanese wayang kulit puppet with a jointed arm, elaborate costume, and a long handle.

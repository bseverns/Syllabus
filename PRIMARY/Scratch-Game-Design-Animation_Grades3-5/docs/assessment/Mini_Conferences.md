# Mini-Conferences (2 minutes each)

Use these in Weeks 4, 7, 10, and 12.

## The 2-minute structure
1) **Show** (20s): “Show me one thing that works.”
2) **Name** (20s): “That’s your core loop.”
3) **Choose** (30s): “Pick your next step: story / challenge / polish.”
4) **Plan** (30s): “What will you build in the next 5 minutes?”
5) **Commit** (20s): Student repeats the plan. Teacher gives a checkmark.

## Prompts (kid-friendly)
- “What should the player do first?”
- “What happens when you win?”
- “Where does the cutscene go?”
- “What’s one bug you want to beat today?”
- “If your game was a snack, what flavor is it?”

## Teacher notes (keep it tiny)
- Strength: ________
- Next step: ________
- Student chose: story / challenge / polish

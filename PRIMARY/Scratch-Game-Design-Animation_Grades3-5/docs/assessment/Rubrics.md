# Rubrics (Student-Friendly)

We use a 4-level ladder:
1) **Seed** — I started it  
2) **Sprout** — it mostly works  
3) **Bloom** — it works and is clear  
4) **Shine** — it works, is clear, and has extra polish/originality  

## A. Game Build Rubric (Levels + Cutscenes)

| Category | Seed | Sprout | Bloom | Shine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Game runs** | Starts sometimes | Starts reliably | Runs reliably | Runs reliably + smooth flow |
| **Player control** | Player exists | Player can move | Player movement feels predictable | Movement feels good + intentional speed/animation |
| **Level 1** | One room | One room + goal idea | Clear goal + win moment | Clear goal + satisfying feedback |
| **Cutscene** | Cutscene idea | Cutscene plays | Cutscene is readable (story/acting) | Cutscene has timing/animation polish |
| **Level 2** | Second room exists | Second room is playable | Level 2 adds a new idea | Level 2 adds a new idea + balanced difficulty |
| **Stability** | Often breaks | Breaks sometimes | Rarely breaks | Rarely breaks + clean resets |

## B. Designer Thinking Rubric

| Category | Seed | Sprout | Bloom | Shine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Explains the goal** | “I don’t know” | Partial | Clear and short | Clear + exciting |
| **Uses feedback** | Hears feedback | Tries one change | Makes a helpful change | Makes a helpful change + can explain why |
| **Debugging** | Needs full help | Finds bug with help | Finds and fixes a bug | Fixes bug + prevents it returning |

## C. Studio Habits Rubric

| Category | Seed | Sprout | Bloom | Shine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Save ritual** | Forgets | Sometimes saves | Saves reliably | Saves + helps others save |
| **Focus + flow** | Needs many resets | Works in short bursts | Works in steady sprints | Helps keep the room calm and moving |
| **Kind playtesting** | Hard to share | Shares with support | Gives kind feedback | Gives kind feedback + specific suggestions |
| **Collaboration** | Mostly solo | Shares tools with reminders | Shares tools and takes turns | Helps peers without taking over |

## Feedback prompts (Glow/Grow)
Use these sentences while you circulate:

### Glow (name the strength)
- “Your game teaches the player by showing, not telling.”
- “That cutscene timing makes the story easy to read.”
- “Nice job making the reset feel fair.”

### Grow (one next step)
- “Add a clearer win moment (sound/text/animation).”
- “Make Level 2 feel different by changing one rule.”
- “Hide the extra sprites when the backdrop changes.”

### Student choice (agency)
- “Do you want your next step to be **story**, **challenge**, or **polish**?”

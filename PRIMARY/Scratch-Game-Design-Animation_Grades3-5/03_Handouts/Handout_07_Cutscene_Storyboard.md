## Coding checklist
- ☐ Cutscene starts when Level 1 is complete
- ☐ Controls freeze (controlsOn = 0 or broadcast freeze)
- ☐ Cutscene uses waits (timing)
- ☐ Cutscene ends by switching to Level 2
- ☐ Controls unfreeze

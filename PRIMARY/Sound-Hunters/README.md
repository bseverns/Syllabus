# Sound Hunters

A four-session, screen-light pilot workshop for Pre-K–Grade 2. Learners listen, make safe Foley sounds, arrange a cue order, and perform a short sound story.

> **PILOT READY:** the package is complete enough for a lead facilitator to deliver. It remains off the public-ready shelf until one real delivery confirms timing, materials, sensory needs, and revision notes.

## Quick start

1. Read [SYLLABUS.md](SYLLABUS.md) and the [K–2 operating model](../K2-PILOT-OPERATING-MODEL.md).
2. Test every Foley object for safe volume and sharp edges; stage two or three sound trays plus a quiet corner.
3. Post the stop/listen signal and volume card before Session 1.
4. Run four 60-minute sessions; preserve the cue strip and one learner quote from the final share.

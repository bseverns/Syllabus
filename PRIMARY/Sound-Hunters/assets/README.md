# Sound Hunters visual assets

## A tray of story sounds

- **Creator and source:** original illustration created for this course by Hermes Agent (Nous Research); no third-party source material.
- **License:** repository license, [LICENSE.md](../../../LICENSE.md).
- **Local file:** [foley-tray.svg](foley-tray.svg); original, not modified from another work.
- **Alt text:** A paper cup, sealed paper bag, folded fabric, and wooden blocks on a tray invite ideas for story sounds.
- **Use and limits:** Session 2 imagination prompt after the facilitator's live quiet/medium/stop demonstration. It is not a required tray or safety approval; test actual objects for safe volume and edges and keep any beans or rice sealed inside the bag.

# Final Showcase Guide

## Goal
End the session with a public-facing celebration where students demonstrate their projects and talk about process, purpose, and learning.

In the intensive version of Build Your Block, the showcase should not appear suddenly in the final days. Soft-opening, station design, reset routines, and audience explanation should begin during Week 6.

## Showcase format options
- gallery walk
- live demo stations
- mini-stage presentations
- family night
- partner-site community open house

## Recommended components
- student/team project labels
- “how it works” cards
- project testing area
- photos from earlier weeks
- welcome sign
- student greeters or docents

## Before a project is showcase-ready

Check that:

- the main interaction works more than once
- the visitor can tell where to start
- the station has a reset routine
- labels support the project instead of doing all the explanation
- the team can explain the project without sounding over-scripted

## Logistics checklist
- tables and power
- extension cords if needed
- labels printed or handwritten
- tape / repair kit on hand
- reset instructions for each project
- staff roles assigned
- audience flow planned
- soft-opening or mock walkthrough completed in advance

## Student speaking prompts
- Welcome to our project.
- We made this because...
- It works by...
- The hardest part was...
- We changed...
- Next we would...

Students do not need to memorize these word for word. They are cue lines, not a script.

## Documentation
Capture:
- photos of students with projects
- close-ups of builds
- short quotes from students
- audience response notes
These become future program evidence and recruitment material.

Also capture:
- one full-station photo for each team
- one note about what held up well in public use
- one note about what needed reset or repair
- one note about what should be kept in the archive: photo set, labels, diagram, quote, or video

# Parent-Facing Description

## Full version

**Build Your Block** is a **7-week neighborhood invention intensive** for students in grades 3–7. Students build projects that light up, move, make sound, respond to touch or sensors, and sometimes use simple coding. They learn how to test ideas, fix what breaks, explain how something works, and turn an idea into something real.

What makes this class different is that students are building toward neighborhood projects from the beginning. They do not spend weeks doing disconnected activities and only later start the “real” project. From the first week, they are already asking: Who is this for? What place, memory, issue, joy, or future dream does it connect to? What would make someone want to use it, notice it, or remember it?

The course ends with a public showcase of neighborhood-rooted work. Students share what they built, how it works, what changed through testing, and why it matters.

This program is hands-on, process-driven, and welcoming to mixed experience levels. Smaller working builds count. Strong revision work counts. Clear explanation counts. The goal is confidence, care, creativity, and public-facing making.

## Short version

**Build Your Block** is a 7-week hands-on maker intensive where students build with circuits, motion, sound, sensing, cardboard construction, and simple coding, then turn those tools into neighborhood-inspired projects for a public showcase.

## Even shorter version

Build Your Block is a neighborhood maker intensive where students turn light, motion, sound, sensing, and simple coding into public-facing projects connected to the places and people around them.

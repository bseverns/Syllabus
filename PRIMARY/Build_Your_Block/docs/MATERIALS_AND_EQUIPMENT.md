# Materials and Equipment

## Core consumables
- copper tape
- coin cell batteries
- LEDs in assorted colors
- masking tape / painter's tape
- cardboard sheets and scraps
- construction paper
- markers, pens, pencils
- index cards
- glue sticks
- hot glue sticks
- zip ties
- brads / paper fasteners
- rubber bands
- popsicle sticks
- string / yarn
- aluminum foil
- recycled clean packaging materials

## Tools
- scissors
- rulers
- hole punches
- wire cutters / strippers
- low-temp hot glue guns
- cutting mats
- screwdrivers
- storage bins / trays

## Electronics / kits
- littleBits kits
- LEGO SPIKE Essential and/or Prime
- Makey Makey boards
- simple DC motors
- AA battery packs
- alligator clips
- buzzers / mini speakers
- simple push buttons
- basic light or touch sensors if available

## Computing
- laptops or Chromebooks
- stable charging access
- one chosen coding platform such as Scratch or SPIKE
- projector or display for demos

## Nice-to-have extras
- 3D printer access for a few custom parts
- label maker
- digital camera or tablet for documentation
- butcher paper for planning walls
- clipboards for reflection and sketching

## Supply philosophy
The center of the class should be rapid building and iteration, not waiting on expensive machines. Machines should support the course, not become the course.

In the intensive version, narrower tool choices are a strength. If a site uses coding, choose one platform and stay consistent across the 7 weeks.

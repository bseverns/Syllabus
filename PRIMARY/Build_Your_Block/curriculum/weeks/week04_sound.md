# Week 4: Sound

## Big idea
Technology can be expressive, noisy, playful, and musical.

## Learning goals
- understand sound as vibration and trigger
- build a simple sound-making or sound-reactive object
- use interfaces like touch or switches to control sound

## Warm-up prompt
What sounds belong to summer in Minneapolis?

## Mini-demo focus
Show an input/output chain that produces sound.

## Main build
littleBits instrument, Makey Makey touch instrument, or found-object sound machine

## Suggested session flow
- 15 min — arrival and warm-up
- 20 min — demo and guided exploration
- 60 min — build block
- 15 min — share-out and documentation
- 10 min — reflection and cleanup

## Materials
Use core materials from `docs/MATERIALS_AND_EQUIPMENT.md` plus any week-specific kits.

## Differentiation
- **Low-floor option:** simplify build to one core mechanism or one visible outcome
- **Stretch challenge:** Create an instrument with two sound options or a themed sound sculpture.
- **Team option:** allow pairs or small groups to divide structure / electronics / decoration / explanation

## Reflection prompt
How did your project invite someone to interact with it?

## Facilitator notes
- keep demonstration concrete and brief
- get materials into students' hands early
- capture at least one photo per table or team
- close by naming what students discovered, not just what they finished

# Week 14: Rehearsal and Polish

## Big idea
A project becomes stronger when its making, meaning, and demonstration are all practiced.

## Weekly learning goals
- practice short project presentations and demonstrations
- complete aesthetic polish and final fixes
- make each team ready to explain purpose, process, and use

## Weekly rhythm
- Day 1 — launch and explore
- Day 2 — build the core skill
- Day 3 — extend and personalize
- Day 4 — share, document, and connect

## Materials
Use core materials from `docs/MATERIALS_AND_EQUIPMENT.md` plus display supplies, fresh labels, cleanup materials, and printed presentation prompts.

## Day 1 — Launch and explore
### Warm-up prompt
How will you welcome a visitor to your table?

### Mini-demo focus
Model a short project talk that includes title, purpose, how it works, and one thing that changed during revision.

### Build activity
Students script a 30- to 60-second demo and identify any missing materials or unfinished surfaces.

### Exit question
What is your strongest sentence so far?

## Day 2 — Build the core skill
### Warm-up prompt
What visual details still make your project look unfinished?

### Mini-demo focus
How to polish without overbuilding: clean edges, clearer labels, better color choices, and neater wiring.

### Build activity
Students complete finish work and align the look of the project, label, and display materials.

### Exit question
What changed the feel of your project the most?

## Day 3 — Extend and personalize
### Warm-up prompt
What should visitors do first when they arrive?

### Mini-demo focus
Practice demo pacing, eye contact, turn-taking for teams, and a calm recovery if the project does not work instantly.

### Build activity
Students rehearse with peers, receive presentation notes, and revise their setup based on those notes.

### Exit question
What part of the demo still needs practice?

## Day 4 — Share, document, and connect
### Warm-up prompt
How do you present with confidence without memorizing every word?

### Mini-demo focus
Model a cue-card style presentation that sounds natural and flexible.

### Build activity
Students complete a full rehearsal with project, signage, and speaking parts in place.

### Exit question
What do you feel ready to share now?

## Differentiation
- **Low-floor option:** one clear demonstration and a short spoken or recorded explanation.
- **Stretch challenge:** add an audience participation moment or a second speaker role with smooth handoff.
- **Team option:** divide speaking into title/purpose, how-it-works, and revision story sections.

## Facilitator notes
- keep rehearsal supportive and matter-of-fact
- remind students that calm presentation often matters more than polished vocabulary
- photograph near-final work this week in case showcase day is busy

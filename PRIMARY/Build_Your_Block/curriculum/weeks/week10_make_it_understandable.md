# Week 10: Make It Understandable

## Big idea
Communication is part of the build.

## Learning goals
- add titles, labels, and explanations
- document process in simple language
- prepare for a public audience

## Warm-up prompt
If someone saw your project for ten seconds, what should they understand?

## Mini-demo focus
Strong examples of project labels and ‘how it works’ cards.

## Main build
Display elements, label cards, explanation diagrams, project refinements

## Suggested session flow
- 15 min — arrival and warm-up
- 20 min — demo and guided exploration
- 60 min — build block
- 15 min — share-out and documentation
- 10 min — reflection and cleanup

## Materials
Use core materials from `docs/MATERIALS_AND_EQUIPMENT.md` plus any week-specific kits.

## Differentiation
- **Low-floor option:** simplify build to one core mechanism or one visible outcome
- **Stretch challenge:** Create a diagram or visual story of how the project changes from input to output.
- **Team option:** allow pairs or small groups to divide structure / electronics / decoration / explanation

## Reflection prompt
What do you want people to notice first?

## Facilitator notes
- keep demonstration concrete and brief
- get materials into students' hands early
- capture at least one photo per table or team
- close by naming what students discovered, not just what they finished

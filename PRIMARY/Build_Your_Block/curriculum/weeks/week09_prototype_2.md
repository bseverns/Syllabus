# Week 9: Prototype 2

## Big idea
Failure is information, and revision makes the idea more real.

## Weekly learning goals
- test, debug, and revise a first prototype
- improve usability and technical reliability
- clarify team roles and short-term build priorities

## Weekly rhythm
- Day 1 — launch and explore
- Day 2 — build the core skill
- Day 3 — extend and personalize
- Day 4 — share, document, and connect

## Materials
Use core materials from `docs/MATERIALS_AND_EQUIPMENT.md` plus repair supplies, stronger tape and glue options, extra connectors, and test logs.

## Day 1 — Launch and explore
### Warm-up prompt
What broke first, what felt confusing, and what needs reinforcement?

### Mini-demo focus
Examples of stronger wiring, sturdier structure, and clearer interfaces.

### Build activity
Students revisit prototype notes, identify their top two problems, and make a repair plan.

### Exit question
Which problem matters most to solve first?

## Day 2 — Build the core skill
### Warm-up prompt
What counts as a successful test this week?

### Mini-demo focus
How to run a repeatable test for function, durability, or audience understanding.

### Build activity
Students rebuild or reinforce the most fragile part of the project and test it multiple times.

### Exit question
What changed after testing?

## Day 3 — Extend and personalize
### Warm-up prompt
Now that the project works better, what could make it feel more complete?

### Mini-demo focus
Ways to improve audience experience through layout, controls, or more intentional visual choices.

### Build activity
Students add a second round of features only after core repairs are stable.

### Exit question
Which revision made your idea easier to understand or use?

## Day 4 — Share, document, and connect
### Warm-up prompt
How do you talk about a mistake without apologizing for it?

### Mini-demo focus
Model a revision story: first version, problem found, change made, result.

### Build activity
Students present a revision snapshot with photos or sketches and update their project planner for week 10.

### Exit question
What did revision teach you that the first build could not?

## Differentiation
- **Low-floor option:** repair one major issue and document one successful test.
- **Stretch challenge:** improve both reliability and user experience in the same week.
- **Team option:** divide roles into tester, builder, and recorder so revisions are based on evidence.

## Facilitator notes
- do not let students skip testing in order to chase polish
- keep revision visible by saving scraps, sketches, or photos from earlier versions
- celebrate precise changes, not just dramatic ones

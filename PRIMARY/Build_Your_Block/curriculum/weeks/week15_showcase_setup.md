# Week 15: Showcase Setup

## Big idea
How a project is installed, labeled, powered, and reset shapes the audience experience.

## Weekly learning goals
- prepare the room, table setup, and project flow for visitors
- solve logistics for power, traffic, signage, and reset routines
- run a soft opening or full walkthrough before the final event

## Weekly rhythm
- Day 1 — launch and explore
- Day 2 — build the core skill
- Day 3 — extend and personalize
- Day 4 — share, document, and connect

## Materials
Use core materials from `docs/MATERIALS_AND_EQUIPMENT.md` plus tablecloths, extension cords if needed, display stands, tape, printed labels, and showcase maps.

## Day 1 — Launch and explore
### Warm-up prompt
What does a visitor need from the room before they even reach your project?

### Mini-demo focus
Examples of clear table layout, traffic flow, power routing, and sign placement.

### Build activity
Students sketch a showcase layout for their table or zone and list setup materials they still need.

### Exit question
What could go wrong during setup if you do not plan ahead?

## Day 2 — Build the core skill
### Warm-up prompt
Where should visitors stand, touch, or wait?

### Mini-demo focus
How to arrange interactive projects so use feels obvious and safe.

### Build activity
Students set up their display space, test power or batteries, and make a reset checklist for between visitors.

### Exit question
What made your setup easier for someone else to use?

## Day 3 — Extend and personalize
### Warm-up prompt
What does your display say about the kind of project this is?

### Mini-demo focus
Using color, labels, and arrangement to match the project's tone and purpose.

### Build activity
Students finish table visuals, place documentation artifacts, and do a walkthrough from a guest point of view.

### Exit question
What did you notice once you walked through like a visitor?

## Day 4 — Share, document, and connect
### Warm-up prompt
How should the room sound and feel when guests arrive?

### Mini-demo focus
Run a soft opening or mock showcase with small groups moving through the room.

### Build activity
Students host a practice walkthrough, troubleshoot setup issues, and finalize a day-of plan with assigned roles.

### Exit question
What still needs to happen before the final showcase?

## Differentiation
- **Low-floor option:** create a clear project station with one sign, one reset routine, and one speaking role.
- **Stretch challenge:** design a more immersive station with audience prompts, display layers, or docent roles.
- **Team option:** assign setup, visitor greeting, reset, and documentation jobs for the final event.

## Facilitator notes
- do not wait until the last day to solve power, labeling, or traffic issues
- rehearse the physical setup, not just the presentation
- keep an emergency fix kit ready with tape, batteries, glue, markers, and labels

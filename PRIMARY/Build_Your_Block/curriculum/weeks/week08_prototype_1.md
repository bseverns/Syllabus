# Week 8: Prototype 1

## Big idea
Build rough, build early, and learn from a first working version.

## Weekly learning goals
- translate sketches and proposal ideas into physical systems
- identify one core interaction or mechanism worth getting working first
- document technical questions and early obstacles

## Weekly rhythm
- Day 1 — launch and explore
- Day 2 — build the core skill
- Day 3 — extend and personalize
- Day 4 — share, document, and connect

## Materials
Use core materials from `docs/MATERIALS_AND_EQUIPMENT.md` plus project-specific kits, planning sheets, tape flags for labeling parts, and storage bins for in-progress work.

## Day 1 — Launch and explore
### Warm-up prompt
What is the smallest version of your idea that can work this week?

### Mini-demo focus
Prototype mindset: rough is allowed, but the test should answer a real question.

### Build activity
Students break their project into parts and choose one core function to prototype first.

### Exit question
What is the first thing your project needs to prove?

## Day 2 — Build the core skill
### Warm-up prompt
What part of your project seems most likely to fail first?

### Mini-demo focus
How to build a quick first version with labeled parts, visible wiring, and simple attachments.

### Build activity
Students build the first working version of the central mechanism, interaction, or structure.

### Exit question
What worked sooner than you expected?

## Day 3 — Extend and personalize
### Warm-up prompt
What does your prototype still need in order to feel like your original idea?

### Mini-demo focus
How to add one more layer without losing clarity: structure, decoration, second output, or cleaner interface.

### Build activity
Students add a second feature or refine the form so the project begins to communicate purpose and audience.

### Exit question
What still feels unfinished, unclear, or fragile?

## Day 4 — Share, document, and connect
### Warm-up prompt
How do you ask for feedback on a rough draft?

### Mini-demo focus
Model a feedback request framed around a real design question.

### Build activity
Students do a mid-prototype share, collect notes on what works and what confuses visitors, and log next steps for week 9.

### Exit question
What are you building next because of today's feedback?

## Differentiation
- **Low-floor option:** focus on one mechanism working reliably before adding decoration or extra features.
- **Stretch challenge:** get the core function working and connect it to a second system such as light plus sound or motion plus code.
- **Team option:** assign one student to structure, one to interaction, and one to test/document, then rotate.

## Facilitator notes
- insist on visible labels for parts and wires so troubleshooting stays manageable
- help students shrink oversized ambitions into testable chunks
- protect storage and reset time so projects survive across multiple days

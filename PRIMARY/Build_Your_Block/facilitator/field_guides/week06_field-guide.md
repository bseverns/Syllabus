# Week 6 Field Guide

## Focus
Observe real users, revise from evidence, and turn the project into a readable station.

## What absolutely must happen this week
- Every team runs at least one real user test.
- Every team makes at least one evidence-based revision.
- Every team creates a clear station plan with reset steps.

## What can be cut if time slips
- extra decoration
- second-round polish that does not improve use
- too many test questions

## Top 3 materials to prep first
- feedback forms or clipboards
- signage and station materials
- timers and reset tools

## Top 3 failure points to watch for
- builders over-explaining during tests
- revisions based on opinion instead of observed behavior
- station design left too late

## Room-shape advice
- set obvious testing paths
- create enough space for observers to watch without crowding
- mark where visitors stand, wait, or touch

## Mixed-age caution
Younger students need shorter test rounds and concrete roles. Older/faster students can sort tester patterns, improve waiting flow, or tighten hosting logic.

## Fatigue / reset note
Use the break block to sort what the room learned, not just to calm down. What did we see? What are we fixing? What can wait?

## What to photograph or capture
- one user interaction
- one confusion point
- one revision that came from testing
- one station sketch or setup photo

## What students should have by the end of the week
- a tested project
- a clearer station
- a hosting and reset plan for Week 7

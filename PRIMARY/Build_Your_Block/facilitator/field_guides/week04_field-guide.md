# Week 4 Field Guide

## Focus
Build the first working version fast and keep continuity stronger than polish.

## What absolutely must happen this week
- Every team gets a real prototype started.
- At least one core function works or gets very close.
- Every project leaves each session with a parking note.

## What can be cut if time slips
- decorative finish
- extra systems before the first works
- long whole-group discussion after the first session

## Top 3 materials to prep first
- team-specific project materials
- repair and battery supplies
- labels, flags, and parking cards

## Top 3 failure points to watch for
- teams trying to build the final version at once
- unlabeled parts and unreadable prototypes
- sprint 2 collapsing because materials are everywhere

## Room-shape advice
- give each team a clear project home
- keep repair tools centralized and visible
- leave enough open space for quick tests without turning the room into chaos

## Mixed-age caution
Younger students need one working action target. Older/faster students can compare versions, add a second system only after proof, or document changes more precisely.

## Fatigue / reset note
Use the break block to relabel parts, shrink targets, and restart with one question: what has to work before anything else?

## What to photograph or capture
- one current-version photo each session
- one labeled part or systems sketch
- one parking note with the next move

## What students should have by the end of the week
- a rough working prototype
- a clear sense of what still fails
- a usable next-step plan for Week 5

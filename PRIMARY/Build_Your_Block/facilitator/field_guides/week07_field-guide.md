# Week 7 Field Guide

## Focus
Install, host, reflect, and archive without letting showcase energy erase the end-of-course learning.

## What absolutely must happen this week
- Projects get installed in a readable way.
- Students host real visitors.
- Reflection and archive capture happen after the event.

## What can be cut if time slips
- extra decoration
- long scripted rehearsal
- optional celebration extras before reflection is secured

## Top 3 materials to prep first
- labels and station pieces
- batteries, cords, and emergency fix kit
- reflection prompts and archive capture tools

## Top 3 failure points to watch for
- showcase energy crowding out reflection
- reset routines failing under repeated use
- archive capture becoming only a few random photos

## Room-shape advice
- keep approach, wait, and touch zones legible
- keep the emergency fix kit central but calm
- plan where finished archive items get placed after the event

## Mixed-age caution
Younger students need short hosting roles and flexible reflection modes. Older/faster students can tighten handoffs, interview visitors, or lead archive capture while still presenting.

## Fatigue / reset note
Use pauses to lower adrenaline. Between hosting rounds, reset stations, give water, and restate the calm next move.

## What to photograph or capture
- one full station photo
- one key interaction close-up
- one student quote
- one visitor response
- one archived item beyond photos: label, diagram, status card, or short video

## What students should have by the end of the week
- a public presentation experience
- a final reflection
- a visible archive record of what they built and changed

# Week 5 Field Guide

## Focus
Make the prototype sturdier, clearer, and more public-ready before outside testing.

## What absolutely must happen this week
- Teams repair the biggest weak point.
- Teams improve one confusing part of the user experience.
- Every project gets at least a basic reset routine.

## What can be cut if time slips
- nonessential finish work
- extra features after the main fix is clear
- polished signage beyond the most necessary cues

## Top 3 materials to prep first
- repair and reinforcement supplies
- labels and arrows
- repeat-use checklist or reset tools

## Top 3 failure points to watch for
- polish replacing repair
- teams adding features instead of fixing the weak spot
- visitors still not knowing where to start

## Room-shape advice
- keep a clear repair zone
- make repeated-use testing visible
- leave space for no-explanation walkthroughs

## Mixed-age caution
Younger students need one fragile area and one confusing area to fix. Older/faster students can compare failure points, improve station flow, or write stronger instructions.

## Fatigue / reset note
If the room drifts into cosmetic tweaking, stop and ask: what would still fail for a visitor right now?

## What to photograph or capture
- one before/after repair
- one clarity fix
- one reset routine or repeat-use note

## What students should have by the end of the week
- a sturdier prototype
- a clearer interaction
- a real testing goal for Week 6

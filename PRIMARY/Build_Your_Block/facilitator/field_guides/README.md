# Build Your Block Field Guides

These field guides are for **live teaching use**.

They are not the full curriculum. They are the quick, practical version a facilitator can glance at before class or during prep.

## What they are for

- fast weekly orientation
- high-risk reminders
- materials triage
- room-shape and fatigue notes
- quick capture goals

## How to use them

- read the full weekly curriculum file when planning
- glance at the matching field guide before the week starts and before each session
- use the field guide to keep the week from overheating when time, staffing, or attention slip

## What they are not

- not the archival version of the plan
- not the full lesson script
- not a replacement for the weekly curriculum docs

They are the “keep the studio running” layer.

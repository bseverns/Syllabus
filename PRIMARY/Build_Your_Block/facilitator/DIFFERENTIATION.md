# Differentiation and Inclusion

## Low-floor / high-ceiling design
Every activity should allow:
- a simple version that works quickly
- optional complexity for fast finishers
- room for personal style and storytelling

## Support strategies
- visual examples
- sentence starters
- team roles
- quick reset options when a build stalls
- choice boards for project direction

## Mixed-readiness room moves
- let students choose between build paths
- create “challenge cards” for older students
- encourage peer teaching, but do not turn advanced students into unpaid staff
- celebrate repair and revision as real achievement

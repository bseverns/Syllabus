# Public Course Overview: Build Your Block

Course slug: build_your_block
Title: Build Your Block: A Neighborhood Makers Lab
Short title: Build Your Block
UI Level: elementary
Grade band: 3-7
Duration: 16 weeks
Session count: 16
Typical session length: 60-75 minutes, 4 meetings per week
Subject tags: maker, engineering, art, neighborhood, circuits, coding, design

Summary:
Build Your Block is a neighborhood-rooted maker course in which students build projects that light up, move, make sound, react to input, and grow into capstone inventions connected to local life. The course combines engineering, art, storytelling, and community observation so students learn how systems work while making things that feel personal and public.

Who this is for:
- Students in grades 3-7 with mixed prior experience
- Partner sites that want visible weekly outcomes and a final showcase
- Programs that need low-floor, high-ceiling making with room for collaboration

What students do:
- Build with circuits, motors, sound tools, sensors, and block-based coding
- Practice prototyping, testing, revision, and explanation
- Move from short skill-builds into neighborhood-responsive capstone projects
- Present finished work in a family- or community-facing showcase

Core outcomes:
- Use basic maker materials and tools safely
- Explain simple systems in plain language
- Test and revise after failure
- Connect invention to neighborhood memory, need, joy, and imagination
- Share finished work with an audience

Course rhythm:
- Day 1: launch and explore
- Day 2: build the core skill
- Day 3: extend and personalize
- Day 4: share, document, and connect

What makes the course work well in an LMS:
- consistent weekly structure
- strong visible artifacts
- adaptable mixed-age entry points
- clear public-facing outcome at the end of the course

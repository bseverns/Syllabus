# Week 06 — Useful-in-60-Minutes Challenge
**Time:** 90 min

### Goals
- Design within a time/material budget; practice reading slicer estimates.

### Agenda
- **Warm-up (5):** What small thing could help someone at school/home?
- **Mini-lesson (10):** Time levers: scale down, hollow, fewer walls, bigger layers. Use the [course visual pack](../assets/course-visuals/README.md).
- **Make (65):** Design a **cable clip**, **bag tag**, or **cord wrap** under 60 min print time.
- **Queue (10):** Capture estimates; submit STLs.

### Deliverables
- `Firstname_Useful60_v1.stl` + time estimate screenshot (optional).

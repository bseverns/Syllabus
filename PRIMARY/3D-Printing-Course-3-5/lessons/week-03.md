# Week 03 — Tolerance (Peg & Hole) or Spinning Top
**Time:** 90 min

### Goals
- Learn that parts need clearance to fit; experiment with 0.2–0.6 mm.
- Practice duplicating and aligning.

### Agenda
- **Warm-up (10):** Pass around a "tolerance ladder" (teacher sample if available).
- **Mini-lesson (10):** What is clearance? Orientation for strong pegs.
- **Make (60):** Option A: **Peg + base** where peg Ø 8 mm and hole Ø 8.4–8.6 mm. Option B: **Spinning top** (balanced, low mass, 20–40 mm).
- **Queue (5):** Submit; note target ≤ 45 min per print.
- **Cleanup (5):** Save and name projects clearly.

### Deliverables
- `Firstname_PegToy_v1.stl` **or** `Firstname_Top_v1.stl`.

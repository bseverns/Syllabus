# Student Notes: Using UltiMaker / MakerBot Digital Factory

> Your teacher manages the printers and workspace. Please **do not start prints** without teacher approval.

## 1) Sign in / Join the workspace
- Go to **digitalfactory.ultimaker.com** (or the link your teacher shares).
- Click **Sign in** and use your school Google/Microsoft account (or the class login).
- If prompted, **Join Workspace** using the invite your teacher provided.

## 2) Upload your model
- Export your Tinkercad design as **.STL**.
- In Digital Factory, click **Prepare** or **Upload file**, then select your `.stl`.
- Rename your file using the class rule: `W##_Firstname_Object_v#`.

## 3) Choose printer & settings
- Select the **class SKETCH** printer/queue your teacher specifies.
- Load the **Class Fast‑Draft** profile (0.30 mm layers, 2 walls, ~12% infill).
- Check **supports** are OFF unless the teacher says otherwise.
- Look at the **time estimate**; aim for **≤ 60 minutes**.

## 4) Preview & submit
- Use the preview slider to inspect layers. Watch for tiny islands or red overhangs.
- Click **Prepare** (slice). Save the project with your name.
- **Tell the teacher** your file is ready for review (don’t press Start without permission).

## 5) After printing
- Only the teacher removes parts from the plate.
- If your part needs changes, update your design and export **v2** (new filename version).

## Troubleshooting (kid‑friendly)
- **“File too big / time too long”** → Scale down a little or make it thinner.
- **“Holes disappear”** → Make walls at least **1.2 mm** (two walls) thick.
- **“Letters break”** → Extrude text **≥ 2–3 mm** thick; use block fonts.

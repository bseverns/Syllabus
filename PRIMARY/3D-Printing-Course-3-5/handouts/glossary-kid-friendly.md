# Glossary
- **Millimeter (mm):** Our tiny measuring unit. 10 mm = 1 cm.
- **STL:** A file that stores your 3D model for printing.
- **Slice:** Turning a model into thin layers the printer can draw.
- **Infill:** The pattern inside your part. [See four slicer previews.](../assets/course-visuals/cc-images/infill-density-slicer.jpg)
- **Wall/Perimeter:** The outside skin of your part.
- **Support:** Extra material that holds up overhangs. [See green supports beneath an orange model.](../assets/course-visuals/cc-images/support-material-overhang.jpg)

# Course visual

## Flap creature

- **File:** [flap-creature.svg](flap-creature.svg)
- **Creator and source:** original illustration created for this repository with Hermes Agent, not copied from an external image.
- **License:** repository default for curriculum media, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); see [repository license](../../../LICENSE.md).
- **Alt text:** A cardboard creature with a raised wing attached to its body by a paper fastener, allowing the wing to pivot.
- **Use:** Sessions 1 of Machines That Move and Tiny Machines; invite learners to find the fixed body, movable wing, and pivot before the live demonstration.
- **Scope:** an illustrative idea, not an assembly plan or permission to handle sharp tools or small parts. Follow each course’s adult preparation and safety guidance; tape hinges are an alternative.

## Automata at Tam Makers

- **File:** [automata-tam-makers.jpg](automata-tam-makers.jpg)
- **Creator and source:** Fabrice Florin, [“Automata at Tam Makers”](https://www.flickr.com/photos/fabola/46294607341/).
- **License:** [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).
- **Modification:** none; Flickr's 799 × 533 rendition is used as provided.
- **Alt text:** Several wooden box automata with side cranks and figures on top; their mechanisms are hidden inside the boxes.
- **Use:** Session 3 observation before the live cam demonstration; ask learners what might move when a crank turns. The internal mechanisms are not visible.
- **Scope:** inspiration, not a mandatory build or a tool-use guide. Adults prepare and cap dowels and manage sharp cutting and thick-card punching; follow local safety rules.

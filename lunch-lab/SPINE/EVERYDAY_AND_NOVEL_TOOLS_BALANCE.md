# Everyday and Novel Tools Balance

The Lunch Labs work best when they pair familiar classroom-accessible tools with one novel or surprising tool.

## Why this matters

Corporate participants may already understand the broad categories: AI, robotics, 3D printing, sound, drones. The lunch-break magic comes from seeing a toolchain become tactile, inspectable, and transferable to youth programming.

## Pairing pattern

| Familiar anchor | Novel feature | Why the pair works |
|---|---|---|
| Chromebooks + Google Docs | source-aware AI workflow | familiar writing tools become inspectable AI systems |
| micro:bit + MakeCode | sensor-based data postcards | simple classroom hardware becomes environmental research |
| printed 3D samples | slicer preview / tolerance analysis | touchable failures become manufacturing logic |
| phones / browser audio | sound identity sprint | everyday recording becomes brand/media design |
| LEGO Spike | behavior design | classroom robotics becomes service/UX prototyping |
| FPV simulator | radio controller stick discipline | gaming-like simulation becomes embodied training |
| paper workflow cards | agent/automation sketch | automation becomes visible before it becomes risky |

## Facilitation rule

Do not let the novel tool become a black box performance. Whenever possible, show:

1. input,
2. transformation,
3. output,
4. verification,
5. human decision.

## Useful closing sentence

> The classroom tool makes it accessible; the novel tool makes it memorable; the human decision makes it responsible.

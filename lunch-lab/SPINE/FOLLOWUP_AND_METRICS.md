# Follow-up and Metrics

## Lightweight participant follow-up

Use a QR form with 5 questions:

1. Which Lunch Lab did you try?
2. What did you make?
3. Would you attend another one?
4. Would you consider volunteering with createMPLS?
5. What should we bring next?

## Signals to track

- Attendance.
- Completion rate.
- Number of artifacts made.
- Number of QR scans.
- Volunteer interest.
- Partner follow-up requests.
- Equipment issues.
- Most repeated participant questions.

## Event recap structure

### Headline
Example: `Target Lunch Lab: 42 team members made Signal Postcards during lunch`

### Include
- 3 photos of artifacts.
- 1 short participant quote.
- 1 sentence about the youth-program connection.
- 1 lesson learned.
- 1 recommended next lab.

## Internal debrief

Ask facilitators:

- What took too long?
- What explanation was missing?
- What tool failed or slowed down?
- Which moment made people smile?
- What should be pre-staged next time?

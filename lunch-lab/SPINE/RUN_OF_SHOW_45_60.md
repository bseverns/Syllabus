# Shared Run of Show: 45–60 Minutes

## Before participants arrive

- Tables labeled.
- Tools powered and tested.
- Participant cards placed at each seat.
- QR codes visible.
- Demo artifact visible.
- Facilitator has 3-sentence mission bridge ready.
- A recovery plan exists if the technology fails.

## 45-minute plan

### 0:00–0:05 — Welcome
- Name the lab.
- Show finished example.
- Define the output.

### 0:05–0:10 — Tool orientation
- Name 3 tool concepts.
- Name 1 constraint.
- Name 1 safety/access note.

### 0:10–0:32 — Make
- Participants work in pairs or small groups.
- Facilitator circulates.
- Use a midpoint callout: “At 20 minutes, choose your final direction.”

### 0:32–0:40 — Finish
- Title the artifact.
- Save, stamp, score, or photograph.
- Fill final prompt.

### 0:40–0:44 — Share
- 2–3 lightning shares.
- Ask: “What changed after the first try?”

### 0:44–0:45 — Bridge
- Connect to createMPLS youth pathways.
- Point to QR follow-up.

## 60-minute plan

Add:
- deeper second iteration;
- more time for documentation;
- 5-minute discussion on workplace relevance.

## Shared exit prompt

> What would you build, measure, test, or remix if we had one more hour?

# Partner Pitch: Target Lunch Labs with createMPLS

## Working title

**Target Lunch Labs with createMPLS**  
*Creative technology experiences for curious teams.*

## Short pitch

createMPLS can bring a series of 45–60 minute Lunch Labs to Target team members: hands-on creative technology experiences designed for lunch breaks. Participants make a small artifact, try a tool used in youth STEAM learning, and connect the experience to systems thinking, design, creativity, and community partnership.

## Why this belongs in a corporate lunch setting

A lunch break is long enough to make something real if the experience is carefully staged.

These labs are not passive presentations. They are short, structured encounters with tools that reward curiosity:

- drones through simulation;
- data through postcards;
- robots through behavior design;
- sound through Foley and branding;
- 3D printing through fit and failure;
- microcontrollers through tiny interactions;
- AI through grounded verification.

## What participants gain

Participants leave with:

- a small artifact or record of their work;
- a new vocabulary for one creative technology;
- an embodied reminder that learning is iterative;
- a clearer view of how createMPLS builds creative confidence with young people.

## What Target gains

A practical, lively employee engagement format that supports:

- creative reset during the workday;
- community partnership visibility;
- volunteer pathway development;
- design and systems thinking;
- approachable STEM/STEAM literacy;
- a memorable link between corporate talent and youth learning.

## Suggested first session

**Signal Postcards: Dispatches from the Room**

This lab is quiet enough for a lunch room, visual enough for a gallery wall, and directly connected to workplace experience, guest flow, space planning, and data storytelling.

## Suggested first three-session series

1. Signal Postcards
2. FPV Flight School
3. Sound Logo Sprint

This sequence moves from observation to control to expression.

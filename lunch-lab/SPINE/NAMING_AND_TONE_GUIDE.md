# Naming and Tone Guide

## Series name options

- Target Lunch Labs with createMPLS
- Creative Tech Lunch Labs
- Lunch Break Prototypes
- The One-Hour Lab
- Small Systems, Big Signals

## Preferred tone

Clear, adult, warm, practical, slightly poetic.

## Good phrases

- Make one small thing.
- Read the room as a system.
- Train in simulation before taking risk.
- Turn a signal into a story.
- Prototype a behavior.
- Listen like a designer.
- Build the smallest useful version.
- Leave with a question better than the one you arrived with.

## Avoid

- “This is easy.”
- “This is for beginners.”
- “This is just like what kids do.”
- “Don’t worry, it’s simple.”
- “Fun activity” as the only value claim.

## Better replacements

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| easy | approachable |
| basic | compact |
| worksheet | field card |
| toy | prototype |
| lesson | lab |
| student activity | creative technology experience |
| coding for beginners | interaction prototype |


## AI lab tone update

For Target employees, avoid pitching AI as new, magical, or mysterious. Assume baseline familiarity.

Use:

- AI workflow
- toolchain
- source-aware
- structured output
- generated asset
- verification gate
- local vs hosted
- human-in-the-loop
- inspectable system

Avoid:

- What is AI?
- Chatbots 101
- prompt magic
- replacing humans
- fully automated decision-making

The strongest framing is: **AI is most useful when the workflow remains visible enough for a human to question it.**

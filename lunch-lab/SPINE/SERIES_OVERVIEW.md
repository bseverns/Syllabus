# Series Overview: Target Lunch Labs with createMPLS

## One-sentence description

**Lunch Labs are 45–60 minute hands-on creative technology sessions where Target team members make a small artifact, learn a tool used in youth STEAM programming, and see how playful experimentation builds real-world systems thinking.**

## Core promise

Participants spend a lunch break making something small, vivid, and real.

They do not attend a lecture.  
They do not need to become experts.  
They leave with a concrete artifact, a useful concept, and a clearer sense of how creative technology learning works.

## Design principles

### 1. Adult-facing, beginner-friendly

The labs should feel accessible without feeling simplified into childhood. Use language like:

- prototype
- signal
- system
- iteration
- guest experience
- interaction
- simulation
- insight
- creative reset
- design decision

Avoid leading with:

- basic
- easy
- worksheet
- kids can do this
- simple coding lesson

### 2. Make first, explain just enough

Participants should touch the tool or artifact within the first 10 minutes.

### 3. One vivid output

Every lab should end with one visible or shareable thing:

- postcard
- pilot card
- sound logo
- robot behavior
- fit report
- micro:bit creature
- AI workflow card

### 4. Corporate relevance without corporate flattening

The labs can connect to workplace ideas: product design, data storytelling, training, guest experience, prototyping, responsible AI, novel toolchains, creative teamwork. But they should still feel handmade and exploratory.

### 5. Mission bridge

Every lab ends with a concise bridge:

> This is the compressed adult version. With young people, the same activity becomes a longer pathway into coding, design, engineering, documentation, and creative confidence.

## Recommended pilot trio

### Signal Postcards
Best for data, space, guest experience, workplace design, and observation.

### FPV Flight School
Best for spectacle, simulation, practice systems, and quick memorable challenge.

### Sound Logo Sprint
Best for emotional connection, brand, media, and rapid creative output.

## Higher-tech add-on

### AI Workflow Arcade
Best for teams already familiar with basic AI and ready to examine toolchains: local vs hosted models, retrieval/source discipline, structured output, generated assets, and human verification gates.

This lab should not be pitched as "Intro to AI." Pitch it as: **How do we keep AI workflows inspectable?**

## Scaling model

| Format | Use case | Labs |
|---|---|---|
| Single lunch lab | One department, one room | 1 lab |
| Rotating lunch series | Monthly partner engagement | 3–7 labs over time |
| Mini-fair | Larger employee event | 3 labs as stations |
| Volunteer preview | Recruit corporate volunteers | 2 labs + mission talk |
| Sponsor briefing | Board / leadership preview | Signal Postcards + Dimension Token or AI |

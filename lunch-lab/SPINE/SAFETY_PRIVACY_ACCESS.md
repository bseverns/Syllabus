# Safety, Privacy, and Access Notes

## General safety

- Avoid live drone flight in corporate lunch settings.
- Avoid soldering, knives, hot glue, and open liquids.
- Pre-stage equipment to reduce clutter and setup time.
- Keep cables taped or routed away from walking paths.
- Use pairs for activities that require gear sharing.

## Privacy

- Do not photograph participants without explicit permission.
- Photograph artifacts separately when possible.
- For gallery walls, use first names only or no names.
- For AI labs, use sample text rather than personal or confidential workplace content.
- For Signal Postcards, measure the room, not individual people.

## Accessibility

- Provide low-tech alternatives for every lab.
- Avoid audio-only instructions.
- Use printed instructions and table cards.
- Offer seated roles in every activity.
- Keep competitive elements optional.
- Do not require participants to install apps on personal devices.

## Corporate content boundaries

- Do not ask participants to use proprietary Target data.
- Do not use confidential work examples.
- Keep AI examples generic or createMPLS-related.
- Treat the event as community engagement, not internal consulting.

## Consent language for artifact gallery

> We may photograph the object you made today for an internal createMPLS event recap. We will not include your face, full name, or personal information unless you explicitly agree.


## AI-specific privacy and access

For AI Workflow Arcade, use synthetic examples and pre-approved tools only. Do not ask participants to enter internal Target data, customer data, employee data, financial data, unreleased product information, strategy documents, or personal information.

If the host cannot approve live AI access, run the lab from pre-generated outputs and focus on verification. The pedagogical core is workflow design and human judgment, not account access.

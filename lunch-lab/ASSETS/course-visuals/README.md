## Alt text

- **Signal Postcards:** A room observation becomes a three-point dot map, then a postcard message with an evidence-based recommendation.
- **FPV Flight School:** A pilot follows the simulator practice loop: hover, pass a gate, reset calmly, and repeat with one recorded goal.
- **Sound Logo Sprint:** Impact, texture, and motion object sounds form a short sound logo, with a small silence before the final mark.
- **Robot Mood Machines:** Speed, pause, turn, light, and sound controls combine into different observable robot moods.
- **Dimension Token:** A 30 by 18 by 3 millimeter token shows a raised mark, optional hole, flat back, and no-support print constraints.
- **Tiny Code Creatures:** Button, shake, and tilt inputs lead to a micro:bit creature's face, sound, or movement response.
- **AI Workflow Arcade:** A brief moves through a supplied source, constrained tool, inspectable output, verification desk, and human decision.

# General Prompt Cards

Cut these into small cards.

---

## Prototype

Make the smallest useful version.

---

## Signal

What can this room tell us if we measure one thing?

---

## System

What changed when you changed one part?

---

## Iteration

What did you change after the first try?

---

## Human judgment

What still needs a person?

---

## Next hour

What would you do if you had one more hour?

---

## Toolchain

What are the parts: input, source, tool, output, verification, decision?

---

## Verification

What should be kept, checked, or rejected?

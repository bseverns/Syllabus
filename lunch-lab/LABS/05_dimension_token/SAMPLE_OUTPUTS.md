# Sample Outputs: Dimension Token

## Sample token 1: Cart Tag

- Size: 30 × 18 × 3 mm
- Raised text: `A12`
- Hole: 4 mm
- Finding: readable, durable, quick to print
- Next iteration: increase contrast with marker fill or color swap in longer workshop

## Sample token 2: Prototype Badge

- Size: 30 × 18 × 3 mm
- Raised icon: simple star or arrow
- Hole: none
- Finding: strongest version because it stays flat and prints fast
- Next iteration: round corners or add a shallow recessed field

## Sample token 3: Failed Fine Text

- Size: 30 × 18 × 3 mm
- Raised text: too many small letters
- Finding: looked good in CAD, became muddy in plastic
- Next iteration: shorten the word, enlarge the font, or use initials only

## Sample token 4: Weak Hole Edge

- Size: 30 × 18 × 3 mm
- Hole: 4 mm center only 3 mm from edge
- Finding: edge is too thin and may snap
- Next iteration: place hole center 5 mm from edge or enlarge overall tag.

# Participant Card: Robot Mood Machines

## Your challenge

Participants remix a prebuilt LEGO Spike robot behavior to express a chosen mood, service style, or personality.

## What you will make

Robot behavior performance plus Behavior Card.

## Steps

1. Look at the example.
2. Choose your starting prompt.
3. Try the tool once.
4. Make one change.
5. Name your artifact.
6. Write one sentence about what changed or what you noticed.

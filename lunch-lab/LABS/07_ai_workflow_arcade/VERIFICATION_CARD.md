# Verification Card

## Label each part of the output

### KEEP

Useful, grounded, and safe enough to carry forward.

### CHECK

Plausible but needs a source, expert review, policy check, or human context.

### REJECT

Invented, confidential, biased, off-brand, legally risky, misleading, or not useful.

## Four checks

1. **Source check** — Where did this claim come from?
2. **Scope check** — Did the tool answer more than it was asked?
3. **Stakeholder check** — Who could be affected if this is wrong?
4. **Boundary check** — Was anything entered that should not have been shared?

## Closing sentence

> The output is not finished until a human can explain why it is safe enough to use.

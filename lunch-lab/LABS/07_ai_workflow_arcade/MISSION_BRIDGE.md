# Mission Bridge — AI Workflow Arcade

## Adult lunch-break bridge

In this Lunch Lab, Target team members explore AI as a workflow: a chain of sources, tools, constraints, outputs, and human decisions.

## Youth-program bridge

With young people, this becomes a deeper creative technology pathway:

- asking better questions,
- using sources responsibly,
- making small tools,
- checking outputs,
- naming bias and uncertainty,
- deciding when a system should not be used.

The same pattern can support writing, coding, art, robotics documentation, design reviews, and project planning.

## Suggested closing language

> AI can feel like a fog machine: impressive, shiny, and hard to inspect. Our goal is to turn it into a workbench. Students should be able to see the parts, question the output, and decide what deserves human care.

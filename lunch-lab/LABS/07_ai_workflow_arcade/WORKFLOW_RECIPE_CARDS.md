# Workflow Recipe Cards

Print and cut these into cards.

---

## Recipe 1 — Source Packet Only

**Prompt skeleton:**

Using only the source facts below, create a [format] for [audience]. If the source facts do not support a claim, say "not in source." Keep the tone [tone].

**Use when:** accuracy and boundaries matter.

**Watch for:** invented details, softened uncertainty, fake confidence.

---

## Recipe 2 — Messy Notes to Decision Table

**Prompt skeleton:**

Turn these notes into a decision table with columns for option, benefit, risk, prep need, and human decision. Do not add facts that are not present. Mark unknowns as "unknown."

**Use when:** notes need shape quickly.

**Watch for:** the model filling unknowns with plausible guesses.

---

## Recipe 3 — Generate, Then Critique

**Prompt skeleton:**

Generate three concepts for [project]. For each, include a title, audience fit, risk, and why it might work. Then critique your own suggestions for tone, accessibility, and overclaiming.

**Use when:** ideation needs friction, not just speed.

**Watch for:** blandness, sameness, false novelty.

---

## Recipe 4 — Human Approval Gate

**Prompt skeleton:**

Before finalizing, list every claim that should be verified by a human. Separate facts, assumptions, opinions, and style choices.

**Use when:** output might be published, shared, or acted upon.

**Watch for:** claims hiding inside polished language.

---

## Recipe 5 — Local vs Hosted Comparison

**Prompt skeleton:**

Answer this same prompt in two systems. Compare the outputs for privacy posture, specificity, usefulness, speed, and risk.

**Use when:** choosing a tool matters as much as writing a prompt.

**Watch for:** assuming bigger/cloudier is always better.

# Facilitator Guide — AI Workflow Arcade

## Core promise

Participants will test a small AI workflow that turns a messy brief into a structured output, then decide what a human must verify before the output becomes usable.

## 45-minute run of show

### 0:00–0:04 — Frame

Say:

> Most people in this room have already seen AI produce text. Today we are looking one layer deeper: sources, constraints, generated artifacts, verification, and human decision-making.

Show the AI Workflow Card.

### 0:04–0:08 — Demonstrate the chain

Project a five-step chain:

1. brief,
2. sources,
3. model/tool,
4. output,
5. verification.

Use a mundane example:

> Brief: Create a lunch-break volunteer invitation for a creative tech nonprofit.
> Source: three facts about the program.
> Output: short invite copy.
> Verification: which claims are true, missing, risky, or too vague?

### 0:08–0:12 — Choose a track

Pairs choose one of three tracks:

- **Source-Aware Answer** — answer only from supplied facts.
- **Generated Concept Asset** — create a visual/title/outline under constraints.
- **Workflow Reshape** — turn messy notes into a table, checklist, or decision matrix.

### 0:12–0:30 — Work period

Participants complete the workflow card while using the assigned tool or station. Keep the timer visible.

Facilitator role:

- ask what source the model is allowed to use,
- ask what the output format should be,
- ask what would need to be checked before sharing,
- keep anyone from disappearing into endless prompt tweaking.

### 0:30–0:38 — Verification desk

Each pair marks the output with three labels:

- **Keep** — useful as-is or nearly so.
- **Check** — plausible but needs confirmation.
- **Reject** — invented, risky, vague, biased, off-brand, or not useful.

### 0:38–0:44 — Fast share-out

Ask three pairs to share one sentence:

> Our toolchain was useful for ______, but a human still had to ______.

### 0:44–0:45 — Mission bridge

Close:

> This is the adult lunch-break version. With young people, this becomes a larger practice of source discipline, creative production, verification, and deciding when a tool should not be used.

## 60-minute expansion

Add one of these:

- Compare a hosted model to a local model.
- Ask the same brief with and without source material.
- Turn a generated output into a Google Sheet or Slides artifact.
- Add an image/audio generation station.
- Add a "human approval gate" where participants must justify publication readiness.

## Facilitation cautions

- Do not invite participants to enter confidential Target material.
- Do not collect employee names unless the host requests it.
- Use synthetic/sample briefs.
- Avoid making claims about tool accuracy, legal compliance, or enterprise approval.
- Make the verification step visible and serious.

## The sentence that keeps it grounded

> The point is not to be amazed. The point is to keep the workflow inspectable.

# Foley Table Labels
## Printable Plain-Language Labels

Cut these into cards or paste them onto index cards.

---

# FOLEY
## Sound design made with ordinary objects

Pick one object from each required bin:

1. Impact
2. Texture
3. Motion

Then record, arrange, and name your sound logo.

---

# BIN 1: IMPACT
## Clicks, taps, starts, stops, arrivals

Try:

- tap a cardboard box
- click binder clips
- jingle keys
- tap a mug
- close a plastic lid

Good for:

- confirmation
- arrival
- attention
- decision
- ending

---

# BIN 2: TEXTURE
## Paper, fabric, shimmer, surface

Try:

- crinkle tissue paper
- rub felt
- sweep microfiber cloth
- shake sealed rice
- brush cardboard

Good for:

- calm
- energy
- browsing
- softness
- background mood

---

# BIN 3: MOTION
## Sliding, rolling, zipping, whooshing

Try:

- zip a pouch
- retract a tape measure
- roll a toy car
- slide a ruler
- swish a shopping bag

Good for:

- movement
- delivery
- scanning
- transition
- speed

---

# BIN 4: HUMAN / GESTURE
## Small human sounds

Try:

- clap once
- snap once
- tap a pencil
- step on cardboard
- say one quiet word

Good for:

- warmth
- service
- attention
- presence
- personality

---

# BIN 5: TONE / ELECTRONIC
## Bell, beep, chime, synth

Try:

- tap a desk bell
- play one synth note
- use a micro:bit beep
- pluck a kalimba

Good for:

- notification
- polish
- brand identity
- digital feeling
- final sparkle

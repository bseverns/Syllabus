# Target / Corporate Prompt Cards
## Sound Logo Sprint

Print, cut, and place these near the Foley bins.

---

## Prompt 1: Found It

Make the sound of a guest finding exactly what they came for.

Desired feelings:

- relief
- clarity
- small delight
- confidence

Try objects:

- paper bag
- zipper pouch
- keys
- bell

---

## Prompt 2: On Time

Make the sound of a product arriving exactly when expected.

Desired feelings:

- reliable
- precise
- efficient
- calm

Try objects:

- cardboard box
- tape measure
- binder clip
- sealed rice container

---

## Prompt 3: Calm Checkout

Make the sound of a checkout lane that feels fast but not rushed.

Desired feelings:

- warm
- quick
- human
- smooth

Try objects:

- microfiber cloth
- plastic card or ruler
- mug tap
- quiet voice

---

## Prompt 4: Helpful Notification

Desired feelings:

- light
- useful
- respectful
- clear

Try objects:

- finger snap
- desk bell
- tissue paper
- micro:bit tone

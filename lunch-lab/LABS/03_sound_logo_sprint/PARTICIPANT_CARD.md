# Start Here: Sound Logo Sprint

## Make this

Make a 5–10 second sound using three ordinary objects.

**Foley** means sound design made with everyday objects.

## You need

A short sound logo with:

- a title;
- 3–5 recorded object sounds;
- one clear feeling or message;
- one sentence explaining your design choice.

Keep the [`SOUND_LOGO_CUE_SHEET.md`](SOUND_LOGO_CUE_SHEET.md) beside this card. It is where you record the object recipe and sound order.

## Step 1: Pick a prompt

Choose one:

- A guest finds exactly what they came for.
- A product arrives on time.
- A checkout lane feels calm and quick.
- An app notification feels helpful, not annoying.
- A team solves a problem quickly.
- A store opens for the day.

## Step 2: Pick a feeling

Circle one:

`calm` / `fast` / `warm` / `precise` / `playful` / `premium` / `curious` / `reliable`

## Step 3: Record three required sounds

Choose one from each bin:

1. **Impact:** a tap, click, snap, drop, or knock.
2. **Texture:** paper, fabric, foil, brush, beads, rice, or soft rubbing.
3. **Motion:** sliding, rolling, zipper, whoosh, swish, or movement.

Optional:

4. **Human gesture:** clap, snap, breath, footstep, small voice.
5. **Tone:** bell, chime, synth, app-like sound.

## Step 4: Arrange your sounds

Use this simple structure:

| Time | What happens? |
|---:|---|
| 0–1 sec | Start sound / attention |
| 1–3 sec | Texture or motion |
| 3–5 sec | Main gesture / ending |
| 5–10 sec | Optional echo, silence, or second ending |

## Step 5: Name it

Title:

`________________________________________`

## Step 6: Say what you want it to feel like

> This sound logo should feel like __________________________________ because __________________________________.

## If you are stuck

- Start with exactly one impact, one texture, and one motion sound.
- Record close to the object; a quiet, short take is better than a loud room recording.
- Listen once, change one thing, then stop. Silence can be your final sound.

# Sample Outputs: Sound Logo Sprint

## Example 1: Guest Finds the Item

**Prompt:** Make the sound of a guest finding exactly what they came for.  
**Desired feeling:** relief, clarity, small delight

**Objects used:**

- keys on keyring
- brown paper bag
- zipper pouch
- desk bell

**Cue:**

| Time | Sound | Purpose |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0–0.5 sec | single key jingle | attention / search |
| 0.5–2.0 sec | soft paper bag crinkle | browsing / texture |
| 2.0–3.0 sec | zipper slide | discovery / motion |
| 3.0–3.5 sec | bell tap | found it |
| 3.5–5.0 sec | silence | clean ending |

**Design intent:**  
This sound logo should feel like a quick moment of relief because the search turns into a clear ending.

## Example 2: Product Arrives On Time

**Prompt:** Make the sound of a product arriving on time.  
**Desired feeling:** precise, reliable, efficient

**Objects used:**

- cardboard box
- tape measure
- binder clip
- sealed rice container

**Cue:**

| Time | Sound | Purpose |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0–0.8 sec | tape measure retract | motion / travel |
| 0.8–1.2 sec | cardboard box tap | arrival |
| 1.2–2.5 sec | rice container soft shake | movement inside system |
| 2.5–3.0 sec | binder clip click | confirmed |
| 3.0–5.0 sec | silence | confidence / no clutter |

**Design intent:**  
This sound logo should feel reliable because every sound is short, clean, and controlled.

## Example 3: Calm Checkout Lane

**Prompt:** Make the sound of a calm checkout lane.  
**Desired feeling:** warm, quick, human

**Objects used:**

- microfiber cloth
- plastic card or gift card
- ceramic mug
- quiet spoken "ready"

**Cue:**

| Time | Sound | Purpose |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 sec | cloth sweep | soft opening |
| 1.0–1.5 sec | card slide | transaction |
| 1.5–2.0 sec | mug tap | gentle confirmation |
| 2.0–2.8 sec | quiet "ready" | human presence |
| 2.8–5.0 sec | silence | calm ending |

**Design intent:**  
This sound logo should feel calm because there are no harsh beeps or rushed impacts.

## Example 4: App Notification That Helps

**Prompt:** Make the sound of a helpful app notification.  
**Desired feeling:** light, useful, not annoying

**Objects used:**

- desk bell, very soft
- tissue paper
- finger snap
- optional micro:bit tone

**Cue:**

| Time | Sound | Purpose |
|---:|---|---|
| 0.0–0.2 sec | soft snap | attention |
| 0.2–0.8 sec | micro:bit tone or bell | notification |
| 0.8–1.5 sec | tissue paper brush | softness |
| 1.5–5.0 sec | silence | respect the listener |

**Design intent:**  
This sound logo should feel helpful because it gets attention and then gets out of the way.

## Prompt bank

- What object did you use?
- What did that object become?
- What feeling were you aiming for?
- Where did silence help?
- What would you remove if this had to be even cleaner?
- What would a longer youth version of this lab teach?

# Mission Bridge: Signal Postcards

## 30-second bridge

> This is the compressed lunch-break version. In createMPLS youth programming, the same activity becomes a longer pathway into tool fluency, creative confidence, documentation, testing, and revision.

## Youth pathway connection

Signal Postcards can expand into:

- vocabulary-building;
- guided experimentation;
- project planning;
- peer testing;
- documentation;
- revision;
- showcase or reflection.

## Volunteer invitation language

> If this kind of making felt alive to you, there are ways to support young people as they build similar skills over longer arcs. You do not need to be an expert in every tool. Curiosity, patience, and willingness to help students test and revise are valuable.

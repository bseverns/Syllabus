# Mission Bridge Card

Use at the end of every Lunch Lab.

> This is the lunch-break version. In createMPLS youth programming, the same activity becomes a longer path: learn the tool, build the prototype, document choices, test with peers, revise, and share.

## Three optional follow-up prompts

- What would you build with one more hour?
- Where does this kind of thinking show up in your work?
- What would young people learn from stretching this over several sessions?

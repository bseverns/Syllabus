# Assessment Rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Good (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Process | Iterates with notes, tests changes, documents decisions | Iterates with some notes | Some iteration; sparse notes | Minimal iteration |
| Technical | Robot demonstrates reliable sensing, mapping, and behaviors | Mostly reliable; minor issues | Works intermittently | Major issues |
| Expression | Clear vibe mapping; coherent motion/sound/light | Mostly coherent; occasional mismatch | Idea present but muddy | Unclear |
| Collaboration | Proactive teammate; respects studio | Generally positive | Mixed participation | Disruptive |

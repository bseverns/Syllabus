# Enrichment Track — Robotics & Vibe Coding — Level 1

For motivated students or extended programs. Use in parallel or as optional homework.

- **Advanced mapping**: multi‑sensor blending (e.g., weighted sums; low‑pass filters).
- **Pattern banks**: save/recall “vibe” presets via buttons or long‑press.
- **Ensembles++**: IR/LED beacons to sync tempo; call/response patterns.
- **Materials**: 3D‑printed brackets, laser‑cut faceplates, cardboard‑rapid prototyping.
- **Documentation**: 60‑second video demo; wiring diagram sketch; short artist statement.

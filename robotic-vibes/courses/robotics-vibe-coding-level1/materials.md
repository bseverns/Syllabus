# Materials & Budget Tiers

**Target:** 12 robots. Prices are ballpark; substitute locally as needed.

## Lean (baseline classroom)
- Controllers: 6× LEGO Spike Essential kits (shared pairs) **or** 12× Pico + motor driver + small chassis kits
- Motors/wheels: included or budget DC gear motors
- Sensors: touch/bump, light, ultrasonic (where applicable)
- Audio/light: piezo buzzers or onboard speaker; addressable LED strips (small)
- Power: USB battery banks or AA holders; safe charging hub
- Tools: small screwdrivers, tape, zip ties, cardboard, hot glue (supervised), cutters
- Safety: eye protection, cable covers, gaffer tape

## Standard (recommended)
- As above + spare motors/sensors (20% overhead)
- Solder‑less jumpers + breadboards per team
- Printed handouts + labels; bin system per team

## Enhanced (performance/production)
- 3D‑printed brackets; laser‑cut faceplates
- Onboard amplified speaker modules
- Extra LED density; IR beacon pairs for ensemble sync

# Syllabus — Robotics & Vibe Coding — Level 3 (Swarms & Scenes)

**Format:** 8 × 2 hours (A/B blocks)  
**Prereq:** Level 2 or equivalent

## Weekly Outline
1. **Systems & Contracts** — Protocols, addresses, time sync; rehearsal architecture.
2. **Localization & Maps** — Fiducials/markers; error bounds; correction loops.
3. **Scenes & Behaviors** — Behavior Trees/advanced state machines; token passing.
4. **Networking & Reliability** — ACKs, retries, channel policy; optical fallback.
5. **Light/Sound Integration** — DMX/MIDI/OSC; cue mapping from robot events.
6. **Rehearsal Engine** — Preset lists, transitions, operator UI; logging dashboard.
7. **Tech & Dress** — Fault drills; load‑in/out; documentation capture.
8. **Premiere** — Public performance; teardown; retrospective + postmortem.

## Assessment
- **Process (25%)**: logs, diagrams, change notes.
- **Network & Sync (25%)**: stable timing; graceful loss handling.
- **Mapping & Motion (20%)**: repeatable paths; bounded error.
- **Cues & Expression (20%)**: readable light/sound storytelling.
- **Documentation (10%)**: video; README; scene graph/BT diagram.

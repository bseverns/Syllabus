# Assumptions (Living Ledger)

- Max 12 robots for the Level 1 course.
- Mixed audience: parent/child pairs and/or high‑performing high schoolers.
- Improvised lab environment; access to power strips, safe floor space, and tables.
- Students can access a browser; installs are optional.
- Three “dialects”: LEGO Spike (Scratch & Python), Arduino (C/C++), MicroPython/CircuitPython (Raspberry Pi Pico).
- Budget is flexible; we plan for **Lean**, **Standard**, and **Enhanced** tiers.

# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, joyful learning environment. Be kind, assume good intent, and help others create.

- No harassment or hate speech.
- Prioritize safety with tools, electricity, and moving parts.
- Respect consent for photos, recordings, and data capture.
- Welcome curiosity; mistakes are part of learning.
- In conflicts, slow down, listen, and seek repair.

Violations may result in removal from activities and follow‑up with program leadership.

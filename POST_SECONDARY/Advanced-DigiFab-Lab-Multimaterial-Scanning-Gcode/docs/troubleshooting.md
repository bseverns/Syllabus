# Troubleshooting Index
- **Under‑extrusion:** check drive gear, nozzle clog, temp, filament moisture.
- **Layer shift:** belts/tension, collisions, speed.
- **MMU mis‑load:** trim tips, reduce retractions, slow changes, dry filament.
- **PVA collapse:** dryer, interface layers, slower speeds, higher temps.
- **Pause resume scar:** park position and purge off‑part before return.

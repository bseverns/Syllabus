# Rubric — Capstone Portfolio (100 pts)
- **Concept & Fit (25):** Clear goal; design for printability and assembly.
- **Craft (25):** Surfaces/joins intentional; clean supports; color/material used well.
- **Technical Depth (20):** Demonstrates multi‑material/scan/G‑code techniques.
- **Iteration & Evidence (20):** Logs show tests, changes, benchmarks.
- **Presentation (10):** Final display, photos, concise write‑ups.

# Photogrammetry Guide

1. **Plan:** Matte subject, even diffuse light, turntable if possible.
2. **Capture:** 60–120 overlapping photos around subject; avoid motion blur.
3. **Process:** Use your chosen tool (Meshroom or other); export a high‑poly mesh.
4. **Cleanup:** In Blender/Meshmixer: remove noise, decimate, fill holes, set scale.
5. **Split & Key:** For large prints, split and add **keys/bosses** for reassembly.

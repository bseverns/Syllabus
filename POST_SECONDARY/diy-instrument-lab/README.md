# DIY Instrument Lab (College, 14 Weeks)

This repository contains a college-level course, **DIY Instrument Lab**, designed as a 14-week studio/seminar.

Students design and build their own musical instruments and sound-making systems, moving from contact mics and found objects to simple analog circuits, sensors, and microcontroller-based controllers. Alongside hands-on builds, we read about instrumentality, noise, labor, access, and the politics of sound tools.

The course is built to be tuned up (for advanced electronics) or down (for more sculptural / low-tech work) depending on your site.

## Files

- `COURSE_DESCRIPTION.md` – catalog-style description, format, and learning outcomes  
- `SCHEDULE.md` – 14-week outline (titles + focus)  
- `ASSIGNMENTS.md` – evaluation sketch and assignment breakdown  
- `READING_LIST.md` – conceptual + practical texts grouped by theme  
- `READING_SCHEDULE.md` – week-by-week reading map  
- `SUPPLEMENTAL_RESOURCES.md` – tools, libraries, suppliers, and practice references  
- `INSTRUCTOR_NOTES.md` – teaching guidance, tech tiers, safety, and contingency planning  
- `LOCAL_NOTES_TEMPLATE.md` – template for your local constraints and adaptations  
- `sessions/` – per-week session plans/stubs (Weeks 1–14)

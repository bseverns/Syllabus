# DIY Instrument Lab (College, 14 Weeks) – Schedule

| Week | Title                                            | Focus                                                        |
|------|--------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1    | Listening to Instruments                         | What counts as an instrument? Safety, tools, expectations   |
| 2    | Sound from Objects                               | Contact mics, found sound, amplification basics             |
| 3    | First Oscillators                                | Breadboarding simple tone/noise circuits                    |
| 4    | Noise, Feedback, and Glitch                      | Distortion, feedback, and unstable systems                  |
| 5    | Interfaces & Enclosures                          | Knobs, switches, ergonomics, and box-making                 |
| 6    | Sensors & Gestures                               | Light, force, touch, and motion as control                  |
| 7    | Microcontrollers & MIDI                          | Reading controls, sending MIDI, hybrid instruments          |
| 8    | Prototype 0 Crit                                 | First playable instrument + critique                        |
| 9    | Effects & Processing                             | Filters, simple FX, routing through DAWs / amps             |
| 10   | Ensembles, Networks, and Shared Control          | Group instruments, networked or collective play             |
| 11   | Final Build Sprint I                             | Stabilizing circuits and control                            |
| 12   | Final Build Sprint II                            | Enclosures, finishing, staging                              |
| 13   | Performance / Installation Night                 | Public or internal showing, documentation in action         |
| 14   | Final Crit & Documentation                       | Reflection, documentation bundle, next-steps                |

# Reading List – DIY Instrument Lab (College)

This is a deliberately generous list. You will not assign all of it in any single run.  
Use `READING_SCHEDULE.md` to select and sequence specific readings.

---

## 1. Instruments, Noise, and Experimental Music

- Nick Collins – *Handmade Electronic Music* (excerpts)  
- David Tudor interviews or documentation (performer as circuit designer)  
- Kim Cascone – “The Aesthetics of Failure: ‘Post-Digital’ Tendencies in Contemporary Computer Music”  
- Pauline Oliveros – texts on Deep Listening and expanded instrument practices  
- Selections from sound art / noise histories that foreground DIY and hacking

---

## 2. Instrument Design, Interface, and Playability

- Writings on instrument design and new interfaces for musical expression (NIME papers, etc.)  
- Short HCI / design texts on affordances and embodied interaction  
- Case studies of alternative controllers (game controllers, hacked toys, accessibility-focused instruments)

---

## 3. Gender, Labor, and Access in Music Technology

- Tara Rodgers – *Pink Noises: Women on Electronic Music and Sound* (interviews)  
- Nina Sun Eidsheim – *Sensing Sound* (selected excerpts)  
- Essays or interviews on who builds instruments, who is expected to perform, and who is left out

---

## 4. Electronics & Practical References

- A beginner-friendly electronics primer (voltage, current, resistance, Ohm's law)  
- Circuit cookbook texts or handouts for audio: oscillators, filters, mixers, amplifiers  
- Microcontroller references for MIDI and sensor input (e.g., Arduino documentation)

---

## 5. Artist & Instrument Case Studies

- Documentation of artists’ self-built instruments and setups (sound art, noise, experimental pop)  
- Short videos or blog posts where artists walk through their rigs  
- Any local or instructor-made instruments you can document and share

You can mix technical and conceptual readings so that each week’s hands-on work has at least one text to rub against.

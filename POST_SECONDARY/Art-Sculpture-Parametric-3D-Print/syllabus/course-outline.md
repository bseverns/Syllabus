# Course Outline — Art, Sculpture & Parametric Patterning (8 weeks)

**A/B block each session**
- **Block A (35–45 min):** Concept, demo, mini-crits.
- **Block B (35–45 min):** Studio work, prints, documentation.

## Weeks
1. **Parametric Thinking** — Rules, variables, and repetition; SCAD starter pack.
2. **Transformation & Symmetry** — Rotate/linear extrude; radial and bilateral forms.
3. **Tiling & Lattices** — 2D motifs → 3D; arrays, offsets, and thickness strategies.
4. **Noise & Fields** — Perlin/noise modulations; heightmaps and reliefs.
5. **Material & Light** — Vase mode, translucency, layer direction as visual texture.
6. **Scan Remix** — Photogrammetry/handheld scans; hybrid forms and mesh cleanup.
7. **Iteration + Crit** — Mid‑course critique; refine piece + statement.
8. **Exhibit Build** — Final print(s), finish, label design, and curation.

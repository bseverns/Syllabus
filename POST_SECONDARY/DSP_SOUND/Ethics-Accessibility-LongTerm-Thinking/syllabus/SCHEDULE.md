# 12‑Week Schedule

1. Ethics as constraints: harms, tradeoffs, and “design promises”
2. Consent-forward systems: data, capture, disclosure, opt-out
3. Accessibility basics: disability models, inclusive interaction, plain language
4. Auditing an artifact: transparency + failure modes + user journeys
5. Repairability + right-to-repair: modularity, parts, serviceability
6. Sustainability + lifecycle: materials, energy, supply chain humility
7. Open-source ecosystems: licenses, attribution, community norms
8. Documentation as care: quick starts, troubleshooting, diagrams, “unknowns”
9. Redesign sprint I: accessibility improvements + tested prototype
10. Redesign sprint II: repairability/maintainability improvements + tested prototype
11. Contribution week: upstream docs/code OR internal system doc overhaul
12. Final release: Responsibility Pack + showcase + postmortem

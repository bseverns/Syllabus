# Policies

## Respect + consent
- Never record or collect data about people without clear disclosure and opt-out.
- “Testing” is not interrogation: the system is being tested, not the person.

## Safety
- No mains wiring. Batteries handled with supervision and documented procedures.
- Any soldering/fabrication follows lab training rules.

## Attribution
- Keep a `CREDITS.md` for your project.
- Cite inspirations, libraries, reference designs, and collaborators.

## Assistants / AI
Allowed as tooling. Not allowed as replacement for understanding.
If you use an assistant, keep a short log: what you asked, what changed, what you verified.

# Week 01 — Ethics as constraints (design promises)

## Goals
- Move from vague ethics to actionable design commitments.
- Select an artifact to audit (your project, lab device, or case study).
- Write a one-paragraph promise and a harm/benefit map.

## Studio plan
Read the course premise. Choose your artifact. Use `templates/PROMISE.md` and `templates/HARM_MAP.md`.

## Deliverables (this week)
- `project/PROMISE.md` (one paragraph + 3 measurable commitments)
- `project/HARM_MAP.md` (specific harms + mitigations)
- artifact selection note (what it is and who uses it)

## Critique prompt
What does your system *guarantee*? What does it refuse to do?

# Week 02 — Consent-forward systems (data + capture)

## Goals
- Identify what data your artifact captures or implies.
- Design disclosure and opt-out paths.
- Draft a transparency statement.

## Studio plan
Complete `templates/CONSENT_AND_DATA.md` for your artifact. If your system captures nothing, document that explicitly and why.

## Deliverables (this week)
- `project/CONSENT_AND_DATA.md` (disclosure + opt-out + retention)
- one ‘failure scenario’ writeup (how the system behaves when consent is withdrawn)

If someone asks ‘what are you collecting about me?’, how quickly can you answer honestly?

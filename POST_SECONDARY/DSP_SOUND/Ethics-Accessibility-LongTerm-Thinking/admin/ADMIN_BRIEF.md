# Admin brief — Ethics, Accessibility, Long-Term Thinking (12 weeks)

## Summary
Students produce a Responsibility Pack for an artifact, integrating:
- accessibility auditing + redesign
- consent-forward transparency documentation
- repairability and maintenance planning
- sustainability/lifecycle humility
- licensing, attribution, and ecosystem contribution

## Why it matters
This course improves:
- student design literacy and professional readiness
- inclusion and accessibility practices in the lab
- documentation quality and maintainability of student projects
- alignment with institutional values (equity, responsibility)

## Staffing (recommended)
- Lead instructor (systems + design)
- Lab support (hardware/software)
- Optional: accessibility/UX mentor

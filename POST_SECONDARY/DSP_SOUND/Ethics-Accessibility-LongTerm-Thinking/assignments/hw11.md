# HW 11 — Responsible contribution

1. Choose an appropriate contribution route: upstream issue/patch/PR, owner handoff, internal documentation change, or private proposal.
2. Follow `templates/DOC_CONTRIBUTION_GUIDE.md` or `templates/OPEN_TOOL_CONTRIBUTION.md`; read the recipient's contribution/community rules first.
3. Prepare the smallest reviewable change with purpose, evidence, testing, access notes, and limits.
4. Save a link, patch, or redacted diff plus `project/CONTRIBUTION_NOTE.md` and `project/COMMUNITY_NOTES.md`.
5. Reflect on feedback, rejection, silence, or adoption without treating acceptance as the only evidence of learning.

Do not publish private project details or contact maintainers repeatedly. Internal contribution is a complete route.

Submit: see `assignments/SUBMISSION.md`.

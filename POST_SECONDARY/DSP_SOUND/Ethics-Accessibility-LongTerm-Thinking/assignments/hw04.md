1. Complete `templates/TRANSPARENCY_AUDIT.md` and `templates/FAILURE_MODES.md`.
2. Draw one user/task journey showing entry, action, feedback, failure, recovery, and exit/deletion where relevant.

4. Rewrite or prototype one failure message so it states what happened, what remains safe, and the next action.

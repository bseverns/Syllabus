# HW 06 — Lifecycle and sustainability claim

1. Complete `templates/LIFECYCLE_NOTE.md` across sourcing, production/hosting, transport, use, maintenance, reuse, and disposal.
2. Distinguish measured facts, sourced estimates, assumptions, and unknowns.
3. Choose one bounded change—fewer parts, lower data/storage/energy demand, repairable material, longer service life, less shipping, or responsible end-of-life guidance.
4. Produce before/after evidence or a calculation with units and stated limits.
5. Write a public-facing claim that is accurate enough to publish, plus one stronger claim the evidence does not support.

# HW 01 — Artifact, promise, and harm map

1. Select an artifact you are permitted to inspect, or use an instructor case packet. Record owner/steward, allowed scope, intended action, and why it fits 12 weeks.
2. Copy and complete `templates/PROMISE.md` with three measurable commitments and one explicit refusal.
3. Copy and complete `templates/HARM_MAP.md` with affected stakeholders, specific failure/barrier, likelihood or uncertainty, mitigation owner, and evidence needed.
4. Add one artifact diagram/photo or nonvisual description to `export/`; remove identifying or private information.
5. Write `reflections/week01.md`: which assumption became less comfortable after making it explicit?

Completion check: the promise can be tested, the harm map names people and decisions rather than vague “users,” and the work does not require unauthorized modification.

Submit: see `assignments/SUBMISSION.md`.

# Licenses primer (plain language)

This is not legal advice — it’s a practical orientation.

## Common patterns
- Permissive licenses (e.g., MIT/BSD): easy reuse, fewer requirements
- Copyleft licenses (e.g., GPL): reuse allowed, but derivatives must remain open in specific ways
- Creative Commons (docs/media): CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, etc.

## Practice rule
Pick licenses that match your values and your community.
Then document them clearly in the repo and in release notes.

Also: include attribution and third-party notices. It's part of respect.

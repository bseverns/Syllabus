# Inclusive interaction notes

Design moves that often help:
- redundant cues (color + text + shape + position)
- clear modes (always visible state)
- forgiving controls (undo, reset, safe defaults)
- low cognitive load (fewer simultaneous concepts)
- tactile landmarks (for physical interfaces)
- explicit recovery paths (no hidden rituals)

Accessibility is not “extra.”
It is good UX under real conditions: fatigue, noise, low light, stress.

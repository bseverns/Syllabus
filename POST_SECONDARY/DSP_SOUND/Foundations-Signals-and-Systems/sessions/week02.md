# Week 02 — Operations, symmetry, and measurement

## Goals
- Perform time shifting, scaling, and reversal on signals.
- Compute energy/power/RMS and interpret the results.
- Build intuition for correlation as “shape matching.”

## Session arc (suggested timing)
1. **Warm-up (10 min)** — students predict what time reversal does to a percussion hit vs a sustained tone
2. **Mini-lecture (25 min)** — even/odd decomposition; energy vs power; RMS; correlation intuition
3. **Guided lab (60–80 min)** — implement operations; compute RMS; simple autocorrelation explorations
4. **Share-out (15 min)** — pair explain: why RMS changes (or doesn’t) after certain operations
5. **Close (10 min)** — introduce reflections folder and submission checklist

## Prep (instructor)
- Bring one short transient sample and one sustained sample.
- Decide whether to introduce autocorrelation formally or as a visual experiment.

## Materials
- JupyterLab + Python env
- Headphones

## Extensions (if time / advanced track)
- Compare autocorrelation of voiced speech vs noise.
- Introduce windowed RMS as a stepping stone to envelope followers.

## Exit ticket (5 min)
Give one operation that *changes* a signal’s spectrum and one that doesn’t (guess allowed).

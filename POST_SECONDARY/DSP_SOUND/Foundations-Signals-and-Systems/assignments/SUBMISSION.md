# Submission Guide

## Repo expectations (weekly)
Each week, students commit:
- the lab notebook with outputs saved (plots visible)
- any required exported audio files (if requested)
- a short reflection in `reflections/weekXX.md` (5–10 sentences)
- answers to any prompts in the assignment markdown

## Naming conventions
- Keep your edits inside your fork of this course folder.
- Don’t rename top-level directories; add your work in the provided places.
- Use `export/` for generated files when requested.

## Checklist before pushing
- Notebook runs top-to-bottom without errors
- Plots are visible
- Reflection is present
- Assignment questions answered

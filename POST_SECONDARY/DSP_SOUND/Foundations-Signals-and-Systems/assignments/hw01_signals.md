# HW 01 — Signals

## Why
This homework exists to make sure you can **explain what you just built**.

## Prompts
1. Generate a 440 Hz sine wave and a 440 Hz square wave. Plot 20 ms of each. What’s different?
2. Change the sample rate from 48 kHz to 8 kHz (keeping frequency constant). What changes in the plot? What changes in playback?
3. Write 5–8 sentences: when is a waveform plot more honest than your ears? when is it less?

## Deliverables
- Notebook outputs saved for the relevant cells
- One exported plot image in `export/` (PNG)
- Reflection `reflections/week01.md`

# Resources

This course is intentionally tool-forward, but here are solid references if you want depth.

## Friendly intros
- ThinkDSP (Allen Downey)
- The Scientist and Engineer’s Guide to DSP (Steven W. Smith)

## Classic textbooks
- Oppenheim & Schafer — Discrete-Time Signal Processing
- Lyons — Understanding Digital Signal Processing

## “Listen while you learn”
- Use Audacity spectrogram view and compare with your notebook plots.
- Build a habit: every plot should correspond to something you can hear or reason about.

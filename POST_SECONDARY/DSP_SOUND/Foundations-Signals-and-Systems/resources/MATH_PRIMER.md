# Math Primer (just enough)

This course is not a math-flex. It’s a *math-use*.

## Complex numbers (why they show up)
Frequency analysis uses complex exponentials because they make oscillations easy to represent and combine.

## Sine + cosine as building blocks
Many signals can be represented as sums of sinusoids. Fourier methods formalize this.

## Log scale (decibels)
Humans hear roughly logarithmically; spectra often look clearer in dB.

## Helpful identities
- sin(A) + sin(B) can be rewritten as a product (beats / interference)
- Euler’s formula: e^{jθ} = cos(θ) + j sin(θ) (don’t panic; we use it as a tool)

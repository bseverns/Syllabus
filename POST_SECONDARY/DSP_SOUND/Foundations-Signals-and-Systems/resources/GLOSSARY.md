# Glossary (starter)

- **Sample**: one measurement of a signal at a moment in time.
- **Sample rate (Fs)**: samples per second.
- **Nyquist frequency**: Fs/2; highest frequency representable without aliasing (under ideal assumptions).
- **Aliasing**: high-frequency content folding into lower frequencies during sampling.
- **Quantization**: mapping continuous values to discrete levels (bit depth).
- **LTI**: linear time-invariant system; characterized by impulse response.
- **Convolution**: operation describing output of an LTI system given input and impulse response.
- **FFT**: fast algorithm for computing discrete Fourier transform.
- **FIR**: finite impulse response filter (no feedback).
- **IIR**: infinite impulse response filter (uses feedback).

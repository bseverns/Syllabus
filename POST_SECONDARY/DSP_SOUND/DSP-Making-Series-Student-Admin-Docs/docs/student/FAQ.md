# Student FAQ

## Do I need to be good at math?
No. You need curiosity and willingness to practice.
We use math as a flashlight, not a gate.

## Do I need prior coding experience?
Helpful, not required. We build from basics and pair learners intentionally.

## What if I’m more “artist” than “engineer” (or the reverse)?
Perfect. The course is designed to cross-wire those identities.

## Can I work in a group?
Yes. Teams are welcome. Credit and documentation are part of the work.

## What if my project breaks?
It will. The skill is: recover, diagnose, simplify, and document what happened.

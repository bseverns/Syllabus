# Program overview

This series teaches students to build **audio and interaction systems** that are both:
- **technically legible** (measurable, testable, documented), and
- **artistically alive** (playable, expressive, meaningful).

The arc is designed like a well-built instrument:
- foundations (signals) become technique (DSP),
- technique becomes interface (instruments),
- interface becomes systems practice (modularity),
- systems practice becomes authorship (composition),
- authorship stays accountable (ethics/accessibility),
- and the whole thing culminates in a **capstone** built to survive other people.

## Who it’s for
- advanced high school students, undergraduates, community lab learners
- artists who want deeper technical agency
- engineers who want musical/creative purpose

## What “success” means here
Not perfection. **Iteration + evidence**:
- a student can show what they tried,
- how they measured/listened,
- what they changed,
- and how they communicated it to others.

## The learning loop (every module)
1) Build
2) Measure
3) Listen / Observe
4) Document
5) Improve
6) Share

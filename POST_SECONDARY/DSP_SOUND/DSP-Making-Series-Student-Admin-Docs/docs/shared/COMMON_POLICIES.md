# Common policies (shared)

## Collaboration
Collaboration is encouraged. Credit is required.
- keep a `CREDITS.md` for your project
- cite code snippets, libraries, and inspirations

## AI / assistants
Allowed as a tool, not a ghostwriter.
- you must understand and be able to explain your system
- assistants must not replace testing and measurement
- keep a short “assistant log” of what you asked and what changed

## Safety (hardware)
- no live mains wiring unless supervised by qualified staff
- PPE and tool training for fabrication spaces
- document safe handling for soldering and batteries

## Data + consent
If your system captures audio/video/sensor data about people:
- disclose clearly
- allow opting out
- default to minimal collection

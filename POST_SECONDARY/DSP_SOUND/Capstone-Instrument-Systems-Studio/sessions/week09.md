# Week 09 — Teaching sprint (optional lane): make it teachable

## Goals
- Extract lessons from your build.
- Create a mini-curriculum around the artifact.
- Design an assessment that rewards evidence.

## Studio
Write one 90-minute lesson plan and one 3-week mini-unit.

## Critique prompt
What concept becomes intuitive only after touching your tool?

## Deliverables
- Lesson plan + slides outline
- Rubric draft
- Student handout v1

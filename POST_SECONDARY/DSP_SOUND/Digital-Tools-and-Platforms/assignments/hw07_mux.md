# HW 07 — Multiplexer scan

1. Record the exact mux part marking, operating voltage, enable/common/select/channel pin map, and datasheet source.
2. Build a three-bit channel truth table before wiring.
3. Log all eight channels with at least two known references and one varying input.
4. Compare two settling delays and identify cross-talk, floating input, order, or speed effects.

Submit power-off wiring diagram/photo, inspection record, firmware, CSV/plot by channel, and `reflections/week07.md`. Do not infer pinout from a different part number.

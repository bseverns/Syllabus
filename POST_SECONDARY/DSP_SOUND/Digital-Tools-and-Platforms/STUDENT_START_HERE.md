# Digital Tools and Platforms — Student Start Here

You do not need previous electronics, Arduino, command-line, or soldering experience. We will build one proven subsystem at a time.

## The bench loop

1. **Power off.**
2. **Name the pin and purpose.**
3. **Make one connection.**
4. **Partner-check orientation and pin number.**
5. **Ask for the required instructor inspection.**
6. **Power, flash, and observe without moving wires.**
7. **Save evidence.**
8. **Power off before the next wiring change.**

Stop and tell the instructor about heat, odor, smoke, a reset loop, a cable or part that becomes hot, uncertain voltage, two power sources, damaged insulation, or unexpected output.

## Words we will use

- **board/microcontroller:** the small computer reading inputs and controlling outputs;
- **pin:** a labeled electrical connection with a particular job;
- **ground/GND:** the shared voltage reference;
- **input:** a value entering the program, such as a button or knob;
- **output:** a response such as an LED, Serial message, or MIDI message;
- **firmware/sketch:** the program uploaded to the board;
- **flash/upload:** copy firmware onto the board;
- **Serial:** a text/data connection used to observe and command the board;
- **debounce:** turning a physically noisy press into one stable event;
- **deadband:** ignoring tiny changes around the current value;
- **state/mode:** a remembered condition that changes how inputs behave;
- **protocol:** agreed message structure and meaning.

## When technology is unfamiliar or unavailable

You may use enlarged pin maps, prewritten firmware, a de-energized board, paper state/logic cards, saved Serial logs, or partner `builder / reader-checker / logger` roles. You can explain with a wiring diagram, annotated code, plot, spoken walkthrough, or test checklist.

Typing speed, prior kit ownership, memorized syntax, and being first to wire are not evidence of mastery. Safe inspection, useful tests, clear data, and reproducible documentation are.

## Keep every week

- exact board/tool versions;
- wiring/pin diagram or inspected photo;
- firmware version;
- Serial CSV/transcript and requested plot;
- failure/recovery note;
- reflection and next action.

Do not submit passwords, tokens, unrelated files, device serial identifiers, or identifiable people without permission.

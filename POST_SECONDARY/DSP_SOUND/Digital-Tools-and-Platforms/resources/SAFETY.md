# Safety

Microcontrollers are forgiving until they're not.

## Rules
- Never power a breadboard rail from two sources.
- Always share GND between board and sensors.
- Know whether your board uses 3.3V or 5V logic.
- If something gets hot, unplug first, debug second.

## Measurement habits
- Label rails
- Take a photo of wiring before changing it
- Change one thing at a time

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams define how the final action will actually run in time and space.
- Students identify setup dependencies and failure points.

## Before-class prep

- Share the run-of-show template.
- Bring a sample setup diagram or site map.

## In-class sequence

1. Walk through event flow and setup logic.
2. Teams draft run-of-show.
3. Site mapping and logistics check.
4. Failure-mode review.
5. Owner assignment.

## Discussion prompts

- What has to happen before the public even sees the project?
- Where is the most fragile handoff in your final action?

## Required artifacts

- Run-of-show draft
- Setup diagram or site logic sketch

## Instructor notes

- Treat logistics as design, not admin.
- Make sure each team has a clear stop condition if the site changes or permission shifts.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Reframe the action as a tabletop or hallway version if the original site becomes unavailable.

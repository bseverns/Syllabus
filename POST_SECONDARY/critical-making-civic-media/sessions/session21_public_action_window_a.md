# Session 21 - Public action window A

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**
> - Action site:
> - Contact person on site:
> - Weather or building access risks:

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams stage or begin staging the final public-facing action.
- Students gather live evidence and adjust in real time.

## Before-class prep

- Confirm site access, materials, and transport.
- Review stop conditions and fallback site.

## In-class sequence

1. Setup and safety check.
2. Soft launch.
3. Public interaction window.
4. Live observation and capture.
5. Quick teardown or reset.

## Discussion prompts

- What did the public do that you did not predict?
- What changed once the work hit the site instead of the classroom?

## Required artifacts

- Live documentation captures
- Observation notes

## Instructor notes

- Keep students attentive to people, not only their own nerves.
- If the action must scale down, document the reason and proceed cleanly.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Shift to a tabletop, hallway, or invited-public version if the full site becomes unusable.

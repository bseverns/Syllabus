## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams present final work through evidence, not hype.
- Students complete a usable handoff to instructor, archive, and partner when appropriate.

## Before-class prep

- Set critique order and timing.
- Ask teams to bring one concise presentation plus the full archive.

## In-class sequence

1. Final critiques.
2. Response and questions from peers, guests, or partners.
3. Archive / handoff check.
4. Individual reflection note.
5. Course close focused on replication and next users.

## Discussion prompts

- What did this project prove, and what did it not prove?
- If another team picked this up next semester, what should they do first?

## Required artifacts

- Final civic media action presentation
- Complete documentation bundle
- Final reflection note

## Instructor notes

- Keep critique tied to the rubric and the evidence packet.
- End with transfer: what survives after the semester?

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Run critique from printed boards, screenshots, and physical artifacts if presentation tech fails.

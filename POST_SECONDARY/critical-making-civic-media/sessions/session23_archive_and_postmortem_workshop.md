# Session 23 - Archive and postmortem workshop

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**
> - Shared drive or archive location:
> - Final critique logistics:

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams organize the documentation bundle into a reusable archive.
- Students write an honest postmortem tied to evidence.

## Before-class prep

- Share a checklist for final bundle structure.
- Bring one sample postmortem that includes failure without melodrama.

## In-class sequence

1. Sort files into final bundle structure.
2. Draft setup notes, captions, and README text.
3. Postmortem writing workshop.
4. Peer review on clarity and completeness.
5. Final critique prep.

## Discussion prompts

- Could a future instructor rerun this from what you are turning in?
- What should the archive say that the final presentation will probably skip?

## Required artifacts

- Organized documentation bundle draft
- Postmortem draft

## Instructor notes

- The archive matters as much as the live action.
- Ask for known unknowns, not just success language.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Assemble the archive as a single PDF or printed packet if shared drives are unreliable.

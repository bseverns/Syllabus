## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams choose a medium based on audience, site, and maintenance reality.
- Teams consider how their work could be rerun or handed off.

## Before-class prep

- Bring examples across print, web, kiosk, audio, and installation modes.
- Prepare a quick comparison chart for medium tradeoffs.

## In-class sequence

1. Medium tradeoff mini-lecture.
2. Teams compare 2-3 possible forms.
3. Draft low-tech fallback and replication logic.
4. Pin-up critique.
5. Commit to one primary form.

## Discussion prompts

- Why is this medium better than a poster, and why is it better than a website?
- If the network or gear fails, what version of the project still survives?

## Required artifacts

- Medium comparison notes
- Chosen form with rationale

## Instructor notes

- Push back on tech inflation.
- Ask what the partner or public can actually maintain after the semester.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Run the comparison with printed exemplars and handwritten planning sheets.

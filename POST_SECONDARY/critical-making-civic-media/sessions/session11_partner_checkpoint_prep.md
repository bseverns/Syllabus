# Session 11 - Partner checkpoint prep

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**
> - Scheduled partner meetings:
> - If no partner, constituency-testing plan:

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams prepare for partner-facing or constituency-facing feedback.
- Students learn how to present a project without overselling it.

## Before-class prep

- Share the partner meeting notes template.
- Prepare a short mini-lesson on asking useful questions.

## In-class sequence

1. Review partner etiquette and boundary language.
2. Teams prepare 5-minute project explainers.
3. Practice rounds with peer feedback.
4. Draft meeting questions and materials.
5. Confirm logistics and owners.

## Discussion prompts

- What are you asking from the partner besides approval?
- Where might your framing create accidental pressure or false promise?

## Required artifacts

- Meeting agenda or outreach note
- Question list
- Presentation draft

## Instructor notes

- Emphasize that students are checking alignment, not pitching a startup.
- Require one practical ask and one listening question.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- If meetings must be asynchronous, record a short walkthrough or send an annotated one-pager.

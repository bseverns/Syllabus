# Session 07 - System mapping lab

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**
> - Wall space or whiteboard access:
> - Diagramming tools:

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams map the institutions, interfaces, and bottlenecks around their issue.
- Students identify leverage points for intervention.

## Before-class prep

- Print the system map template or preload a shared board.
- Prepare one sample map with visible limits and missing pieces.

## In-class sequence

1. Short framing on systems versus topics.
2. Teams build first-pass system maps.
3. Midpoint critique focused on missing actors and failure points.
4. Revise maps.
5. Identify 2-3 leverage points.

## Discussion prompts

- Where does the system already communicate, and where does it break down?
- Which node is tempting but actually out of scope for a semester?

## Required artifacts

- System map draft
- Leverage point list

## Instructor notes

- Ask teams to include media channels and physical sites, not only institutions.
- Insist on arrows, flows, and actual sequence.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Use index cards and tape on the wall; photograph the final arrangement.

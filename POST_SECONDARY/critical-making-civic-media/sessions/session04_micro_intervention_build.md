# Session 04 - Micro-intervention build

> - Printing options:
> - Shared materials:
> - No-code tools available:

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Students produce a fast tactical media artifact.
- Students experience an early win tied to a public claim.

## Before-class prep

- Bring paper, markers, tape, cardstock, and access to basic digital tools.
- Set a hard scope: one-session prototype only.

## In-class sequence

1. Review constraints and acceptable forms.
2. Rapid sketch round.
3. Build sprint.
4. Pair critique on legibility and public tone.
5. Quick export or documentation capture.

## Discussion prompts

- Can a stranger understand the intervention in under 10 seconds?
- What did the constraint make sharper?

## Required artifacts

- One micro-intervention prototype
- Short reflection note

## Instructor notes

- Do not allow students to spend the whole session researching.
- Encourage poster, handout, QR flow, or mini-kiosk formats if teams stall.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Run the whole sprint on paper with printed mockups and photographed outputs.

# Session 14 - Prototype v1 and legibility test

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**
> - Testing locations:
> - Accessibility support available:

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams build a first substantial prototype.
- Teams test whether the public can understand it without a long explanation.

## Before-class prep

- Confirm prototype materials and devices.
- Prepare a quick public legibility feedback form.

## In-class sequence

1. Build sprint.
2. Mid-session micro-crit.
3. Legibility testing with peers or invited testers.
4. Capture feedback.
5. Debrief and rank revisions.

## Discussion prompts

- What did testers misunderstand first?
- What part required insider language that needs to disappear?

## Required artifacts

- Prototype v1
- 3-5 legibility test responses

## Instructor notes

- Legibility testing should be blunt and fast.
- Make students observe behavior, not only collect polite comments.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Use paper prototypes, scripted walkthroughs, or still-image mockups if tech is unstable.

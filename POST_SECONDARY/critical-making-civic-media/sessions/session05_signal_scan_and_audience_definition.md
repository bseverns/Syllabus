# Session 05 - Signal scan and audience definition

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**
> - Team formation constraints:
> - Available local constituencies or partner leads:

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Students turn their issue observations into a usable civic signal scan.
- Students define a primary audience and rule out at least one non-target audience.

## Before-class prep

- Prepare a sample signal scan dossier.
- Bring a short audience worksheet.

## In-class sequence

1. Mini-lesson on audience specificity.
2. Students organize captures into a signal scan.
3. Write "for / not for" audience statements.
4. Issue clustering and team formation.
5. Dossier work time.

## Discussion prompts

- Who is currently paying the cost of confusion or exclusion?
- Who does not need to be persuaded for this project to work?

## Required artifacts

- Civic signal scan draft
- Audience statement

## Instructor notes

- Teams should form around usable scope, not only shared vibes.
- Push students to define where the public contact happens.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Build the dossier as a printed packet or slide deck if network tools are unstable.

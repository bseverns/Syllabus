# Session 13 - Ethics, consent, and unintended consequences

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**
> - Relevant campus policies:
> - Sensitive topic flags:

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams identify consent, privacy, representation, and reputational risks.
- Students revise their plans before public testing.

## Before-class prep

- Prepare a short ethics checklist and one cautionary case.
- Pull any site-specific rules students need to know.

## In-class sequence

1. Mini-lecture on foreseeable harm.
2. Teams complete ethics / risk review.
3. Cross-team red-team critique.
4. Revision time.
5. Commit to a public test plan.

## Discussion prompts

- Who could be exposed, embarrassed, or confused by this work?
- What are you asking people to reveal, click, trust, or believe?

## Required artifacts

- Ethics / risk review
- Revised test plan

## Instructor notes

- Push students to change the build if the review surfaces a problem.
- Watch for teams treating "public" as permissionless.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Conduct the red-team critique on paper with printed project briefs and sticky-note risks.

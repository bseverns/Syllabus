## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams turn research into a concise intervention brief.
- Teams define success criteria, scope guardrails, and dependencies.

## Before-class prep

- Share the intervention brief template.
- Prepare one sample brief with clear exclusions.

## In-class sequence

1. Review the elements of a good intervention brief.
2. Team drafting time.
3. Midpoint desk critiques.
4. Team revisions.
5. Brief readout focused on clarity.

## Discussion prompts

- What are you explicitly not doing?
- What would count as failure in a way you can observe?

## Required artifacts

- Intervention brief draft
- Dependency list

## Instructor notes

- The "not doing" section is often the most important part.
- Ask for success criteria tied to audience behavior or comprehension.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Draft the brief on paper, then photograph and transcribe later.

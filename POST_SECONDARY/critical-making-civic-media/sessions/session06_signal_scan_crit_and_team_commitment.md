# Session 06 - Signal scan critique and team commitment

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**
> - Critique format:
> - Team size target:

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams receive critique on issue selection and scope.
- Teams leave with a committed issue, public, and working direction.

## Before-class prep

- Set critique groups and timing.
- Prepare a board with columns: issue, public, intervention zone, risk.

## In-class sequence

1. Team presentations of signal scan.
2. Structured critique using the rubric categories already in play.
3. Team revision time.
4. Final commit to project direction.
5. Process log update and next-step assignment.

## Discussion prompts

- Is the issue precise enough to act on this semester?
- What version of this project would still matter if it had to stay very small?

## Required artifacts

- Revised civic signal scan
- Team direction statement

## Instructor notes

- Be willing to stop teams from choosing impossible problems.
- Require a clear "smallest valuable version" before they leave.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- If time is tight, use sticky-note critiques around printed boards rather than full presentations.

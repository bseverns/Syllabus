## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams assign roles and rehearse setup / interaction flow.
- Students catch weak spots before final production.

## Before-class prep

- Ask teams to bring all available materials.
- Prepare a simple role matrix: setup, public interaction, documentation, teardown.

## In-class sequence

1. Inventory and missing-parts review.
2. Role assignment.
3. Dry-run rehearsal.
4. Trouble spot notes.
5. Next-step lock-in.

## Discussion prompts

- If one person disappears on action day, what breaks?
- Who is responsible for documentation while the event is live?

## Required artifacts

- Role matrix
- Revised materials checklist
- Rehearsal notes

## Instructor notes

- Push against the common failure mode where one student silently carries all the technical work.
- Rehearsal should include public language, not only setup.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Rehearse with stand-ins, paper labels, and cardboard proxies if full materials are not ready.

# Session 12 - Partner feedback and scope reset

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**
> - Feedback format:
> - Site visits or remote calls:

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams collect partner or constituency feedback.
- Teams revise scope based on what they learned.

## Before-class prep

- Confirm partner attendance or alternative public review structure.
- Build in time for notes and immediate synthesis.

## In-class sequence

1. Partner feedback sessions or live review.
2. Immediate note capture.
3. Team synthesis: what changed, what stayed, what got cut.
4. Revised scope statement.
5. Instructor check-ins.

## Discussion prompts

- What did the partner actually need versus what you assumed they wanted?
- What are you cutting now to protect clarity?

## Required artifacts

- Partner meeting notes
- Revised intervention brief

## Instructor notes

- If partner feedback is contradictory, teach students to narrow rather than panic.
- No team should leave with the same scope if the feedback revealed real limits.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Use peer role-play from a stakeholder perspective if outside feedback collapses at the last minute.

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams finalize the build and create a documentation capture plan.
- Students prepare to observe the action, not just survive it.

## Before-class prep

- Bring the documentation checklist.
- Confirm who can photograph, film, or record at the final site.

## In-class sequence

1. Final production sprint.
2. Documentation checklist review.
3. Capture plan assignment: who records what, when, and why.
4. Backup plan review.
5. Final readiness check.

## Discussion prompts

- What evidence will you need later to prove the project worked or failed?
- How will you document without turning participants into props?

## Required artifacts

- Final documentation capture plan
- Near-final artifact

## Instructor notes

- This session is where documentation becomes operational.
- Require at least one low-effort fallback for capturing the action.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Use phone cameras, field notes, and still photos if formal capture gear disappears.

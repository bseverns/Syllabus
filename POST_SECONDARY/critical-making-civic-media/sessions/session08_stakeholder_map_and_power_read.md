## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams identify who is affected, who decides, and who could block or distort the work.
- Teams clarify who they need to talk to before building further.

## Before-class prep

- Share the stakeholder map template.
- Bring one example of a stakeholder everyone forgets.

## In-class sequence

1. Warm-up on visible versus invisible stakeholders.
2. Team stakeholder mapping.
3. Power analysis discussion.
4. Draft partner outreach or public check plan.
5. Wrap with next-contact commitments.

## Discussion prompts

- Who has power without being the intended audience?
- Who could be harmed or embarrassed if the work is framed poorly?

## Required artifacts

- Stakeholder map
- Draft outreach or partner check plan

## Instructor notes

- This is where extractive instincts often surface; name them.
- Require teams to identify one skeptical stakeholder, not just supporters.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- Run stakeholder mapping on paper and use color coding for power and proximity.

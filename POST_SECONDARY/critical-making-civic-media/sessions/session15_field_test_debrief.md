# Session 15 - Field test debrief

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**
> - Any fresh partner or site feedback:
> - Shared critique wall:

## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams interpret prototype test evidence.
- Students prioritize revisions based on public response.

## Before-class prep

- Ask teams to bring all test evidence into one place.
- Prepare a debrief board: worked, confused, blocked, surprising.

## In-class sequence

1. Teams post evidence.
2. Debrief focused on what actually happened.
3. Revision prioritization.
4. Small-group consultation.
5. Process log update.

## Discussion prompts

- Which feedback is noise, and which feedback reveals a design flaw?
- What should change in the artifact versus in the framing around it?

## Required artifacts

- Test evidence board
- Revision priority list

## Instructor notes

- Keep teams from defaulting to cosmetic tweaks if the issue is structural.
- Ask for one change that improves access or public readability.

## Contingency / low-tech fallback

- If no field test occurred, simulate a debrief with peer walkthroughs and a documented honesty note.

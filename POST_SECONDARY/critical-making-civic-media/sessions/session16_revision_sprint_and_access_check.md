## At-a-glance outcomes

- Teams revise prototypes with accessibility and public use in mind.
- Students confirm the smallest viable final form.

## Before-class prep

- Bring a short access checklist: language level, physical reach, captions, contrast, timing.
- Prepare examples of plain-language improvement.

## In-class sequence

1. Access checklist review.
2. Focused revision sprint.
3. Peer access check.
4. Freeze final form direction.
5. Update materials list.

## Discussion prompts

- What version of accessibility work is realistic and necessary here?
- What can you simplify right now without losing the claim?

## Required artifacts

- Prototype v2
- Access check notes
- Updated materials list

## Instructor notes

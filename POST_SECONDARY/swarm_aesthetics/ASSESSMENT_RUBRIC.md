# Swarm Aesthetics: Assessment Rubric

## 1. Systems thinking

Exceeds:

- identifies layers, dependencies, and failure points with precision
- uses diagrams and language that make the system readable to others

Meets:

- describes the major elements of the system and their relationships

Below:

- describes the project only in aesthetic or thematic terms

## 2. Process and iteration

Exceeds:

- prototypes early, revises visibly, and keeps clear revision evidence

Meets:

- produces the required intermediate artifacts and responds to critique

Below:

- arrives at the final event with little evidence of structured iteration

## 3. Roles, protocols, and collaboration

Exceeds:

- defines roles clearly and can hand off work without confusion
- uses recipes, scripts, and run sheets effectively

Meets:

- group responsibilities are legible and mostly reliable

Below:

- collaboration depends on unstated assumptions or one overloaded student

## 4. Critical framing and ethics

Exceeds:

- names the assumptions, politics, and audience effects of the system clearly

Meets:

- addresses ethical and framing questions with some specificity

Below:

- treats the work as neutral or self-explanatory

## 5. Final event and legibility

Exceeds:

- stages a concise, coherent showing with readable audience framing and strong timing

Meets:

- produces a functioning event with understandable intentions

Below:

- event logic, audience cues, or transitions remain unclear

## 6. Documentation and reflection

Exceeds:

- submits a strong documentation bundle with evidence, interpretation, and next steps

Meets:

- documents the work clearly enough for critique and future reference

Below:

- documentation is incomplete or too thin to explain what happened

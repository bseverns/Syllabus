# Session 04 – Telemetry as Material

## At a glance

- approximate length: 2 to 3 hours
- focus: observability, state, and how signals become aesthetic material
- output: telemetry semantics sheet + source inventory

## Before class

- prepare 2 to 3 examples of live signals, dashboards, or reactive artworks
- duplicate `templates/telemetry-semantics-sheet.md`

## In-class plan

1. Introduce the difference between hidden state, observed state, and displayed state.
2. Compare a few example signals and ask:
   - what does the signal mean?
   - when is it valid?
   - what happens when it drops out?
3. Have groups list candidate sources for their project:
   - human actions
   - timers
   - simulated agents
   - telemetry or sensor streams
4. Complete one semantics sheet for the most promising signal.
5. Critique whether the chosen signal is actually legible to the audience.

## Artifacts

- one telemetry semantics sheet
- one list of possible sources and why they matter

## Instructor notes

- avoid letting "data" become a vague aesthetic garnish
- ask what the audience can perceive without a lecture

# Session 05 – Mappings, Assumptions, Ethics

## At a glance

- approximate length: 2 to 3 hours
- focus: transformations, bias, legibility, and the politics of measurement
- output: mapping sketch + ethics memo

## Before class

- assign one reading on surveillance, measurement, or interface politics
- ask each group to bring one signal they might map and one concern about it

## In-class plan

1. Diagram the path from signal to audience perception.
2. Ask what gets amplified, hidden, or normalized by the mapping.
3. In groups, sketch one transformation chain:
   - source
   - conditioning
   - threshold or rule
   - output
4. Write a short ethics memo answering:
   - what assumption is built into this mapping?
   - who or what becomes legible?
   - what gets misread or excluded?
5. End with a fast critique focused on consequences, not intention alone.

## Artifacts

- one mapping sketch
- one short ethics memo

## Instructor notes

- students often treat mappings as neutral
- keep the session grounded in concrete examples rather than generalized politics

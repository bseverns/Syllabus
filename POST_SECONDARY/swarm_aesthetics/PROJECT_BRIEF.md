# Swarm Aesthetics: Final Project Brief

## Prompt

Design and stage a modest drift event that makes collective behavior perceptible. The final work may be screen-based, tabletop, room-scale, or hybrid, but it must remain understandable as a system and as an audience experience.

## Requirements

Each group must submit:

1. a concise project statement
2. a system map
3. a drift recipe
4. a role-card set
5. a rehearsal run sheet
6. signage or audience-facing instructions
7. photo, video, or capture documentation
8. a short debrief memo

## Constraints

- keep scope modest enough to rehearse
- define roles before the final week
- include an operator fallback plan
- document what the audience is expected to notice

## What counts as success

Success does not require technical scale. It requires:

- a legible system
- intentional rules
- observable collective behavior
- clear framing
- evidence of iteration

## Debrief questions

Students should answer:

1. what rules or assumptions most shaped the final event?
2. what did the audience likely understand, misread, or miss?
3. what failed, and what did that reveal?
4. what should a future version keep, cut, or sharpen?

# FPV Systems Primer — Student Start Here

You do not need prior flight experience. This course begins with props removed and can be completed as a bench-only systems course.

## The stack

Learn to point out and narrate these paths:

1. power and battery;
2. radio/control link;
3. receiver and flight controller;
4. motor output;
5. camera/video link;
6. telemetry and saved logs.

Control, video, and telemetry are different paths. One may fail while another still appears healthy.

## Your working routine

1. Name today's goal and proof.
2. Confirm props are removed for bench work.
3. Follow the named team roles and power procedure.
4. Change one documented thing.
5. Observe the result and save evidence.
6. Mark what is proven and what is still unknown.
7. Power down, inventory, and record whether the rig remains grounded.

## You may always stop

Call the instructor for unexpected heat, smell, smoke, battery damage, unintended motor output, confusing control movement, unexplained restarts, uncertain failsafe, or an unclear plan. Stopping is evidence of good systems judgment.

You may participate with a de-energized rig, screenshots, saved configuration, simulator, or recorded telemetry while equipment is unavailable or if powered work is not an appropriate participation route.

## What you will keep

- annotated parts map;
- bench-proof checklist;
- saved configuration or diff;
- telemetry semantics and capture;
- mapping sheet and comparison;
- session/flight log;
- troubleshooting or triage narrative.

A file or checklist does not authorize motor testing or flight. Instructor signoff and local rules remain controlling.

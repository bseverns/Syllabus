# Lab 04 — Post-crash triage and relaunch decision

**Goal:** inspect an Air65-class whoop after impact and justify whether it should relaunch, return to bench test, or be grounded.

## Setup

Use either:

- a real post-flight crash case
- an instructor-prepared rig with known minor faults
- a simulated case study with photos and logs

## Steps

1. Disarm and secure the rig.
2. Complete `templates/post-crash-triage-sheet.md`.
3. Inspect props, ducts, canopy, motors, battery lead, and antenna path.
4. Check whether the issue is purely mechanical or whether control-link confidence also needs reproof.
5. Decide on one of three outcomes:
   - relaunch cleared
   - bench test first
   - grounded for repair
6. Defend the decision to the instructor or another team.

## Observe

- Tiny whoop damage is often small but consequential.
- Students tend to underestimate canopy shift, motor debris, and marginal props.
- A relaunch decision is stronger when it cites evidence rather than optimism.

## Why

Crash recovery is part of piloting. A course that teaches launch without teaching inspection is incomplete.

## Artifact

- completed triage sheet
- photo or note set documenting the key findings
- one short relaunch or grounding rationale

## Extension

If the rig is not cleared, assign a bench-proof retest path before the next live session.

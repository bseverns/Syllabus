# Assessment rubric

Use this rubric for a short post-secondary primer sequence built around an Air65-class ELRS whoop.

## 1. Safety discipline

Exceeds:

- performs props-off, power, and relaunch discipline without prompting
- catches unsafe assumptions in self or peers

Meets:

- follows bench and launch procedures reliably
- can explain why a stop condition applies

Below:

- treats the checklist as optional or cosmetic
- cannot explain why work should pause

## 2. Stack literacy

Exceeds:

- accurately explains power, control, video, and telemetry paths
- connects symptoms to the correct layer quickly

Meets:

- identifies major components and their roles
- can name likely failure layers with some prompting

Below:

- confuses link types or cannot narrate the stack coherently

## 3. Configuration proof

Exceeds:

- produces clean evidence for receiver setup, modes, and failsafe
- records what is proven versus still unproven

Meets:

- provides screenshots, diff, or notes showing minimum viable setup

Below:

- relies on memory or vague claims instead of evidence

## 4. Telemetry semantics and mapping

Exceeds:

- defines signal meaning, units, valid states, and dropout behavior clearly
- produces mappings that feel intentionally different while remaining stable

Meets:

- documents one believable source signal and one working mapping

Below:

- maps values without defining what they mean or when they are valid

## 5. Troubleshooting and maintenance

Exceeds:

- uses symptom-first reasoning and justifies relaunch or grounding decisions
- catches small whoop-specific issues such as canopy shift, motor debris, or prop damage

Meets:

- performs a competent inspection and states a plausible next step

Below:

- guesses at causes without checking obvious failure layers

## 6. Artifacts and communication

Exceeds:

- submits a complete portfolio bundle with clear naming, logs, and concise interpretation

Meets:

- submits the required artifacts in a usable format

Below:

- artifacts are incomplete, ambiguous, or not replayable

## Minimum passing bundle

Students should submit:

1. one completed bench-proof checklist
2. one config export, diff, or screenshot set
3. one telemetry capture or replayable dataset
4. one mapping sheet
5. one session log
6. one troubleshooting or post-crash triage narrative

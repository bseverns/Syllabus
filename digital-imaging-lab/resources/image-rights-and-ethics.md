# Image Rights & Ethics — Student-Friendly Notes

> **Local notes (edit for this implementation):**  
> - School photo / media release policy summary:  
> - Where students’ work may or may not be shared (hallway only, school website, etc.):  

We will come back to these ideas many times.

## Consent

- Ask permission before photographing people in the class.
- If someone says “no,” we respect that. No questions, no pressure.
- We do not share or post images of classmates without their permission.

## Attribution

- If you use an image you did not make:
  - Check if you’re allowed to use it (license, public domain, etc.).
  - Credit the creator (name / source / link if possible).

## Editing honestly

- Editing is part of photography. Cropping, color changes, collages—these are creative tools.
- But images can also be used to mislead:
  - Removing or adding people.
  - Changing context to make someone look bad.
- We will talk about:
  - When editing is clearly artistic or playful.
  - When editing might change the meaning in harmful ways.

## AI-generated images

- Some images now are made by algorithms trained on millions of other images.
- Questions we’ll keep asking:
  - Who made the training data?
  - Who benefits from the tool?
  - Whose style, face, or work is being borrowed or copied?

You don’t have to resolve these questions, but you do have to notice them.

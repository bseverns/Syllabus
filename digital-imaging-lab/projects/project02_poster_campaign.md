## Goal

Design a poster that uses **image + text** to communicate a clear message.

Examples:
- Advertise a school event.
- Raise awareness about an issue.
- Promote a fictional band, game, or story world.

## Requirements

- At least **one photograph** (original when possible).
- At least **one clear phrase** of text (headline).
- Use of **layers** to combine elements.
- Intentional choices about:
  - Color (palette or mood).
  - Typography (font size, style, alignment).
  - Composition (where the eye goes first).

## Process outline

1. **Brainstorm topics**
   - Make a quick list of 3–5 possible poster topics.
   - Pick one that feels urgent or exciting.

2. **Collect assets**
   - Capture or find images that fit the topic.
   - Sketch 2–3 thumbnail layouts on paper.

3. **Build in the editor**
   - Set up a canvas at poster size (e.g., 11×17 inches at 150–300 DPI).
   - Place background image(s).
   - Add text on separate layers.
   - Adjust hierarchy: what do we read first?

4. **Iterate**
   - Create at least **two** layout variations.
   - Ask a peer: “What do you notice first? What is this about?”

5. **Finalize**
   - Clean up stray marks, fix spelling, check readability.
   - Export as a high-quality PNG or PDF.

6. **Reflect**
   - Short written response:
     - Who is this poster for?
     - What do you want them to feel or do?
     - Which design decision mattered most?

## Teacher notes

- Introduce basic poster design principles: hierarchy, contrast, alignment, repetition.
- For students who finish early: have them create a **series** of two posters with the same campaign but different moods (e.g., “loud” vs. “quiet”).

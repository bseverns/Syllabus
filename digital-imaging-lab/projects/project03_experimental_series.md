## Goal

Create a series of **3–5 related images** that explore a theme using:
- Color grading,
- Retouching,
- Glitch techniques,
- And/or simple generative/code-based transformations.

The series should feel like it belongs together.

## Theme prompts (pick one or invent your own)

- “Signals / Noise”
- “Things I Can’t Say Out Loud”
- “My City at Night”
- “Memory Errors”
- “Future Artifacts”
- “Comfort / Danger”

## Process

1. **Choose a theme**
   - Write 1–2 sentences about what you want to explore.

2. **Collect or create source material**
   - Photos, scans, screenshots, generated images, etc.
   - Plan at least 2 more images than you think you’ll need.

3. **Experiment**
   - Try at least **two different experimental techniques**, such as:
     - Heavy color grading to shift mood.
     - Glitch (databending, overlaying, cutting/pasting).
     - Repeated cropping of the same scene.
     - Simple p5.js scripts that draw or filter images.

4. **Curate**
   - Choose the 3–5 images that best fit together.
   - Arrange them in a sequence (which goes first, last?).

5. **Write an artist statement**
   - 1–2 paragraphs:
     - What is your theme?
     - What techniques did you use?
     - How did your images change as you worked?

## Teacher notes

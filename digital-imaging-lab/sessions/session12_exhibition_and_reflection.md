## Goals

- Present Experimental Series work.
- Practice giving and receiving critique.
- Reflect on growth over the course.

## A-Block (≈45 min)

### 1. Final setup (10–20 min)

Students:

- Export final series images in display format (JPG/PNG or printed).
- Finalize short artist statements.

Teacher:

- Arrange images:
  - On walls or tables (if printed),
  - In a slideshow or online gallery (if digital).

### 2. Quiet viewing (10–15 min)

- Everyone walks around (or watches the slideshow) in silence.
- They note:
  - One series that sticks with them.
  - One surprising technique.

## B-Block (≈45 min)

### 3. Critique & celebration (25–30 min)

Options (pick or combine):

- **Small-group circles** where each student:
  - Summarizes their theme.
  - Shares one challenge and one success.
- **Whole-group highlights**:
  - Students shout out peers’ work they admired and why.

Focus on:

- Naming technical strengths.
- Respecting vulnerability in concept.

### 4. Written reflection (10–15 min)

Prompt options:

- How has your relationship to digital images changed?
- Which piece are you most proud of, and what did it take to make it?
- What would you do next if you had 3 more weeks?

### 5. Closing (5 min)

- Thank students for their work.
- Invite them to keep making and noticing images beyond class.

## After the course

- Archive student work (with permissions).
- Note what to adjust in future iterations:
  - Pacing,
  - Tools,
  - Project scaffolds.

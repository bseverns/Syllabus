## Goals

- Introduce tone adjustments (brightness/contrast, levels).
- Introduce basic color shifts (temperature, saturation).
- Finish Pixel Self-Portraits for mini-critique.

## A-Block (≈45 min)

### 1. Warm-up comparison (10 min)

- Show before/after of an image where only tone/color changed.
- Ask students to describe the mood difference.

### 2. Mini-lesson: tone controls (15–20 min)

Demonstrate on a sample image:

- Brightness / Contrast.
- Levels (if your editor has a straightforward version).
- Shadows / Highlights (optional).

Talk about:

- Losing detail vs. gaining clarity.
- Not over-crushing blacks or whites (unless on purpose).

### 3. Mini-lesson: color controls (10–15 min)

- White balance / temperature (cool vs warm).
- Saturation / vibrance.

Show:

- Same image with different color moods (cool, warm, desaturated).

## B-Block (≈45 min)

### 4. Work time: tone & color on pixel portraits (30–35 min)

- Students open their pixel portraits.
- Adjust:
  - Tone to clarify important features.
  - Color to create a mood.
- Save at least two versions:
  - Version A: closer to natural.
  - Version B: exaggerated / experimental.

### 5. Mini-critique (10–15 min)

- Put pixel portraits into a quick slideshow or grid.
- For each, ask:
  - What feels like this person?
  - What technical choices helped?

### 6. Exit ticket (5 min)

Prompt:

> Which version of your pixel portrait feels most like you? Why?

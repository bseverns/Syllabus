## Goals

- Introduce layers and the idea of stacking images.
- Introduce basic masking / erasing to blend images.
- Begin a collage-based self-portrait or identity piece.

## A-Block (≈45 min)

### 1. Warm-up: physical stacking (10 min)

- On the board or projector, show a simple layered diagram:
  - Background color
  - Photo
  - Text
- Use transparent paper or simple slides to demonstrate “stacking.”

### 2. Mini-lesson: layers (15–20 min)

In editor:

- Show the Layers panel.
- Add:
  - Solid color layer.
  - Photo layer.
  - Text layer.
- Move layers above/below each other.
- Toggle visibility (eye icon).

### 3. Mini-lesson: masks / selection (10–15 min)

- Demonstrate a simple mask or eraser:
  - Roughly cut out a figure from a background.
  - Feather the edge slightly (if supported).
- Emphasize non-destructive editing if your tool supports masks.

## B-Block (≈45 min)

### 4. Collage project start (30–35 min)

Students create a new file for a **collage self-portrait**:

- Use:
  - Their pixel portrait,
  - Additional photos or found textures,
  - Simple shapes/colors.
- Goal: an image that says something about them beyond literal likeness.

Teacher:

- Provide a library of textures/patterns if possible.
- Encourage experimentation more than cleanliness at this stage.

### 5. Quick pair share (5–10 min)

- Students show their layers panel to a partner and explain:
  - What each layer contributes.
  - What they might try next.

### 6. Exit ticket (5 min)

Prompt:

> How did thinking in layers change how you built your image today?

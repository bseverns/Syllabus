# Session 04 – Recording Sound Sketches

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Performance + recording  
- **Goals:**
  - Perform and record short improvisations based on gesture maps.
  - Label recordings in a way that ties back to each drawing.
- **Deliverables:**
  - 30–60 second sound sketch per student or pair.

## Before class (instructor)

- Set up recording station(s) and test levels.
- Decide whether students record solo or in pairs.

## In-class plan

### 0–10 min — Setup & recap

- Review recording basics:
  - Input levels
  - Avoiding clipping
  - Staying aware of background noise.

### 10–65 min — Record gesture map performances

- Rotating through stations:
  - Students perform their gesture map (30–60s).
  - Partner or instructor runs the recorder.
- Name files to match drawings, e.g.:
  - `studentname_gesturemap1_take1.wav`.

### 65–80 min — Group listen (selected clips)

- Play 3–5 sketches.
- Show the corresponding gesture maps.
- Discuss:
  - “Did the sound match the drawing?”

### 80–90 min — Exit ticket

- “One moment in my recording that surprised me was…”

## After class / Homework

- Instructor backs up recordings and associates them with each student’s drawing.

# Session 05 – Layers and Loops

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Lab  
- **Goals:**
  - Introduce layering and looping to build thicker textures.
  - Encourage contrast (thin vs dense, soft vs loud).
- **Deliverables:**
  - A short layered sketch (60–90 seconds).

## Before class (instructor)

- Import recordings into the DAW, or show students how to do so.
- Prepare a simple example with two layers.

## In-class plan

### 0–15 min — Listen to an example

- Play a piece that:
  - Starts with one voice
  - Adds layers over time.
- Ask:
  - “Where does it feel thick? Where does it thin out?”

### 15–65 min — Student layering lab

- Students:
  - Import their main gesture map recording.
  - Add at least one more layer:
    - Another recording
    - A copy with different volume/panning
    - Simple loops repeated at different times.
- Encourage use of contrast:
  - Quiet sections vs loud
  - Sparse vs busy.

### 65–80 min — Small-group listening

- Groups of 3:
  - Share layered sketches.
  - Peers note where they heard the biggest change.

### 80–90 min — Exit ticket

- “One place I intentionally changed the texture was…”

## After class / Homework

- Students can keep refining layers at home if they have access.

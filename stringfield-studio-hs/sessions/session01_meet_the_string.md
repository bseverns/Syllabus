# Session 01 – Meet the String

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Hands-on exploration + discussion  
- **Goals:**
  - Introduce the StringField (or similar instrument) and basic safety.
  - Let students explore different ways of exciting the string.
  - Start noticing how gesture changes sound.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Informal list of gesture ideas and first impressions.

## Before class (instructor)

- Set up instrument(s) and test for:
  - Stable tuning (if relevant)
  - Safe volumes (amps/monitors)
- Decide how many students per station.

## In-class plan

### 0–15 min — Welcome & overview

- Introduce the course:
  - “We’ll treat this string like a pen that draws in sound.”
- Basic safety:
  - Respect for gear.
  - Volume and listening.

### 15–45 min — Free exploration (structured)

- In small groups, students:
  - Try plucking, tapping, scraping, muting, sliding.
  - Are encouraged to discover at least one “weird” sound.
- Instructor prompts:
  - “What happens if you touch closer to the bridge?”
  - “What if you move slowly vs quickly?”

### 45–65 min — Group share: gesture brainstorm

- On the board, list gesture categories:
  - Plucks, bows, taps, slides, scratches, mutes…
- Students suggest names:
  - “Short ping,” “ghost slide,” “buzz scrape,” etc.

### 65–85 min — Quick demos & vocabulary

- Invite volunteers to demonstrate 1–2 gestures.
- As a group, decide on a working name for each.

### 85–90 min — Exit ticket

- “Name one gesture you want to explore more next time.”

## After class / Homework

- Optional: students sketch (draw) one gesture they liked.

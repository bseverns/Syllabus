# Family Sound Lab

A portable mixed-age family `PILOT` built around listening, Foley, cueing, and a short live sound story. Recording is optional; no account or participant device is required.

- **Audience:** mixed-age family teams with participating caregiver
- **Format:** one 90-minute workshop
- **Screen load:** `S0` default; optional `S1–S2` recording
- **Equipment:** `E0` default; `E2` with recording/editing
- **Readiness:** `PILOT`

Teams rotate sound-maker, conductor, listener, and recorder roles. Public performance, voices, and file retention are always optional.

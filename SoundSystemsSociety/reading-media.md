# Readings and Media — Sound / Systems / Society

This list is a starting point for the course. Treat it as a living document: swap in local materials, add zines and blog posts, bring in recordings that matter to your context.

## Anchor texts (from the brief)

- **Lloyd Bradley – _Bass Culture: When Reggae Was King_ (excerpt)**
  - Historical grounding in Jamaican sound system culture and its diasporic spread.
- **Documentary film – _Dub Echoes_ (dir. Bruno Natal)**
  - Traces the connections between Jamaican dub, sound systems, and later electronic music.
- **DIY sound system blog / zine materials**
  - At least one accessible, practical resource on building and running DIY systems.
- **Dave Randall – _Sound System: The Political Power of Music_**
  - Broad argument about music, politics, and how sound systems can challenge or reinforce power.

## Suggested additions (examples and placeholders)

These are organized by theme; plug in the specific articles, chapters, or media you have access to.

### Listening as method

- Pauline Oliveros – selections from “Deep Listening” writings.
- Salomé Voegelin – chapters from _Listening to Noise and Silence_.

### Sound, policing, and control

- Articles on sirens, school bells, and protest sound.
- Local reporting on noise ordinances and nightlife regulation.

### Labor and venues

- Interviews with sound engineers, venue staff, and community organizers.
- Technical riders and stage plots from local bands or touring acts.

### Platforms and streaming

- Essays on platform capitalism and streaming music.
- Technical explainer on codecs and streaming architectures.

### Community projects and interventions

- Documentation of community soundwalks.
- Guides to listening sessions, story circles, or mobile PA practices.

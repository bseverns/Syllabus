# Sound Hunters

> **ADAPT skeleton.** [Exploration Sound Design](../../SECONDARY/ExplorationSoundDesign/README.md) is a high-school, 22-session source. This is a listening-and-performance adaptation, not a DAW course.

## Promise

Children listen for sounds, imitate them with body and object percussion, arrange a short sound story, and perform it together.

## Smallest delivery shape

1. **Sound walk** — listen, point, imitate, and sort sounds as loud/soft, near/far, fast/slow.
2. **Foley table** — test safe everyday objects and name what each sound could become.
3. **Sound story** — arrange three to five sounds for a place, weather event, animal, or journey.
4. **Listening performance** — perform, listen back as a group, and name one sound to change.

## Tote

Safe Foley objects, paper, cups, blocks, fabric, dried beans in sealed containers, rhythm cards, painter's tape, and one optional facilitator recording device/playback speaker.

## Keep it K–2

- Protect ears: no forced loudness and no headphone requirement.
- Use sound words, gestures, and picture cards before notation.
- The screen, if used, is only a brief shared recording/playback moment.

## Reuse, do not copy

Borrow listening, Foley, cue, and reflection moves from [Exploration Sound Design](../../SECONDARY/ExplorationSoundDesign/README.md). Do not import its account, editing, or production workflow.

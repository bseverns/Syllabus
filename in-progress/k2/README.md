## Shared operating model

### Schedule and staffing

- **Audience:** Pre-K–Grade 2; begin with the oldest/most independent band if piloting across a wide age range.
- **Schedule:** 4 × 60-minute sessions; weekly is the default, twice weekly is acceptable if projects can be stored safely.
- **Group:** 8–12 learners. One lead can run 8; add an assistant at 10+ learners, for cutting-intensive work, or whenever participant support needs require it.
- **Room:** circle/demo edge, build tables or floor trays, materials table, and a clear parking shelf. Keep the same zones each session.

### 60-minute session rhythm

- **5 min:** arrival ritual and one visible prompt
- **5 min:** short demo with one new material or action
- **20 min:** build/play sprint 1
- **5 min:** water, movement, materials reset
- **15 min:** build/play sprint 2
- **10 min:** share, one documentation trace, cleanup, and parking

Do not cut the last ten minutes. A named tray and one spoken/photo/sketch next step are how four short sessions stay connected.

### Shared safety and access moves

- Adults manage cutting, hot glue, electrical setup, and any small/loose parts that are not suitable for a specific learner.
- Offer pre-cut, pre-punched, larger-grip, and partner roles before a learner has to ask for them.
- Use picture prompts, gestures, spoken explanation, and demonstration as valid alternatives to writing.
- Keep a quiet observation role and a lower-sensory work spot available; no learner must make a loud sound, move quickly, or touch an unfamiliar material.
- Use a one-sentence stop/reset: **“Hands off; this is the next safe move.”** Relaunch quickly.

### Evidence of learning

At each session, capture the lightest useful trace: one photo plus a spoken note, a marked sketch, a facilitator quote, or a parking card. Assess four observable habits:

| Look-for | Beginning | Growing | Ready for the next challenge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Makes | joins a build with support | completes a visible action | combines actions purposefully |
| Tests | watches a test | repeats a test | changes one variable after a result |
| Explains | points or names a part | says what happened | connects an action to an effect or audience |
| Cares | follows a reset with support | returns materials | helps keep a shared build usable |

## Promotion rule

A pilot can move to `GO` only after one documented delivery confirms the timing, materials quantities, safety/access needs, and facilitator flow. Record what changed in the course file; do not promote from a desk review alone.

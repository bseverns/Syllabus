# Tiny Machines

> **PILOT skeleton.** This is the K–2 wrapper for [Machines That Move](../machines-that-move.md), not a second mechanism curriculum.

## Promise

Children make one visible movement at a time—spin, slide, swing, lift, or flap—then combine it into a moving creature or tiny scene.

## Smallest delivery shape

1. **Make a hinge** — paper/cardboard flap and brad pivot.
2. **Make a slider or wheel** — compare two ways to move a character.
3. **Make a hand crank** — turn a cam or wheel to repeat a motion.
4. **Make it tell a story** — add one character, one action, and a show-and-tell.

## Tote

Cardboard, index cards, brads, tape, straws, skewers, bottle caps, rubber bands, markers, child-safe scissors, and optional LEGO. Motors are a facilitator enrichment, not a prerequisite.

## Keep it K–2

- One mechanism word per session; children may demonstrate before naming it.
- Pre-cut stiff card and pre-punch holes when fine motor load would block the idea.
- No participant logins. A camera or projected close-up is optional S1 documentation only.

## Reuse, do not copy

Use the mechanism vocabulary, materials, and final-object logic from [Machines That Move](../machines-that-move.md). Do not import its 8–12-week scope into this four-session version.

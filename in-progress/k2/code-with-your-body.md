# Code With Your Body

> **PILOT skeleton.** There is no exact K–2 packet in the repo. The closest local lineage is [Scratch Game Design](../../PRIMARY/Scratch-Game-Design-Animation_Grades3-5/README.md); this offering keeps its sequence, event, and debugging ideas unplugged.

## Promise

Children give clear instructions, follow them, notice when an instruction fails, and change one step to make an obstacle course or movement game work.

## Smallest delivery shape

1. **Command game** — use `go`, `stop`, `turn`, and `repeat` cards.
2. **Path game** — guide a partner through a taped floor path.
3. **Bug hunt** — act out a wrong instruction, name the problem, and change one card.
4. **Invent a routine** — teams make a short movement algorithm another team can run.

## Tote

Painter's tape, arrow cards, start/finish cards, simple picture-command cards, cones or soft markers, clipboards, crayons, and a large floor space.

## Keep it K–2

- Commands must be visible, physical, and no more than three to five cards long.
- Every learner alternates between coder, runner, and debugger.
- No screens, accounts, or reading-heavy syntax.

## Reuse, do not copy

Borrow event/sequence/debug vocabulary from [Scratch Game Design](../../PRIMARY/Scratch-Game-Design-Animation_Grades3-5/README.md). Do not present Scratch blocks or require a computer.

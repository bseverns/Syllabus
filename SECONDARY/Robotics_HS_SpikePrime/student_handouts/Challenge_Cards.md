# Start Here: Challenge Cards

Before every run: clear the lane or ring, put hands outside the area, and wait for the instructor's start signal. Stop the program before touching or adjusting a robot.

### Maze Navigation
- Reach the goal without touching walls.
- Use at least one sensor.
- First draw or say your route, run once, then record one change before a second run.

### SuGo Battle
- Stay inside the ring.
- Push opponent out.
- Win two of three rounds.
- Teams have three minutes to set up. Do not touch either robot while a round is running; wait for the instructor to call the round finished before retrieval.

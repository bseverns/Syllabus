# Robotics with LEGO Spike Prime — Syllabus
- Duration: 12 sessions (1 hour each)
- Goals: Build, code, and compete using LEGO Spike Prime.
- Culmination: Maze challenge + SuGo competition.

## ClassHub Delivery Map

| Phase | Required private evidence | ClassHub materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sessions 1–3 — build/movement | Build inspection, versioned program, movement/turn test, and next-step note | [Build Instructions](../student_handouts/Build_Instructions.md); [Coding Tutorials](../student_handouts/Coding_Tutorials.md); private code/image upload; reflection |
| Sessions 4–7 — sensors/maze | Sensor readings, obstacle behavior, maze test result, failure point, and revision | [Coding Tutorials](../student_handouts/Coding_Tutorials.md); [Challenge Cards](../student_handouts/Challenge_Cards.md); private code/video upload; midpoint reflection |
| Sessions 8–11 — SuGo/strategy | Robot configuration, strategy sketch, match observation, code/build change, and final inspection | [Challenge Cards](../student_handouts/Challenge_Cards.md); private files/video; design-log reflection |
| Session 12 — tournament | Final robot/code, team role evidence, repair note, and retrospective | [Reflection Prompts](../student_handouts/Reflection_Prompts.md); private portfolio upload; optional consent-cleared gallery clip |

ClassHub evidence does not authorize robot operation or certify competition readiness; instructor inspection and live ring safety remain controlling. Do not use win/loss ranking as the learning record.

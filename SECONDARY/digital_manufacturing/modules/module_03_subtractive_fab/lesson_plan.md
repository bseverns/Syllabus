# Lesson Plan
**Why this matters:** Machines follow the logic you give them. Clear logic becomes clean parts.

## Objectives
- Understand today's core ideas.
- Practice with guided steps.
- Produce a small artifact or result.

## Plan (2 hours)
- 0:00–0:15 — Project the [CNC anatomy reference](../../docs/visuals/cnc-router-anatomy.jpg), name gantry, spindle, bed, rails, and controller, then compare only those names with the instructor-approved local setup. Its [CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution](../../docs/visuals/README.md) is maintained with the asset.
- 0:15–0:50 — Guided build
- 0:50–1:30 — Studio work
- 1:30–2:00 — Share & reflect

## Safety
- Zero before you go. Retract before rapid moves. Eye protection.
- The anatomy reference is not local operating instruction; the instructor retains control of setup, workholding, zeroing, and first run.


> *Focus:* CNC, workholding, zeroing, feeds & speeds

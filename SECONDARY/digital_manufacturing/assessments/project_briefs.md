# Project Briefs

## Mini‑Project 1 — Nameplate (Laser/CNC)
- Goal: Clean edges, correct spelling, safe hold‑downs.
- Deliverable: Physical plate + logbook entry.

## Mini‑Project 2 — Parametric Keychain (3D Print)
- Goal: Variables for thickness and hole diameter.
- Deliverable: STL + printed part + screenshot of parameters.

## Final Project — Hybrid Assembly
- Goal: Combine printed and milled parts for a function (hinge, latch, stand, etc.).
- Deliverable: Models, files, artifact, and 1‑page reflection.

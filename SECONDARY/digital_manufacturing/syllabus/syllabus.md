# Digital Manufacturing — Studio/Lab (12 Sessions)

**Audience:** Middle–High School • **Format:** 12 × 2 hours = 24 hours • **Mode:** Studio/Lab  
**Voice:** Student‑facing

## What you'll do
- Generate 3D models and 2D vectors for fabrication.
- Translate models to toolpaths and **read G‑code** at a glance.
- Fabricate safely on CNCs and 3D printers with instructor support.
- Build **hybrid assemblies** from additive and subtractive parts.
- Document decisions, parameters, and results like a designer.

## Supported machine boundary

Repository machine assets are built around the **Genmitsu Cubiko** and **LulzBot Mini 2/3**. Other printers or CNC machines require local slicer/CAM, material, connection, sample-file, safety, and recovery validation before delivery; repository files do not authorize a machine run.

## How you'll be assessed
- **Design Logbook (30%)** — sketches, parameters, screenshots, tests.
- **Mini‑Projects (40%)** — each phase yields a physical artifact.
- **Final Project (20%)** — a complete design‑to‑fabrication object.
- **Reflection (10%)** — a short write‑up on choices and impacts.

## Safety rhythm
- Tie hair/sleeves back. Glasses on. Dust extraction on.
- **Zero before you go** (X/Y/Z). Hand on e‑stop. First pass: shallow.
- Ask “What can go wrong?” before pressing Start.

> *The rhythm of machines is a form of thought.*

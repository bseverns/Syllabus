# Overview — From Bits to Matter

You will practice **concept → computation → craft**. Files become toolpaths; toolpaths become motion; motion becomes parts. We work with CNC routers and 3D printers. You generate designs; with your teacher, you bring them to life.

- **Tool‑agnostic skills** — tolerances, offsets, parametrics, feeds/speeds.
- **Documentation** — your logbook is part of the artifact.
- **Ethics** — materials matter; design with care.

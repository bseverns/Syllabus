# Assessment Rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (A) | Proficient (B) | Developing (C) | Emerging (D) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Design Process | Clear intent, multiple iterations, justified parameters | Iterates with notes | Minimal iteration | Single attempt |
| Craft & Fit | Clean edges, correct tolerances, functions as intended | Minor defects | Fit issues | Non‑functional |
| Technical Literacy | Reads/edits G‑code and slicer settings with purpose | Uses presets thoughtfully | Relies on defaults | Unaware of settings |
| Safety & Setup | Independent, consistent, anticipates risks | Follows checklist | Needs reminders | Misses critical steps |
| Documentation | Logbook is complete and reflective | Mostly complete | Partial | Sparse |

# Digital Manufacturing — Studio/Lab (12 Sessions)

**Tagline:** *From concept to code to cut — you design, the machines sing.*

This repository contains a complete, student‑facing course on digital manufacturing for middle–high school learners. It emphasizes **design generation** by students and **guided fabrication** on machines with the instructor, building literacy across CAD, CAM, slicing, and machine operation.

## What's inside
- A full **12-session syllabus** (student‑facing) with outcomes and rubrics
- Eight **modules** with lesson plans, student handouts, demo scripts, and reflections
- Ready‑to‑run **G‑code** for the Genmitsu Cubiko and **Cura profiles** for LulzBot Mini 2/3
- Posters and workflow diagrams for classroom use
- Licensed MIT for maximum remixability

## Machines & Software (suggested)
- **CNC:** Genmitsu Cubiko (GRBL) → Candle/UGS
- **3D Printers:** LulzBot Mini 2 / Mini 3 → Cura LulzBot Edition
- **CAD:** Tinkercad, Fusion 360, Onshape
- **CAM:** Carbide Create, Fusion Manufacture

## Structure
See `syllabus/syllabus.md` and each `modules/module_*` for details. Printable posters in `docs/printable_posters`. Sample G‑code and slicer profiles in `resources/`.

> *Precision is empathy for material.*

---

### Quickstart for instructors
1. Read `syllabus/overview.md` and `syllabus/syllabus.md`.
2. Test the **hello_square.nc** on the Cubiko with air‑cut first.
3. Import Cura profiles in `resources/cura_profiles/` as a baseline, then tune per filament.
4. Copy `assessments/` forms for your students’ design logs and critiques.

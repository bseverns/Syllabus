# Sensing & Gesture (College, 14 Weeks)

This repository contains a college-level version of **Sensing & Gesture**, designed as a 14-week studio/seminar.

Students explore how bodies, sensors, and systems entangle. We treat **gesture** not just as physical movement but as signal, protocol, and politics. Students experiment with cameras, microcontrollers, and wearables; prototype gesture-driven systems; and build a final piece that uses sensing to register and respond to embodied action.

## Files

- `COURSE_DESCRIPTION.md` – catalog-style overview and learning goals  
- `SCHEDULE.md` – 14-week outline  
- `ASSIGNMENTS.md` – grading sketch and week-linked deliverables  
- `READING_LIST.md` – core texts grouped by theme  
- `READING_SCHEDULE.md` – which readings map to which weeks  
- `SUPPLEMENTAL_RESOURCES.md` – tools, libraries, and practice references  
- `INSTRUCTOR_NOTES.md` – teaching guidance, tech tiers, safety/ethics notes  
- `LOCAL_NOTES_TEMPLATE.md` – template for local site/constraints notes  
- `sessions/` – per-week session plans/stubs (Weeks 1–14)

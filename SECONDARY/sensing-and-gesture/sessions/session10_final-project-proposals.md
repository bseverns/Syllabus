# Week 10 – Final Project Proposals

## At a glance

- Length: 2–3 hours  
- Theme: Scoping projects in terms of bodies, spaces, publics, and ethics.

## Goals

By the end of this week, students should:

- Deepen their understanding of this week's theme.  
- Connect at least one reading to their own gesture/sensing experiments.  
- Produce one small artifact (study, mapping, or reflection) that feeds the final project.

## Suggested readings / media

- Petra Kuppers – excerpt on community performance/eco soma.
- Institutional policy or ethics document on recording, research, or accessibility.

## Before class (prep)

- Assign the readings/media above and ask students for 3–5 bullet-point notes or questions.  
- Prepare any demos or slides you want to use to anchor discussion.

## In-class plan

Use this section to sketch your timings and activities. A typical pattern might be:

- Opening warm-up or score-based movement exercise  
- Short discussion of readings, guided by student questions  
- Lab or studio time (sketching, coding, testing, rehearsing)  
- Short shares or mini-crits

## After class / Homework

- Extend or revise the in-class study.  
- Short written or sketched response connecting the week's readings to their own work.

## Local notes

- What worked well this week?  
- What would you change next time?  
- Any logistical or space issues to remember?

**Milestones**
- Week 1: baseline working
- Week 2: at least 2 controls mapped
- Week 3: add a scene + feedback LED
- Week 4: demo + reflection

**Troubleshooting**
- Nothing prints? Check baud 115200.
- Jitter? Smooth with a deadband / simple filter.
- Clipping? Constrain 0–127 before sending.

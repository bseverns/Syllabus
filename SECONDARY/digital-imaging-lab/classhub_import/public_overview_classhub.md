# Digital Imaging Lab — Level 1

Course slug: digital_imaging_lab_level1
Grade level: 6th-9th
Session length: 90 minutes
Total sessions: 12
UI Level: secondary

## Course overview

This studio course introduces students to digital imaging as both a technical craft and a way of thinking critically about how pictures shape the world. Students learn to capture, edit, combine, and transform images while discussing consent, representation, restoration, glitch, and AI-generated imagery.

The course moves from composition and camera basics into layers, masks, posters, retouching, experimental image practices, and a final small exhibition or gallery.

## Learning goals

By the end of the course, students will be able to:

- describe pixels, resolution, and basic file formats
- capture images with stronger attention to composition and light
- use a layer-based editor for cropping, tone, color, masks, and compositing
- create short image series around a theme or feeling
- discuss ethics around consent, representation, and misleading edits
- experiment with images as code through a simple generative workflow

## What students make

Students produce:

- image scavenger hunt captures
- a pixel self-portrait
- a poster campaign piece
- an experimental image series
- short artist statements and reflections

## Course format

Each session usually includes:

1. warm-up or image discussion
2. mini-lesson
3. guided making
4. studio work
5. reflection or share-out

## Tools and materials

- phones, tablets, or cameras
- laptops or desktops
- a layer-based image editor such as Photopea, GIMP, or Photoshop
- optional printer or slideshow/gallery format for the final showcase

## Assessment

Assessment emphasizes:

- process and revision
- technical understanding
- image-making choices tied to intent
- critique and reflection

## Final showcase

Students present a short experimental image series or gallery selection with a brief artist statement and reflect on how their understanding of digital images changed over the course.

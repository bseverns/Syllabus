## Alt text

- **Pixels and tone:** Smooth and pixelated circles beside neutral, warm, and cool versions of one landscape.
- **Layers and posters:** A three-layer stack with a mask beside crowded and clearly ordered poster layouts.
- **Color and restoration:** Three mood palettes beside restore-versus-mislead editing examples.

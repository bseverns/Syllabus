# Session 1–2 visual pack

Project these files; do not turn them into a slide deck.

## Session 1

1. [`image-types.svg`](image-types.svg) — pixel image, poster, and glitch image. Ask what the eye notices first.
2. [`leading-lines-architecture.jpg`](leading-lines-architecture.jpg) — high-resolution photograph and leading-lines example.
3. [`composition-overlays.svg`](composition-overlays.svg) — rule of thirds, leading lines, and depth overlays.

## Session 2

1. [`light-direction.svg`](light-direction.svg) — front, side, and back lighting.
2. [`backlit-dreamer.jpg`](backlit-dreamer.jpg) — backlighting and silhouette example. The person is not identifiable from the image.

## Alt text

- **Image types:** Three panels show a blocky pixel landscape, a bold orange poster, and a colorful glitch image.
- **Leading lines:** Black-and-white concrete corridor with ceiling beams shrinking toward a distant vanishing point.
- **Composition overlays:** Three diagrams demonstrate rule of thirds, leading lines, and foreground-to-background depth.
- **Light direction:** Three diagrams compare front, side, and back lighting on the same face.
- **Backlit Dreamer:** Dark silhouette of a seated person against a bright city walkway and red bus.

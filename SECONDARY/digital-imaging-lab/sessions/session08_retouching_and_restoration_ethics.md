## Goals

- Introduce basic retouching tools (clone, heal).
- Practice restoring or cleaning up an image.
- Discuss ethical questions around editing people and reality.
- Launch ideas for the Experimental Series.

## A-Block (≈45 min)

### 1. Warm-up: spot the difference (10–15 min)

- Show a before/after of a retouched image:
  - Remove a small object,
  - Smooth skin slightly,
  - Restore a torn photo.
- Frame the comparison with the restore-or-mislead sheet in the [Session 3–10 visual pack](../assets/session03-10/README.md).
- Have students find changes.

### 2. Mini-lesson: retouch tools (15–20 min)

In editor:

- Demonstrate clone stamp / healing brush.
- Show:
  - Fixing dust or small scratches.
  - Repairing a torn scan.

### 3. Ethics discussion (10–15 min)

Guiding questions:

- When is retouching helpful or kind?
- When can it become harmful or dishonest?
- What about news vs. fashion vs. personal art?

Connect back to `resources/image-rights-and-ethics.md`.

## B-Block (≈45 min)

### 4. Retouching exercise (20–25 min)

Students:

- Work on a provided scanned/damaged image or a cluttered photo.
- Goal: clean it up without erasing important information.

### 5. Introduce Experimental Series (15–20 min)

- Present Project 3 prompt (see `projects/project03_experimental_series.md`).
- Have students brainstorm possible themes in a quick list.
- Ask them to circle 1–2 favorites.

### 6. Exit ticket (5 min)

Prompt:

> Name one situation where you think retouching is okay, and one where it might not be.

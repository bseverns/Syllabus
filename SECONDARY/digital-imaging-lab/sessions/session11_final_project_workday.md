## Goals

- Give sustained studio time to build Experimental Series.
- Provide structured check-ins and feedback.
- Help students plan what will be ready to show.
- Use the [Session 11–12 visual pack](../assets/session11-12/README.md) to check repetition, change, and sequence.

## A-Block (≈45 min)

### 1. Check-in circle (10–15 min)

Ask each student:

- What is your theme?
- How many images do you have started?
- What is your goal for today?

### 2. Clarify expectations (5–10 min)

By end of next class, students should have:

- 3–5 finished images in their series.
- A draft of their artist statement (can be bullet points today).

## B-Block (≈45 min)

### 3. Studio time (35–40 min)

Students work on:

- Creating, editing, and curating images.
- Starting their artist statement.

Teacher:

- Make short 3–5 minute conferences with each student:
  - Ask about concept and techniques.
  - Offer 1–2 specific suggestions (e.g., “What if all of them shared a subtle color tint?”).

### 4. Wrap-up (5–10 min)

Prompt:

> List 3 tasks you need to complete for next class:  
> 1) …  
> 2) …  
> 3) …

Collect these or have them written somewhere visible to the student.

## Prep

- For Session 12: plan exhibition format (print, slides, online gallery).
- Prepare a simple critique structure that fits your group.

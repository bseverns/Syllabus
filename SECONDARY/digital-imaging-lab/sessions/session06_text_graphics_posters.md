## Goals

- Introduce typography basics and text as image.
- Connect layers to poster design.
- Begin Poster Campaign project.

## A-Block (≈45 min)

### 1. Warm-up: poster walk (10–15 min)

- Show 3–4 posters (school event, movie, activism, etc.).
- Ask:
  - What’s the first thing you read?
  - What’s the loudest element?
  - How do image and text work together?

### 2. Mini-lesson: type & hierarchy (20–25 min)

Key ideas:

- Hierarchy: title > subtitle > small details.
- Fonts: readable vs decorative.
- Contrast: size, weight, color.

In editor:

- Show:
  - Large title text.
  - Smaller supporting text.
  - Alignment and spacing.

### 3. Introduce Project 2 (10 min)

- Present the Poster Campaign prompt (see `projects/project02_poster_campaign.md`).
- Have students pick or narrow down topic.

## B-Block (≈45 min)

### 4. Layout prototypes (30–35 min)

Students:

- Sketch 2–3 poster ideas on paper or digitally (thumbnails).
- Choose one to build in software.
- Set up canvas and place text and basic background blocks (color or image).

Teacher:

- Emphasize **blocking in** layout first, detailed polishing later.

### 5. Micro-critique (5–10 min)

- Students pair up to ask:
  - “What do you see first?”
  - “What is this about?”

### 6. Exit ticket (5 min)

Prompt:

> What is your poster trying to get someone to feel or do?

## Prep

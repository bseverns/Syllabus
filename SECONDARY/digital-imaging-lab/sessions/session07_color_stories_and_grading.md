## Goals

- Use color grading to create mood.
- Refine posters with intentional color choices.
- Introduce the idea of a color palette or set.

## A-Block (≈45 min)

### 1. Warm-up: mood matching (10–15 min)

- Show 3 small color palettes (e.g., cool blues, muted browns, neon brights).
- Project the color-mood sheet in the [Session 3–10 visual pack](../assets/session03-10/README.md).
- Ask: “Which mood do you associate with each?”

### 2. Mini-lesson: color grading (20–25 min)

In editor:

- Show:
  - Global color adjustments (Color Balance, Hue/Saturation).
  - Targeted changes (selective color, gradient overlays).
- Demonstrate:
  - Taking one image and making a “warm memory” version and a “cold future” version.

### 3. Connect to poster work (5–10 min)

- Ask: “What mood should your poster have?”  
  (Urgent, calm, mysterious, joyful, etc.)

## B-Block (≈45 min)

### 4. Poster refinement (30–35 min)

Students:

- Choose or create a color palette (3–5 main colors).
- Adjust poster to:
  - Use palette consistently.
  - Improve contrast/readability.

Teacher:

- Help with accessibility (avoid super low contrast text).

### 5. Mini share (10–15 min)

- Small groups: students show before/after of their poster’s color changes.
- Each person says one color decision they’re proud of.

### 6. Exit ticket (5 min)

Prompt:

> What color choice did you make today that changed your poster the most?

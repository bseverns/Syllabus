This course assumes a **layer-based image editor** plus a **browser**.

## Image editors (recommended)

- **Photopea (browser-based)**
  - Pros: Runs in the browser, Photoshop-like interface, works on Chromebooks.
  - Cons: Ads on free tier; needs reliable internet.

- **GIMP (desktop)**
  - Pros: Free, open-source, powerful; good for labs with install privileges.
  - Cons: Interface can feel heavy; more setup.

- **Adobe Photoshop**
  - Pros: Industry standard; smooth workflow if your institution already has licenses.
  - Cons: Cost and account management.

## Code-based image tools

- **p5.js Web Editor**
  - Great for inline demonstrations of image drawing and filtering.
  - Works in any modern browser.

If your context has strict IT policies, choose one primary editor and document any deviations from the lesson plans in the local notes at the top of the relevant session files.

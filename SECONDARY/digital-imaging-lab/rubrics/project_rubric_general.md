Scale: 4 = Exceeds, 3 = Meets, 2 = Developing, 1 = Beginning

| Criteria                 | 4 — Exceeds                           | 3 — Meets                             | 2 — Developing                         | 1 — Beginning                         |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Craft & Technique        | Precise edits, clean files, careful use of tools; choices clearly deliberate. | Solid control of tools; small mistakes do not distract. | Some control, but frequent technical issues (resolution, rough edges, etc.). | Tools used minimally or incorrectly; technical issues block understanding. |
| Concept & Intent         | Strong, original idea; clear point of view; all choices support the concept. | Clear idea; most choices support the concept. | Idea is present but underdeveloped; visual choices sometimes feel random. | Concept unclear; work feels accidental or incomplete. |
| Risk & Experimentation   | Takes brave risks; tries new tools/approaches; documents experiments. | Tries at least one new technique beyond demos. | Stays close to examples; limited experimentation. | Avoids experimentation; minimal engagement beyond requirements. |
| Process & Revision       | Multiple drafts; visible iteration and improvement; reflects thoughtfully. | At least one revision; some evidence of improvement. | Limited revision; work mostly first draft. | Little to no revision; rushed or incomplete. |
| Reflection / Statement   | Writing clearly connects technical choices to meaning and audience. | Writing explains idea and some technical choices. | Writing is brief or vague; technical and conceptual links are unclear. | Little or no reflection; cannot articulate choices. |

Use this as a starting point; adjust descriptors to your students and context.

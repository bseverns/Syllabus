| Criteria           | 4 — Exceeds                   | 3 — Meets                     | 2 — Developing                     | 1 — Beginning                      |
|--------------------|------------------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Engagement         | Consistently focused, self-directed; helps peers productively. | Usually focused; works steadily with occasional redirection. | Often distracted; needs frequent reminders. | Rarely on task; disrupts others. |
| Collaboration      | Listens, shares resources, gives constructive feedback. | Participates in group work; generally respectful. | Uneven participation; sometimes dismissive. | Avoids collaboration or is unkind. |
| Care for Equipment | Handles gear responsibly; helps with setup/cleanup without prompting. | Uses equipment appropriately when reminded. | Occasionally careless; needs reminders about safe/appropriate use. | Regularly careless or unsafe with equipment. |
| Reflection         | Thoughtful in critiques; asks questions and connects ideas. | Participates in critiques when prompted. | Seldom contributes to critique. | Avoids critique spaces or derails. |

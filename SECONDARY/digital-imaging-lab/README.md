# Digital Imaging Lab — Level 1 (Grades 6–9)

Digital images are how we remember, argue, dream, and design in public.

This repo holds a 12-session, 90-minutes-per-session course for middle and early high school students that treats digital imaging as both craft and inquiry: how pictures work, how they’re made, and what they *do* in the world.

## Course snapshot

- **Audience:** Grades 6–9 (mixed middle / early high)
- **Length:** 12 sessions × 90 minutes
- **Format:** Studio / lab, with mini-lessons + sustained project time
- **Focus:**
  - How digital images work (pixels, resolution, formats)
  - Capturing images with intention (composition, light)
  - Editing & compositing (layers, masks, color grading)
  - Experimental practices (glitch, generative images)
  - Ethics of images (consent, representation, AI imagery)
- **Final outcome:** A small exhibition or digital gallery with artist statements

## How to use this repo

- **New instructor?**  
  Start with `syllabus/DigitalImaging_Level1_syllabus.md` and `syllabus/schedule-at-a-glance.md`.  
  Then prep the next session in `sessions/`.

- **Planning long-term?**  
  Look at the three anchor projects in `projects/` to see how skills accumulate.

- **Adapting for your tools?**  
  This course assumes access to a **layer-based image editor** (e.g., GIMP, Photopea, Photoshop) and cameras (phones, tablets, point-and-shoot).  
  Swap in whatever tools you have and fill in the “Local notes” block at the top of each session file.

## Directory guide

- `syllabus/` — high-level syllabus and schedule.
- `sessions/` — lesson plans for each of the 12 meetings (with local notes at the top).
- `projects/` — detailed prompts for major projects.
- `resources/` — software options, setup checklists, and image ethics notes.
- `rubrics/` — common rubrics for projects and participation.
- `assets/` — ready-to-project visuals with alt text and attribution.

## Local notes convention

At the top of each session file you’ll find a block like this:

> **Local notes (edit before teaching):**  
> - Site / room:  
> - Devices:  
> - Image editor(s):  
> - File saving path:  
> - Special constraints / adaptations:  

Edit these lines to reflect your actual lab, hardware, and policies so future-you (or another instructor) remembers how the plan was implemented in this context.

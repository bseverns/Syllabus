# Tolerances & Fits (FDM, student baseline)
- **Through holes:** +0.2 to +0.4 mm over nominal for M3/M4 screws.
- **Shaft to bore (printed-on-printed):** 0.2–0.3 mm clearance for free spin.
- **Snap-fits:** leave 0.2–0.5 mm relief at latch interfaces.
- **Bridges & overhangs:** reduce speed; add chamfers over supports.
- **Post-processing:** plan for **drill/ream** to final; avoid tapping printed threads for high load.

# Mechanisms Primer (Quick)
- **Gears:** transmit torque; ratio = driven_teeth / driver_teeth; watch backlash & lubrication-free print constraints.
- **Cams:** profile transforms rotation to linear/oscillatory motion; match follower stiffness and contact width.
- **Ratchet & Pawl:** one-way lock; tooth angle trades holding vs. ease of drive.
- **Four-Bar:** convert rotation to trajectories; tune bar lengths; add stops.
- **Geneva:** intermittent motion with dwell; avoid over-speeding the pin.
- **Compliant:** flexures replace joints; avoid sharp corners; mind fatigue.

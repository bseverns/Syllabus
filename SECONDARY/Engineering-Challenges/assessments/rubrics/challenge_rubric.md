# Rubric — Challenge Project (100 pts)
- **Concept & Requirements (20):** Meets task constraints; concise proposal.
- **Engineering Depth (25):** Appropriate mechanism choice(s); correct ratio/geometry.
- **Craft & Reliability (25):** Clean assembly; durable; reproducible results.
- **Evidence & Data (20):** Logs, photos, and calculations support claims.
- **Presentation (10):** Clear visuals, brief demo video, 120‑word summary.

# Intro to Sound Design & Engineering

Course slug: exploration_sound_design
Grade level: 9th-12th
Session length: 60 minutes
Total sessions: 22
UI Level: secondary

## Course overview

This high school course introduces sound design and audio engineering through a mobile- and Chromebook-friendly workflow. Students learn how to record, edit, shape, and present sound using phones, Chromebooks, BandLab, and rotating stations built around foley, synthesis, editing, and analysis.

The course treats sound as both a technical medium and a storytelling tool. Students record the world around them, turn everyday materials into instruments and effects, explore synthesis and processing, and finish with a short polished final project that can support music, podcasting, video, or game audio.

## Learning goals

By the end of the course, students will be able to:

- record cleaner audio with phones in different environments
- edit and arrange audio clips in a multi-track workflow
- use sampling and synthesis to design original sounds
- apply EQ, compression, reverb, delay, and automation with intention
- organize a session, export files clearly, and manage versions
- critique work using shared vocabulary

## What students make

Students produce:

- short field recordings and sonic IDs
- found-sound beat studies
- synthesis postcards
- foley and ADR micro-scenes
- a final audio or audio-to-picture vignette

## Course format

Each class follows a simple rhythm:

1. short hook
2. mini-lesson
3. guided practice
4. studio or station work
5. exit ticket

## Tools and materials

- phones
- Chromebooks
- BandLab
- headphones
- Korg/littleBits synth kit
- classroom file-sharing workflow such as Google Drive

## Assessment

Assessment emphasizes:

- process and versioning
- capture and editing technique
- design choices that fit the intended sound
- reflection on revision and critique

## Final showcase

Students share a final sound design piece or short sound-to-picture scene and explain one production decision they are proud of and one technical problem they solved.

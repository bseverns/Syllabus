### Assignment: Scratch Starter Remix #1 – Change One Thing

**Title:** `Scratch Starter Remix #1 – Change One Thing`

**Instructions:**

> Today you’ll open a simple starter game in Scratch and make it your own just a little bit.  
>  
> 1. Click the starter project link:  
>    • `[PASTE YOUR TESTED REMIX URL HERE BEFORE PUBLISHING]`
> 2. Click **“Remix”** (or **File → Save as a copy**) to make your own version.  
> 3. Make at least **one visible change**, for example:  
>    • Change the main character’s costume  
>    • Change the background  
>    • Change a number that controls speed  
> 4. Test your game. Make sure it still runs.  
> 5. Turn in:  
>    • The **link** to your remixed project  
>    • A sentence finishing this sentence:  
>      > “The change I made was ______ and on screen it made the game feel ______.”  
>  
> You don’t have to create a new game from scratch—your job is to notice what happens when you change one thing.

### Assignment: Control Remix – Change the Keys

**Title:** `Control Remix – Change the Keys`

**Instructions:**

> In this task you’ll change which keys control a character and how far it moves.  
>  
> 1. Open the project we used in class today, or the version you saved.  
> 2. Find the code that uses blocks like:  
>    • `when [right arrow] key pressed`  
>    • `change x by [10]` or `change y by [-10]`  
> 3. Make these changes:  
>    • Change which keys control the character (for example, switch from arrow keys to WASD).  
>    • Change at least one **step size number** (how far the sprite moves each press).  
> 4. Test:  
>    • Is the game too fast, too slow, or just right?  
> 5. Turn in:  
>    • The link to your project  
>    • A short answer:  
>      > “When I press ____, the sprite does ____. I changed the movement amount from ____ to ____ and now it feels ______.”

---

### Assignment: Win/Lose Logic – Editing Conditions

**Title:** `Win/Lose Logic – Editing Conditions`

**Instructions:**

> You will change how your game decides when you win or lose.  
>  
> 1. Open your current project.  
> 2. Find at least one **if** block, for example:  
>    • `if <touching color [red]> then`  
> 3. Make at least **two** changes, such as:  
>    • Change which color counts as a wall.  
>    • Change what happens when you hit it (lose a life, reset position, play sound).  
>    • Add a new goal sprite and show a “You win!” message when you reach it.  
> 4. Test the game and notice:  
>    • Does it feel more fair, less fair, harder, easier?  
> 5. Turn in:  
>    • The project link  
>    • A short reflection:  
>      > “I changed the condition from ______ to ______. Now, when the player ______, the game ______. This feels more/less fair because ______.”

### Assignment: Score or Timer – Add a Variable

**Title:** `Score or Timer – Add a Variable`

**Instructions:**

> You will give your game a memory: score, lives, or time.  
>  
> 1. Open your project.  
> 2. Create a new variable called one of these:  
>    • `score`, `lives`, or `time`  
> 3. Add code that:  
>    • Sets the variable to 0 at the start of the game.  
>    • Changes the variable when something good or bad happens (e.g., +1 for a coin, -1 for a hit).  
> 4. Make sure the variable is visible on screen.  
> 5. Turn in:  
>    • The link to your project  
>    • A sentence:  
>      > “My variable is called ____. It changes when ______. Players can use it to know ______.”

## Topic: Week 6 – Mini Arcade & Reflection

### Assignment: Final Game Submission

**Title:** `Final Game Submission`

**Instructions:**

> Turn in your game in its current form. It does not have to be perfect.  
>  
> Your project should:  
> • Use at least one event (key press or click)  
> • Use at least one condition (if… then…)  
> • Use at least one variable (score, lives, or time)  
> • Include some kind of feedback (sound, animation, or message)  
>  
> Turn in:  
> • A working project link  
> • A short “game card” in the private comments using this format:  
>   > Title: ______  
>   > How to play (controls): ______  
>   > Goal: ______  
>   > One thing I changed in the code: ______

# Session 9 – Practice Heats & Race Prep

**Theme:** Rehearse the event before it is the event.  
**Duration:** 90–120 minutes

---

## Goals

By the end of this session, students will:

- Understand how heats, time trials, or race groups will run.
- Complete a final tech and safety check.
- Fly practice heats with race-day commands.
- Save a final backup of the setup they plan to race.

---

## Instructor Prep

- Decide on the race format for Session 10:
  - time trial
  - heats
  - small bracket
- Print or share `materials/Race_Day_Heat_Sheet.md`.

---

## Materials

- Student quads and radios
- Indoor track
- Heat sheet
- Pre-flight and crash-recovery checklists

---

## Schedule (example for 2 hours)

### 0:00–0:15 – Race Format Briefing

- Explain:
  - pilot order
  - start commands
  - false start policy
  - marshal roles
  - what happens after a crash

### 0:15–0:35 – Final Tech Check

Students:

1. Use the pre-flight checklist.
2. Confirm their preferred profile.
3. Save a final race-day backup.
4. Confirm channel plan if using FPV.

### 0:35–1:30 – Practice Heats

- Run short practice heats or timed runs.
- Keep the focus on:
  - clean starts
  - track discipline
  - safe recovery after mistakes

### 1:30–1:50 – Debrief and Tune Lock

- Students decide what they will and will not change before race day.
- Encourage a "tune lock" mentality:
  - no random last-minute experiments

### 1:50–2:00 – Wrap & Exit Ticket

- What is your race-day plan?
- What is one thing that could still ground your quad tomorrow?

---

## Notes & Variations

- If students are very uneven in skill, split into beginner and advanced heats.
- Time trials are often easier to manage than multi-whoop racing in tight rooms.

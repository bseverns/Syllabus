# Week 9 - Practice Heats & Race Prep

**Theme:** Rehearse the event before it is the event.
**Tuesday focus:** Race format, heat procedures, final tech check, and channel plan
**Thursday focus:** Practice heats, tune lock, and race-day planning
**Class length:** Two 90-minute meetings

---

## Goals

By the end of the week, students will:

- Understand how heats, time trials, or race groups will run.
- Complete a final tech and safety check.
- Use race-day commands and roles.
- Fly practice heats or timed runs.
- Save a final backup of the setup they plan to race.
- Make a race-day plan that prioritizes safety and consistency.

---

## Instructor Prep

- Decide on the Week 10 race format:
  - time trial
  - heats
  - small bracket
  - showcase format
- Print or share `materials/Race_Day_Heat_Sheet.md`.
- Finalize or nearly finalize the track.
- Decide the channel plan if multiple FPV systems will be powered.
- Prepare a clear grounding policy for damaged or unsafe quads.
- Print or share `materials/Flight_Readiness_Card.md`.
- Print or share `materials/Student_Tune_Log_Template.md`.

---

## Materials

- Student quads and radios
- Indoor track
- Heat sheet
- Pre-flight and crash-recovery checklists
- Flight readiness card

- Shared board for pilot order and roles

## Tuesday Class - Race Procedures & Final Tech Check

### 0:00-0:20 - Race Format Briefing

Explain:

- event format
- pilot order
- start commands
- false start policy
- marshal roles
- crash/restart policy
- scoring method
- what can ground a quad

Keep the format simple enough that students can run it safely.

### 0:20-0:35 - Heat Sheet Setup

Students help complete:

- pilot list
- quad or team name
- channel if using FPV
- active profile
- notes or restrictions
- marshal assignments

### 0:35-1:10 - Final Tech Check

Students:

1. complete the pre-flight checklist
2. confirm the preferred profile
3. confirm arming and flight mode
4. inspect props, frame, canopy, antenna, and battery lead
5. confirm channel plan if using FPV
6. save a final race-prep backup
7. confirm failsafe has been verified
8. update the flight readiness card and tune log

Instructor signs off or grounds the quad until fixed.

### 1:10-1:25 - Race Commands Rehearsal

Practice without flying first:

- pilots ready
- arm
- go
- stop
- land now
- course clear

Then run one slow launch/landing rehearsal if safe.

### 1:25-1:30 - Exit Ticket

Students answer:

- My race setup is ready / not ready because...
- One thing that could still ground my quad is...

---

## Thursday Class - Practice Heats & Tune Lock

### 0:00-0:10 - Warm-Up and Safety Reset

- Revisit race commands.
- Confirm pilot order and marshal roles.
- Remind students that today is rehearsal, not experimentation.

### 0:10-1:05 - Practice Heats or Timed Runs

Run the chosen format at low stakes:

- time trials
- short heats
- launch and land drills
- clean-lap challenge

Focus on:

- clean starts
- predictable line
- safe recovery
- listening to the race director
- marshals waiting for props to stop

### 1:05-1:20 - Debrief and Tune Lock

Students decide:

- what profile they will race
- what settings are locked
- what checklist item they must remember
- what role they may serve when not flying

Emphasize no random last-minute changes before race day.

### 1:20-1:30 - Exit Ticket

Students answer:

- My race-day plan is...
- I will not change...
- I need help with...

---

## Evidence of Learning

- Student completes final tech check.
- Student saves a final race-prep backup.
- Student has a race-readiness card signed or a clear reason for not being cleared.
- Student participates safely in practice heats or timed runs.
- Student can explain race commands and roles.

---

## Notes & Variations

- Time trials are often easier than multi-whoop racing in tight rooms.
- If student skill levels vary widely, split into beginner and advanced heat groups.
- If the room gets chaotic, shorten the event format before safety degrades.

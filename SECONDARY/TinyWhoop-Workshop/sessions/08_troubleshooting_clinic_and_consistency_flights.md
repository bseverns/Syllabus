# Session 8 – Troubleshooting Clinic & Consistency Flights

**Theme:** Fix the pattern, not just the symptom.  
**Duration:** 90–120 minutes

---

## Goals

By the end of this session, students will:

- Work through a structured troubleshooting process.
- Recognize common arming flags and setup errors.
- Fly repeatable laps on a stable setup.
- Use a pre-flight checklist without prompting.

---

## Instructor Prep

- Prepare a demo rig with one or two intentional problems.
- Print or share `materials/Preflight_Checklist_student.md`.

---

## Materials

- Student quads and radios
- Laptops with Betaflight Configurator
- Printed checklists or notebooks
- Indoor track

---

## Schedule (example for 2 hours)

### 0:00–0:15 – Warm-Up & Status Check

- Ask students:
  - What is your whoop's biggest weak point right now?
  - What still feels unpredictable?
- Have each student connect briefly to confirm:
  - receiver response
  - mode behavior
  - current profile

### 0:15–0:45 – Troubleshooting Clinic

On the projector, use your intentionally broken demo rig:

1. Show a problem.
2. Ask students to hypothesize the cause.
3. Walk through a systematic process:
   - Setup tab for orientation
   - Motors tab for numbering and direction
   - Receiver and Modes tabs for switch behavior
   - arming flags or warnings

### 0:45–1:00 – Student Pair Diagnostics

- Students pair up and inspect each other's setup.
- Encourage them to narrate their reasoning.

### 1:00–1:45 – Consistency Flights

- Students use their checklist, launch, complete repeatable laps, and land.
- Focus:
  - same takeoff ritual
  - same line
  - same landing discipline

### 1:45–2:00 – Wrap & Exit Ticket

- What issue did you diagnose today?
- Are you race-ready yet? Why or why not?

---

## Notes & Variations

- If many quads still need bench work, cut flying time and stay in troubleshooting mode.
- If students are stable, run mini time trials at the end.

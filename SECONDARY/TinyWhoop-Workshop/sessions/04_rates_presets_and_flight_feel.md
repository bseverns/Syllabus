# Week 4 - Rates, Presets & Flight Feel

**Theme:** Turn numbers into feel, then test the feel.
**Tuesday focus:** Baseline presets, rates, expo, profile setup, and simulator feel tests
**Thursday focus:** Simulator time trials, rate-profile comparison flights, and reflection
**Class length:** Two 90-minute meetings

---

## Goals

By the end of the week, students will:

- Apply a sensible baseline tune or preset if one fits their hardware.
- Adjust rate and expo settings conservatively.
- Compare at least two rate profiles.
- Explain how a calmer profile differs from a more responsive one.
- Complete a clean-lap simulator time trial or challenge.
- Choose a practice profile based on evidence, not guessing.

---

## Instructor Prep

- Decide on a baseline preset or stock-tune recommendation for your fleet.
- Prepare one demo showing gentle vs more responsive rates.
- Prepare a simple comparison track or flight pattern.
- Prepare a short VelociDrone clean-lap or time-trial course.
- Confirm whether students should change actual settings, simulator settings, or both.

---

## Materials

- Student quads and radios
- Laptops with Betaflight Configurator
- VelociDrone stations if available
- Indoor flight area or short comparison course
- Tune notebooks or shared documentation space

---

## Tuesday Class - Presets, Rates & Expo

### 0:00-0:10 - Safety & Flight Feel Recap

- Review pilot-area discipline and pre-flight checks.
- Ask students what felt too sensitive, too slow, or unpredictable in Week 3.
- Explain that this week changes feel in small, documented steps.

### 0:10-0:30 - Baseline Tune or Preset

On the projector:

- Show where presets live in your Betaflight version if you plan to use them.
- Explain:
  - presets are starting points
  - presets are hardware-dependent
  - backups come before major changes

Students:

- Save a backup before any preset or rate work.
- Apply only instructor-approved presets, or keep stock tune if no preset clearly fits.

### 0:30-1:05 - Rates & Expo as Character Controls

On the PID/Rates tab:

- Define:
  - lower rates = calmer maximum rotation
  - higher rates = faster response
  - expo = softer center stick
- Show a mild profile and a more responsive profile.

Students:

1. Record their starting values.
2. Create **Profile 1** as the gentle/practice profile.
3. Create **Profile 2** as the more responsive comparison profile.
4. Save settings and note which profile is active.

### 1:05-1:20 - Simulator Feel Test

- Have students try the same concept in VelociDrone.
- Focus on small movements:
  - hover correction
  - one turn
  - landing
- Ask them to notice center-stick control, gate approach, and exit control, not just speed.

### 1:20-1:30 - Exit Ticket

Students answer:

- Which profile should be safer for practice and why?
- What value or screenshot records my current setup?

---

## Thursday Class - Simulator Time Trials & Comparison Flights

### 0:00-0:15 - Sim Race Brief and Profile Check

Students:

- Use the pre-flight checklist.
- Confirm the profile they will test first.
- State what difference they expect to feel.
- Review the sim challenge rule: clean lap first, time second.

### 0:15-0:45 - VelociDrone Clean-Lap Time Trials

Run a simple simulator challenge:

- one practice lap
- two scored attempts
- clean completion beats faster crash-heavy runs
- students record time, crashes/resets, and which profile they used

Use this as competition, but keep the scoring control-based:

- clean lap
- fewest resets
- smoothest landing
- best improvement

### 0:45-1:10 - Profile Comparison Flights

Run short, controlled comparison flights.

Each test should include:

1. takeoff
2. one hover or short straight move
3. one turn or gate
4. landing
5. quick note before changing profile

Keep the comparison simple enough that students can isolate flight feel from track difficulty.

### 1:10-1:22 - Choose a Practice Profile

Students compare notes:

- Which profile felt steadier?
- Which felt twitchy?
- Which profile produced a cleaner simulator lap?
- Which would be safer on race day right now?
- What change would they make next, if any?

### 1:22-1:30 - Exit Ticket

Students answer:

- My current preferred rate profile is...
- I prefer it because...
- My clean-lap result tells me...
- I should not change everything at once because...

---

## Evidence of Learning

- Student records starting rate values or screenshots.
- Student creates or compares two rate profiles.
- Student completes a simulator clean-lap or time-trial challenge.
- Student explains a profile choice using observed flight behavior.

---

## Notes & Variations

- If students are very new, make rate differences small and conservative.
- If space is tight, compare profiles through hover and one turn rather than full laps.
- If hardware diversity makes profile changes risky, run the comparison in VelociDrone and document concepts without changing real quads.

# Session 5 – Profiles, Backups & Tune Notes

**Theme:** Keep your work legible.  
**Duration:** 90–120 minutes

---

## Goals

By the end of this session, students will:

- Save labeled backups after tuning changes.
- Record which profile they prefer and why.
- Build a simple tune log they can return to later.
- Fly short laps on a documented setup.

---

## Instructor Prep

- Prepare one example of a clearly named backup file and one example of a bad filename.
- Decide how students will store tune screenshots, notes, and backups.

---

## Materials

- Student quads and radios
- Laptops with Betaflight Configurator
- Shared folder or student notebooks
- Indoor flight area

---

## Schedule (example for 2 hours)

### 0:00–0:15 – Documentation Culture

- Explain why beginners lose progress:
  - no backup
  - no file names
  - no notes on what changed
- Show a simple naming convention:
  - `studentname-whoop1-profileA-2026-04-04.json`

### 0:15–0:40 – Save and Label

Students:

1. Connect their quad.
2. Save a new backup with a meaningful file name.
3. Note which rate profile is their current default.
4. Take screenshots of key tabs if that fits your workflow.

### 0:40–1:00 – Build a Tune Log

Students make a short log that includes:

- date
- profile name
- main rate choice
- OSD notes
- what felt better or worse in flight

### 1:00–1:40 – Controlled Laps

- Students fly short laps or repeated patterns on the setup they just documented.
- Focus on consistency, not experimentation.

### 1:40–2:00 – Wrap & Exit Ticket

- Which backup is your current restore point?
- What profile would you put on the line tomorrow?

---

## Notes & Variations

- If you already use a class notebook or LMS, have students store tune notes there.
- If time is short, prioritize backups first and flights second.

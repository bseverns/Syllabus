# Session 6 – Crash Recovery, Batteries & Maintenance

**Theme:** Keeping a whoop alive is part of piloting.  
**Duration:** 90–120 minutes

---

## Goals

By the end of this session, students will:

- Perform a basic crash inspection and know when not to relaunch.
- Identify common tiny-whoop wear points.
- Practice safe battery handling and cooldown habits.
- Separate "quick field fix" from "bench repair needed."

---

## Instructor Prep

- Print or share `materials/Crash_Recovery_Checklist_student.md`.
- Bring a demo quad with a few common post-crash issues if possible:
  - bent prop
  - shifted canopy
  - hair in motor
  - cracked duct

---

## Materials

- Student quads and radios
- Crash-recovery checklists
- Spare props and basic tools if available
- Tweezers and bright inspection light

---

## Schedule (example for 2 hours)

### 0:00–0:15 – Why Tiny Whoops Stop Flying Well

- Brief talk on what crashes actually damage most often.
- Show examples of props, ducts, motors, and battery leads that should not be ignored.

### 0:15–0:40 – Demonstration: Post-Crash Ritual

Demonstrate:

1. Disarm.
2. Approach safely.
3. Inspect props, ducts, frame, canopy, battery lead, and camera angle.
4. Spin each motor gently by hand and listen for rubbing or grit.
5. Decide whether the quad is safe to relaunch.

### 0:40–1:10 – Student Inspection Practice

- Students inspect their own whoops.
- If possible, rotate through one or two "problem" demo quads and decide:
  - fly
  - bench check
  - ground it

### 1:10–1:30 – Battery Habits

- Review:
  - battery damage signs
  - cooling between flights
  - unplugging by the connector, not the wires
  - why puffy packs are a stop sign

### 1:30–1:50 – Maintenance Stations

Possible stations:

- prop inspection and replacement
- motor hair cleanup
- canopy and camera-angle reset
- battery-lead inspection

### 1:50–2:00 – Wrap & Exit Ticket

- One thing I would relaunch after.
- One thing that means I should ground the quad.

---

## Notes & Variations

- If you do not want students changing props yet, keep this as an inspection-only day.
- If your group crashes hard and often, repeat this checklist before every later flight session.

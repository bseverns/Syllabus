# Tiny Whoop Track Roles and Marshal Guide

Use this on practice and race days.

## Pilot

- Stay in the pilot area.
- Know your arming switch before the heat starts.
- Do not launch until the start call.
- Disarm immediately after a crash or on command.

## Spotter or helper

- Watch the room, not just the screen.
- Warn the pilot if someone enters the flight zone.
- Help confirm that the pilot is clear to launch.

## Marshal

- Only enter the course when safe and instructed.
- Wait for props to stop before touching a quad.
- Reset the whoop quickly and leave the track.
- Do not become an obstacle for active pilots.

## Race director or instructor

- Own the start and stop calls.
- Stop the heat if safety gets unclear.
- Ground damaged quads when needed.

## Commands everyone should know

- **Pilots ready**
- **Arm**
- **Go**
- **Stop**
- **Land now**
- **Course clear**

## The classroom version of racing

Fast is good.
Safe, predictable, and fair is better.

# Tiny Whoop Pre–Flight Checklist (Student Version)

Use this as a starting point. Customize it for your own habits and hardware.

---

## 1. Before Plugging In

- [ ] I have permission to fly here today.
- [ ] I know whether this is an indoor or outdoor flight session.
- [ ] The flying area is clear of people, pets, and fragile objects.
- [ ] My radio is **on** and the correct model is selected.
- [ ] My batteries are not damaged or puffy.
- [ ] My props, ducts, canopy, and battery lead passed a quick visual check.

---

## 2. After Plugging In

- [ ] I plug in the battery with the props clear of everything.
- [ ] I hear the normal startup tones.
- [ ] My radio link is working (RSSI or link icon looks normal, or the quad responds in the Receiver tab).
- [ ] My OSD shows battery voltage and a timer.
- [ ] If I am using FPV, my goggles / video receiver are on the correct channel and my video looks normal.

---

## 3. Before Arming

- [ ] I am standing or sitting in the pilot area.
- [ ] I know which switch arms and disarms my quad.
- [ ] The quad is on a flat surface, facing a safe direction.
- [ ] My flight mode is what I expect (for example, ANGLE mode for easier flying).
- [ ] My quad has passed the class flight-readiness checks, including failsafe, or the instructor has cleared this flight.
- [ ] I quickly check that no one has walked into the flying zone.
- [ ] If this is outdoors, I have checked the site-specific rules the instructor gave us.

---

## 4. After Landing

- [ ] I disarm before I walk toward the quad.
- [ ] I unplug the battery by the connector, not by the wires.
- [ ] I let the battery cool before charging again.
- [ ] I glance over the frame, props, and wires for obvious damage.
- [ ] If I crashed, I complete the crash-recovery check before I fly again.
- [ ] I make a quick note (in a notebook or on my phone) if something felt wrong.

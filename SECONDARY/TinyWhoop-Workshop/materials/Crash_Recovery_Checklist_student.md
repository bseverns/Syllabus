# Tiny Whoop Crash Recovery Checklist

Use this after any real hit, tumble, or hard landing.

## 1. Immediate action

- [ ] I disarmed immediately.
- [ ] I waited for props to stop before touching the quad.
- [ ] If needed, I unplugged the battery before handling it.

## 2. Quick visual inspection

- [ ] No prop is chipped, missing, badly bent, or rubbing a duct.
- [ ] The frame and ducts do not show a fresh split or major crack.
- [ ] The canopy and camera are still mounted correctly.
- [ ] The battery lead and connector are secure.
- [ ] The antenna is still attached and routed normally.

## 3. Motor check

- [ ] I checked for hair, thread, tape, or carpet fiber in the motors.
- [ ] I gently spun each motor by hand.
- [ ] No motor felt gritty, stuck, or obviously rough.

## 4. Relaunch decision

- [ ] I believe this quad is safe to relaunch.
- [ ] If I was unsure, I asked the instructor before flying again.

## Ground the quad if any of these are true

- A prop is badly damaged.
- A duct or frame crack is spreading.
- The battery lead is loose or damaged.
- The camera or antenna is hanging loose.
- A motor sounds wrong or feels rough.

## Notes

- What happened in the crash?
- What did I check?
- What did I change before flying again?

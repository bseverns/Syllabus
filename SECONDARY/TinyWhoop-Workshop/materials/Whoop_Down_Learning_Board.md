# When Your Whoop Is Down, You Are Still Learning

Post this as a station board or use it when hardware problems interrupt flight time.

## If You Have No Data Cable

- Borrow a tested data cable if available.
- Label your cable if it is charge-only so it does not waste the next person's time.
- Complete simulator drills while waiting.
- Inspect props, frame, canopy, antenna, and battery lead.
- Fill out the setup sections you can complete without Betaflight.

## If Your Whoop Will Not Bind

- Confirm the radio is on the correct model.
- Write down the receiver type if known.
- Join a working partner for the Receiver tab demo.
- Practice stick axes in VelociDrone.
- Record questions for instructor troubleshooting.

## If Betaflight Shows the Wrong Firmware Target or Unknown Board

- Stop before changing firmware.
- Record what Betaflight reports.
- Ask the instructor to inspect the board and target.
- Work on simulator practice, tune logs, or checklists while waiting.

## If the Frame, Duct, Motor, or Prop Is Damaged

- Complete the crash-recovery checklist.
- Ground the quad if damage affects safety or control.
- Use a repair station if the instructor opens one.
- Observe a working quad and record the diagnosis steps.

## If Video Is Missing

- Check goggles/receiver power.
- Check channel.
- Check antenna and camera position.
- Do not raise VTX power without instructor approval.
- Fly line-of-sight only if the instructor clears it.

## If the Battery Is Dead or Unsafe

- Do not charge or fly damaged batteries.
- Follow the class charging/storage rule.
- Rotate into simulator, marshal, timing, scoring, or documentation work.

## If There Is No Simulator Station Free

- Complete a tune log from your last flight.
- Run the troubleshooting decision tree with a partner.
- Time or score another student's sim run.
- Watch one pilot and write one control habit they used well.
- Prepare your pre-flight checklist for your next turn.

## Useful Roles While Waiting

- Safety checker
- Cable checker
- Tune-log helper
- Simulator timer
- Heat-sheet recorder
- Track marshal in training
- Troubleshooting narrator

The goal is not continuous stick time. The goal is continuous progress.

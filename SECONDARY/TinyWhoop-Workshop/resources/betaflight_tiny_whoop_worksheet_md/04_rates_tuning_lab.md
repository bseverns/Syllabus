## What Are Rates?

Rates control how strongly the drone responds when you move the sticks.

A low-rate drone feels calm and heavy.

A high-rate drone feels quick and sharp.

Expo changes how soft the center of the stick feels.

The goal is not to find the “best” number. The goal is to notice what changed.

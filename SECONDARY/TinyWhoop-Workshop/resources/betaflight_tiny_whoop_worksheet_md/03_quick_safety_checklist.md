# Tiny Whoop Betaflight Safety Checklist

## Before Connecting

☐ Props removed  
☐ Battery disconnected  
☐ Radio on  
☐ Arm switch off  
☐ Correct drone selected / labeled  
☐ Workspace clear  

---

## Before Changing Settings

☐ `diff all` backup saved  
☐ Firmware/configurator version recorded  
☐ Drone name/number recorded  
☐ Teacher confirms correct model/board  

---

## Before Receiver Testing

☐ Props off  
☐ Radio bound  
☐ Sticks centered except throttle  
☐ Arm switch verified off  

---

## Before Motors Tab

☐ Props off again  
☐ Everyone says “props off” out loud  
☐ Battery connected only when instructed  
☐ Motor sliders moved slowly  
☐ Motor order checked  
☐ Motor direction checked  
☐ Battery disconnected after test  

---

## Before First Flight

☐ Props installed correctly  
☐ Battery secured  
☐ OSD battery voltage visible  
☐ Flight timer visible  
☐ ARM switch known  
☐ Disarm reflex practiced  
☐ Failsafe checked  
☐ Flight area clear  
☐ Pilot knows landing rule  

---

## Landing Rule

Land when:

- timer reaches class limit, or
- low-voltage warning appears, or
- teacher calls land, or
- the pilot feels out of control

There is no shame in landing. Landing is how pilots stay in the story.

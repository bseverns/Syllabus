# VelociDrone Setup and Beginner Drills

Use this to make simulator time a core practice space for the workshop, not an add-on.

## Why use VelociDrone here?

VelociDrone gives students repetitions they cannot safely or cheaply get from real whoops alone.

It is especially useful for:

- launch and landing rhythm
- throttle control
- smooth turning
- orientation recovery
- early race-line practice
- low-risk competition before real drones are ready to race

## Recommended use in this workshop

- **Week 1:** first flight-control hook; students feel throttle, turning, crashes, resets, and improvement.
- **Week 2:** control drills tied to radio mapping, stick axes, and arming habits.
- **Week 3:** race-line drills before first real-flight work.
- **Week 4:** clean-lap challenge or simple time trial tied to rate/profile choices.
- **Week 5:** short simulator heats, consistency challenge, or best-improvement race.
- **Weeks 7 and 9:** short warm-ups before real-flight track work.
- **Any day with limited working quads:** run stations and keep a simulator lane active

## Basic setup

- Connect the student's radio as a USB controller if supported.
- Calibrate sticks before class starts.
- Keep rates in the simulator beginner-friendly at first.
- Start with a simple environment, not a cluttered map.

## Beginner drill ladder

### Drill 1: Arm, lift, hover, land

Goal:

- controlled takeoff
- stable hover
- clean landing

### Drill 2: Forward move and stop

Goal:

- move through space without panic
- stop without bouncing into the floor

### Drill 3: Left and right turns

Goal:

- steady yaw and roll coordination
- no sudden overcorrection

### Drill 4: Figure-eight

Goal:

- repeatable turns
- smooth transitions

### Drill 5: One clean gate line

Goal:

- line choice
- consistent throttle
- finishing the line under control

### Drill 6: One clean lap

Goal:

- race rhythm without racing chaos

### Drill 7: Clean-lap time trial

Goal:

- one complete lap
- fewer resets before faster time
- profile choice based on evidence

### Drill 8: Low-crash heat race

Goal:

- race with other students while keeping control
- reset calmly after mistakes
- treat sportsmanship and clean flying as part of the score

## Instructor tip

Do not let students use VelociDrone as an excuse to skip real checklist habits.

Carry the same discipline over:

- arm on purpose
- fly one plan
- land on purpose
- reflect on what changed
- race clean before racing fast

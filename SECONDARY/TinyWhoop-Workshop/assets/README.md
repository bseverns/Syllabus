# Original course visuals

These are original, editable SVG teaching diagrams created for this curriculum repository.

| Asset | Use | Alt text | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `flight-systems-map.svg` | Orient students to the distinct power, control, and video systems before configuration or flight. | Three color-coded tiny-whoop systems: power from 1S battery to board to motors, control from pilot radio through ELRS to the board, and video from camera through analog VTX to goggles/receiver. | Concept map only; it is not a wiring diagram, pinout, configuration guide, or substitute for instructor safety clearance. |

Each SVG has a `<title>`, `<desc>`, and `role="img"`. The course's MIT license governs reuse unless a downstream use requires a more specific notice.

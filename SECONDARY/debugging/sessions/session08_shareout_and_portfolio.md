# Session 8 — Share-Out and Debugging Portfolio

## At a glance

- length: 60 to 90 minutes
- focus: communicate debugging as a practice
- artifact: final portfolio or presentation

## In-class plan

1. Students organize their portfolio:
   - debugging log
   - bug report
   - one revision example
   - one reflection on goals or emotion
   - final case study
2. Use `handouts/portfolio_checklist.md` to confirm required pieces.
3. Students present a short debugging story:
   - what happened
   - what they tried
   - what they learned
4. Class closes by identifying what strong debugging culture looks like.

## Instructor notes

- reward honesty and evidence
- do not make the share-out depend on having fixed everything

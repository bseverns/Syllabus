# Session 5 — Refactoring and Revision

## At a glance

- length: 60 to 90 minutes
- focus: when and why to clean up code or process
- artifact: before/after comparison

## In-class plan

1. Show two ways to do the same task:
   - a longer but understandable version
   - a shorter or more elegant version
2. Ask:
   - which one is better right now?
   - better for whom?
3. Students revise a small piece of code or process documentation.
4. Reflect on what improved and what became harder.

## Suggested handout

- `handouts/refactoring_compare.md`

## Instructor notes

- avoid teaching refactoring as automatically superior
- connect to the workshop source idea that efficiency can become a narrow norm

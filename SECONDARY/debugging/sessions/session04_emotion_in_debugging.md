# Session 4 — Emotion in Debugging

## At a glance

- length: 60 to 90 minutes
- focus: emotions shape debugging decisions
- artifact: emotion check-in + timeline

## Before class

- print `handouts/emotion_checkin.md`
- prepare a short debugging clip or scenario where frustration is visible

## In-class plan

1. Ask students what emotions show up when they are stuck.
2. Introduce the idea that emotion changes what risks people take.
3. Students complete a quick emotion check-in during a live debugging task.
4. Debrief:
   - what made the task feel harder?
   - what support actually helped?

## Instructor notes

- keep this grounded in classroom practice, not therapy language
- the goal is not to remove emotion, but to notice its effect

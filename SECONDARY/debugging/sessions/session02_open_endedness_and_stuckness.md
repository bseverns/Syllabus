# Session 2 — Open-Ended Problems and Productive Stuckness

## At a glance

- length: 60 to 90 minutes
- focus: ambiguity, open-ended work, and how stuckness can still be useful
- artifact: stuckness map

## Before class

- prepare an intentionally messy or incomplete problem
- print `handouts/stuckness_map.md`

## In-class plan

1. Ask students to compare:
   - a bug with one likely fix
   - a problem with several possible directions
2. Introduce the idea that some stuck moments are about ambiguity, not simple error.
3. Students map where they got stuck:
   - understanding the goal
   - tracing the system
   - choosing a next move
4. Share strategies that helped without pretending the problem was simple.

## Instructor notes

- validate uncertainty without romanticizing confusion
- keep the focus on process language

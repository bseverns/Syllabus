# Session 7 — Debugging a Real System

## At a glance

- length: 60 to 90 minutes
- focus: apply the full process to a larger project or shared case study
- artifact: case study draft

## In-class plan

1. Students choose a real bug, failure, or confusing behavior from their own work or a shared class example.
2. They document:
   - the problem
   - the evidence
   - tests run
   - what changed
   - what remained unresolved
3. Students begin `handouts/case_study_template.md`.

## Instructor notes

- unresolved problems are acceptable if the process is strong
- the goal is honest analysis, not heroic success stories

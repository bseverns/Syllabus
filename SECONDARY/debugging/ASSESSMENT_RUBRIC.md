# Assessment Rubric

## 1. Problem description

Exceeds:

- clearly describes what happened, what was expected, and what evidence is available

Meets:

- identifies the bug or stuck point in understandable terms

Below:

- describes the problem vaguely or only as "it doesn't work"

## 2. Debugging process

Exceeds:

- tests deliberately, changes one thing at a time, and records results

Meets:

- attempts multiple steps and can explain some of the reasoning

Below:

- changes many things at once or cannot explain what was tried

## 3. Reflection on goals and tradeoffs

Exceeds:

- compares more than one debugging goal and explains the tradeoff clearly

Meets:

- identifies at least one relevant goal beyond "make it work"

Below:

- treats debugging as only speed or only correctness

## 4. Collaboration and communication

Exceeds:

- gives useful peer support and writes clear bug reports or coaching notes

Meets:

- participates respectfully and communicates next steps clearly

Below:

- peer support is unclear, overly controlling, or too vague to help

## 5. Revision and portfolio

Exceeds:

- final portfolio shows evidence, iteration, and honest reflection on process

Meets:

- portfolio includes required artifacts and a readable explanation

Below:

- final submission focuses only on the final fix and leaves out process

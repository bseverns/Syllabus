# Peer Coaching Moves

Use these sentence starters when helping someone debug:

- "Show me where it starts."
- "What did you expect to happen?"
- "What changed right before this?"
- "What do you know for sure?"
- "What is one small thing we can test?"
- "Do you want help fixing it, or help understanding it?"

Avoid:

- grabbing the keyboard immediately
- changing many things at once
- saying "just do this" without explanation

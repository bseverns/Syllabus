# Start Here: Robotics → FPV Lab and Flight Card

Use this with your team at every session. The instructor decides when a build may receive power, props, or flight clearance.

## 1. Start every work block

1. Check your team station, the posted channel plan, and today's drill.
2. Pick a role: pilot, observer, technician, or logger. Rotate roles when the instructor says to.
3. Open the matching course surface:
   - simulator: [`sim/Setup.md`](sim/Setup.md) and [`sim/Drill-Cards.md`](sim/Drill-Cards.md)
   - build/bench: [`assessments/Bench-Check-Checklist.md`](assessments/Bench-Check-Checklist.md)
   - flight: [`assessments/Flight-Checkride-Checklist.md`](assessments/Flight-Checkride-Checklist.md)
4. Record the date, minutes, drill, crashes/repairs, and one note in the [`Pilot Logbook`](assessments/Pilot-Logbook.csv).

## 2. Simulator first

- Use only the controller and simulator station your instructor has tested.
- Calibrate before the first drill. Start with throttle ladders, then hover box, figure-8, and gate line.
- If controls are wrong or the simulator will not connect, stop and ask for the station to be reset; do not change another team's rates, arm switch, or controller mapping.

## 3. Bench and battery boundary

- Read the Bench-Check Checklist with your partner before a power test.
- First power happens **with no props** and only at the instructor-approved bench.
- Use the posted VTX channel plan; indoor VTX power stays at **25 mW**.
- Charging is supervised. Do not start a charge, move a battery, or reconnect a damaged craft without the instructor.

## 4. Flight boundary

- Fly only when the instructor calls the flight zone ready.
- FPV flights require a spotter. Follow the posted channel plan.
- Only one quad is in the cage unless the instructor has designated multi-pilot time.
- Keep prop guards/ducts on classroom craft.

## 5. If a craft crashes

1. Say “crash” and wait for the flight area to be made safe.
2. Do not re-power or re-arm the craft.
3. Follow the [`Crash Recovery Checklist`](checklists/Crash-Recovery-Checklist.md), then log the repair.

# Start Here: Simulator Drill Cards (Print or Project)

Before you start: use the instructor-tested station, calibrate the sticks, and write the date in your Pilot Logbook. Keep physical quads powered down during simulator practice.

**D‑01 Throttle Ladders (5 min)**
- Do: lift to 1 m; hold; drop to 0.5 m; hold; repeat smoothly.
- Log: the number of smooth repeats.

**D‑02 Hover Box (5 min)**
- Do: stay inside a 2×2 m virtual box with minimal yaw.
- Log: your longest controlled hover.

**D‑03 Figure‑8 (10 min)**
- Do: fly around two cones/gates; keep the horizon level.
- Log: one control change that made the path smoother.

**D‑04 Gate Line (10 min)**
- Do: fly three gates in a line; prioritize consistency over speed.
- Log: your cleanest run and one retry note.

**D‑05 Horizon→Acro Intro (optional)**
- Do: try short acro bursts; recover to stable horizon flight.
- Log: whether you recovered without a crash.

If the controller mapping, rates, or simulator connection is wrong, stop and ask for the station to be reset. Do not change another team's configuration.

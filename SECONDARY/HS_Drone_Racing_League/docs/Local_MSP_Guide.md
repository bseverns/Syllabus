# Minneapolis–Saint Paul (MSP) Operations Guide — 2025-11-03

**Home base:** PCYC / PYC Arts & Technology (North Minneapolis) — 2210 Oliver Ave N, Minneapolis, MN 55411. Confirm gym availability for **indoor Tiny‑Whoop** practice and secure storage for LiPos and gates.

## Permissions & permits (outdoors)
- **Minneapolis Park & Recreation Board (MPRB)** requires permits for drone operations on park property. File permitting requests 10+ days in advance.
- For filming/photography in parks, use the MPRB filming permit channel. Keep documentation on‑site.
- When not on park property (e.g., school grounds/private venues), obtain written site permission; follow a **CBO safety code** and FAA recreational rules.

## Remote ID & FRIA
- Use **sub‑250 g** craft or **RID‑equipped** aircraft when required outdoors. Indoors (gyms) is **not** in the NAS.
- Check the **FAA FRIA map** to locate nearby fields where non‑RID aircraft may fly. Many AMA club sites in MSP now have FRIA status.

## Track types around MSP
- **Micro/Tiny‑Whoop (indoors):** PYC gym; community centers; school cafeterias and hallways (after hours).
- **Outdoor 3–5″:** FRIA club fields or permitted open athletic fields when allowed.

# Course Setup, Frequencies, and Video Hygiene

**Gates/flags (typical):** Championship gates ≤ 7′ W × 6′ H; flags ≥ 8′ tall (CDRA guide). See: https://www.multigp.com/cdra/rules/ and https://www.multigp.com/cdra/track/

**Frequency management (5.8 GHz):**
- Use legal U.S. ranges (avoid 5645/5925/5945 MHz). Reference: https://www.multigp.com/legal-frequencies-in-drone-racing/
- Prefer **Raceband** groupings and **25 mW** for indoor/multi‑pilot.
- Use **pit mode** on bench and after crashes.
- Pre‑load VTX tables; label quads with pilot name and channel.

**Helpful charts/tools:**
- Channel tables and overlap discussion: https://oscarliang.com/fpv-channels/
- Interactive 5.8 GHz channel tool: https://www.mrd-rc.com/.../interactive-5-8ghz-channel-chart/

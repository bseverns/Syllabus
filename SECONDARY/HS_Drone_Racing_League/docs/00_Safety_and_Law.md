# Safety & Law (U.S.) — Coach Quickstart

**Non‑negotiables for student pilots and coaches:**
- Fly **only** where you’re allowed and safe. Indoors is *not* in the NAS; outdoors follow FAA + CBO rules.
- Use a **Community‑Based Organization (CBO)** safety code (e.g., AMA, FPV Freedom Coalition, Flite Test Community Association).
- Every recreational pilot should complete the **TRUST** (free) and carry proof.
- Know **Remote ID** and **FRIA** basics (school can apply for a FRIA; otherwise use RID‑equipped drones/modules where required).
- Keep **visual line of sight** with a **co‑located visual observer** for FPV flights.
- Treat **LiPo batteries** like laboratory equipment. Use bags, correct charge rates, and supervision.

## Core FAA links
- Recreational Flyers rules overview (Section 44809): https://www.faa.gov/uas/recreational_flyers
- TRUST test (free): https://trust.pilotinstitute.com/ or https://trust.modelaircraft.org/
- Remote ID overview: https://www.faa.gov/uas/getting_started/remote_id
- FRIA overview & eligibility (schools/CBOs can apply): https://www.faa.gov/uas/getting_started/remote_id/fria  (AC 89‑3: https://www.faa.gov/documentLibrary/media/Advisory_Circular/AC_89-3.pdf)
- FAA‑recognized CBO list: https://www.faa.gov/uas/recreationalfliers/faa-recognized-community-based-organizations

**Key notes:**
- **Indoors**: Remote ID does *not* apply to operations exclusively inside buildings.
- **Sub‑250 g**: If the aircraft **doesn’t need registration** (≤ 0.55 lb/250 g, used recreationally), **Remote ID is not required**.
- **FRIA**: Once approved, any pilot with physical access may fly in the FRIA *without* Remote ID.

## CBO safety codes (choose one and stick to it for consistency)
- AMA National Safety Code: https://www.modelaircraft.org/safety
  - AMA FPV supplemental: https://www.modelaircraft.org/sites/default/files/files/550.pdf
- FPV Freedom Coalition guidelines: https://fpvfc.org/safety-guidelines
- Flite Test Community Association (FTCA): https://www.ftca.com/

## Airfield & insurance pragmatics
- Consider AMA youth memberships and club/site insurance when applicable: https://www.modelaircraft.org/membership/programs-benefits
- If racing at public venues, coordinate with facilities, athletic directors, and district risk managers. Keep printed **Emergency Action Plans** on‑site.

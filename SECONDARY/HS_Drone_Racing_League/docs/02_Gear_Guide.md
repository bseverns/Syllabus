# Gear Guide (Coach Cheatsheet)

**Start here (indoors):**
- 65–75 mm **Tiny‑Whoop** class quads (ducted) for gym practice; 1S/2S packs; prop guards; durable frames.
- Radio: Multiprotocol/ELRS radios running **EdgeTX**; simulator‑compatible via USB.
- Goggles: Any safe option; digital (HDZero/DJI) or analog. For racing latency/timing, **analog or HDZero** often integrates best with timing systems.

**Outdoor step‑up:**
- 3–5″ quads on 3–6S with Betaflight; durable race frames; props in several pitches.
- VTX with **pit mode** + SmartAudio/Tramp control. Carry **VTX tables** pre‑loaded and labeled.
- Spares: Props (lots), motors (x1 per arm), camera mount, battery straps, antennas, XT60 pigtails, gummies, screws, zip ties, smoke stopper.

**Support gear:**
- Smart chargers with balance boards; parallel charge with caution; LiPo bags; fire‑safe surface.
- Field tools: Hex drivers, nut drivers, soldering iron, flux, leaded solder, isopropyl, tweezers, heat‑shrink.
- Timing: LiveTime + LapRF 8‑way (or **RotorHazard** for open‑source) + start/finish gate.

**Reference docs:**
- Betaflight Quickstart & Modes: https://betaflight.com/docs/development/Modes
- VTX tables & control: https://oscarliang.com/smartaudio-tramp-vtx-control-vtxtables/
- Frequency/band planning & legality: https://www.multigp.com/legal-frequencies-in-drone-racing/

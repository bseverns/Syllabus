# Battery Safety (LiPo/LI‑ion) — Student‑Facing

**Rules that keep us flying:**
- Charge **in LiPo bags**, attended, on fire‑safe surface, correct cell count and **≤1C** unless specified.
- **Never** charge puffed/damaged packs. Storage at **3.75–3.85 V/cell**.
- Land by **3.5–3.7 V/cell under load** for longevity.

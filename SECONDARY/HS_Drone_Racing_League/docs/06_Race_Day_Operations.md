# Race‑Day Operations (Coach Playbook)

**Formats:** seeding heats → qualifying (2‑3 rounds) → double‑elimination or ladder mains. Target 4–6 packs per pilot at practice meets.

**Stations & roles:**
- **Start/Finish**: timing gate + marshal
- **Pits**: soldering, battery tables, goggles charging
- **Queue**: next heat staging + VTX channel board
- **Race Control**: laptop (Windows) with LiveTime or RotorHazard

**Checklist:**
- Print heat sheets; verify pilot channels; announce “Arm your quads… here we go!”
- **Spotters** watch airspace; call “land” for manned aircraft overhead (outdoors).
- Post results; rotate heats; run mains.

- LiveTime downloads: https://www.livetimescoring.com/download/
- LiveTime training: https://www.livetimescoring.com/training/

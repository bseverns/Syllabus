# Summer Extension (Repeatable Weekly Cycle)

**Mon/Tue:** Sim (30–60 min): time trials + targeted drill  
**Wed/Thu:** Field practice (2 packs skills + 2 packs hot laps)  
**Fri:** Open practice / repair lab / media and community outreach  
**Monthly:** Host/attend meets; rotate venues; collect full race data

**Coach KPI:** attendance, packs per pilot, % heats finished, repair turnaround time, and PB deltas.

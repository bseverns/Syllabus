# Course Outline — Design for Printability & Function (10 weeks)

Each session uses an **A/B block**:
- **Block A (35–45 min):** Mini-lecture, live CAD demo, printability focus.
- **Block B (35–45 min):** Studio build/test, measurement, iteration, logging.

## Week-by-week
1. **Measurement & Constraints** — Units, calipers, datums; make a "calibration coupon".
2. **Sketching Rules** — Constraints, dimensions, extrudes; make a simple bracket.
3. **Printability I: Overhangs** — Angle limits, chamfers vs. supports; print overhang test.
4. **Printability II: Bridging** — Cooling & span; print bridge ladder; compare failures.
5. **Slicer Parameters → Strength** — Shells, infill, layer height; tensile/bend coupons.
6. **Tolerances & Fits** — Clearance, press/interference; print gauge; record best fit.
7. **Snap-fits & Living Hinges** — Cantilever hooks; min thickness; test cycles-to-failure.
8. **Assemblies & Orientation** — Keys, bosses, dog-bones; orient for strength vs. finish.
9. **Capstone Design Review** — Locking container requirements, DFM, test plan.
10. **Capstone Test & Iterate** — Drop/shake tests, revisions, documentation & reflection.

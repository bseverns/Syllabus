# Rubric — Capstone: Locking Container (100 pts)

- **Design for Printability (25):** Appropriate orientation, minimal supports, chamfers/fillets used intelligently.
- **Tolerance & Fit (25):** Mates assemble smoothly; documented clearance choice and evidence from gauge tests.
- **Strength & Durability (20):** Survives 1 m drop test closed; hinge or snap shows no critical fracture.
- **Documentation & Data (20):** Test logs complete with settings, photos, and iteration notes; readable CAD exports.
- **Process & Teamwork (10):** Equitable roles; timely design review and response to feedback.

# Privacy & Ethics

- **Data light**: Avoid collecting personal data. If a platform logs data (e.g., accounts), prefer pseudonyms or group logins.
- **Consent**: Obtain explicit consent for photos, audio/video, and publishing student work.
- **Attribution**: Credit open‑source projects, libraries, and community resources.
- **Safety**: Document safety steps, disclaimers, and supervision needs near any tool use.
- **Accessibility**: Offer alternative activities and materials where needed.

# Start Here: Your Lo-Fi Beat Project

## Make this

Make a **30–60 second track** from sounds you find or make. Your track needs a beginning, one change in the middle, and an ending.

## Before you record

- Record sounds, not people talking.
- Use only the places your teacher says are okay.
- Do not record in restrooms, locker rooms, private offices, or anywhere people expect privacy.
- Keep clips short: 1–5 seconds is enough.
- If you cannot record a sound, use a class clip or make a tabletop sound instead.

## Your course checklist

- [ ] **Session 1:** Collect 3–5 short sounds. Include one indoor and one outdoor sound if that is safe and possible.
- [ ] **Session 2:** Turn one sound into a 4–8 bar loop.
- [ ] **Session 3:** Cut one longer sound into at least four pieces and rearrange them.
- [ ] **Session 4:** Make a beat with a low, middle, and high sound.
- [ ] **Session 5:** Compare your normal loop with a version recorded in an approved space. Write one thing that changed.
- [ ] **Session 6:** Arrange a 30–60 second draft: start → middle change → ending.
- [ ] **Session 7:** Make sure the important sounds can be heard. Add a title and simple cover art.
- [ ] **Session 8:** Share your piece and write your reflection.

## Save your work

Use the file name your teacher gives you. Save after every work block. Keep the project file even if you export an audio file.

## If you are stuck

1. Pick one sound you like.
2. Repeat it four times.
3. Add one second sound halfway through.
4. Make one thing quieter or louder.
5. Save it and ask a partner what they notice.

## Finish with these three sentences

1. One sound I used was `____________________________`.
2. After listening, I changed `____________________________`.
3. The room changed my sound by `____________________________`.

# Lo-Fi Beats & Broken Rooms (High School)

## Course snapshot

- **Duration:** 8 × 90-minute sessions  
- **Audience:** Grades 9–12, mixed tech comfort  
- **Prereqs:** None (basic computer comfort helps)  
- **Core tools:**
  - Computers with browser (Chrome/Firefox)
  - Headphones for each student
  - Simple DAW / sampler (BandLab, Soundtrap, GarageBand, etc.)
  - Built-in webcam + mic or simple USB mic
  - Optional: instructor-approved hallway, stairwell, or gym for “room re-recording”; never restrooms, private offices, or unconsented people

## Big idea

We treat **everyday sound** and **imperfect spaces** as musical instruments.

Students:

- Record and manipulate sounds from their own lives  
- Chop and loop recordings into short beats  
- Use **physical rooms** as “effect units” (capturing reverb, echo, noise)  
- Share a short “broken room” piece at the end of the course

## Learning goals

By the end of the workshop, students should be able to:

- Capture short audio recordings using simple tools  
- Edit and loop sounds in a DAW or online sampler  
- Build a basic beat or soundscape from non-traditional sources  
- Describe how a room’s acoustics shape what we hear  
- Reflect on mood, texture, and “imperfection” in music

## Session list

1. **Lo-Fi & Listening to Rooms** – What is lo-fi? What is room tone?  
2. **Found Sounds & First Loops** – Import, clean up, and loop everyday audio.  
3. **Chopping & Slicing** – Turn one recording into many usable fragments.  
4. **Beats from Noise** – Rhythm and groove without traditional drum samples.  
5. **Broken Rooms: Space as Effect** – Re-record loops in real spaces.  
6. **Arranging a Mini-Track** – Turn loops into a 30–60s piece.  
7. **Mix, Title, Artwork** – Basic mix, naming, and visual identity.  
8. **Listening Party & Reflection** – Sharing and reflecting on listening habits.

# Session 05 – Broken Rooms: Space as Effect

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Field recording + lab  
- **Goals:**
  - Use physical rooms as natural “effect units.”
  - Hear differences between dry and re-recorded audio.
- **Deliverables:**
  - One loop re-recorded in a live space.

## Before class (instructor)

- Clear any permissions needed to record in hallways/stairwells/etc.
- Identify 2–4 spaces with noticeably different acoustics.

## In-class plan

### 0–15 min — Setup & safety

- Explain today’s plan and boundaries:
  - Which areas are okay to record in.
  - How long groups have outside the classroom.
  - Never record in restrooms, locker rooms, private offices, or spaces with people who have not agreed.
- Assign groups (2–4 students each).

### 15–40 min — Room re-recording field trip

- In groups, students:
  - Play a loop from a phone/laptop speaker.
  - Re-record it using another device (phone/recorder).
  - Try at least one space per group.
- Instructor circulates to supervise.

### 40–65 min — Import & compare

- Back in the lab, students:
  - Import “dry” and “roomed” versions.
  - Listen A/B and describe:
    - Reverb/echo
    - Noise (HVAC, voices)
- Optionally, show waveforms or spectrograms.

### 65–85 min — Integrate roomed audio

- Students:
  - Replace or layer their dry loop with the roomed version.
  - Adjust volume so added noise feels intentional.

### 85–90 min — Exit ticket

- “Which room changed your sound the most? How?”

## After class / Homework

- Optional: record one extra roomed version at home (bathroom, hallway, etc.).

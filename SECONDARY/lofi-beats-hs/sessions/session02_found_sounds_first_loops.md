# Session 02 – Found Sounds & First Loops

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Lab  
- **Goals:**
  - Import students’ recordings into a DAW or online tool.
  - Trim, normalize, and loop simple sounds.
  - Create a first 4–8 bar loop from found audio.
- **Deliverables:**
  - One saved loop per student.

## Before class (instructor)

- Confirm you can access students’ audio files.
- Test importing a sample into the chosen DAW / platform.
- Prepare a demo project you can screen-share.

## In-class plan

### 0–15 min — Check-in & file wrangling

- Quick check:
  - Who recorded sounds? Any tech roadblocks?
- Help students get files onto lab machines or into the DAW.

### 15–35 min — Demo: From Raw to Loop

- Show:
  - Importing a clip
  - Trimming silence
  - Adjusting gain/normalize
  - Looping a 1–2 bar section to the grid
- Keep vocabulary light; emphasize listening.

### 35–70 min — Student work time

- Students:
  - Import at least 2–3 of their recordings.
  - Find a segment in one recording that loops well.
  - Build a **simple loop** (4–8 bars).
- Instructor circulates:
  - Help with DAW basics.
  - Encourage experimentation: “What if you loop the ‘wrong’ part?”

### 70–85 min — Share a few loops

- Invite 3–5 volunteers to play their loop.
- Quick responses from peers:
  - “What does it sound like it’s made from?”
  - “What mood does it give?”

### 85–90 min — Exit ticket

- “Name your loop” (title only) in LMS / on a slip of paper.

## After class / Homework

- **Students:** Optional – tweak loop title or make a second version.  
- **Instructor:** Collect a few standout examples for next class reference.

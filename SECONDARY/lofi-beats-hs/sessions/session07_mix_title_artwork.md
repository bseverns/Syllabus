# Session 07 – Mix, Title, and Artwork

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Lab + mini-crit  
- **Goals:**
  - Introduce very basic mixing concepts.
  - Give each piece a title and visual identity.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Named track with rough mix.
  - Simple cover art.

## Before class (instructor)

- Decide what mixing tools to allow (volume, pan, EQ, reverb).
- Prepare examples of simple cover art (collage, abstract shapes, etc.).

## In-class plan

### 0–25 min — Mix mini-lesson

- Show:
  - Volume leveling (avoid clipping).
  - Simple panning to separate sounds.
  - Optional: one plugin (EQ or reverb).
- Stress “good enough to hear everything” rather than perfection.

### 25–60 min — Individual mix sessions

- Students:
  - Adjust levels so key elements are clear.
  - Pan selected tracks.
  - Check loudness with headphones and speakers if possible.

### 60–80 min — Title & cover art

- Students:
  - Choose a title that fits mood or story.
  - Create a simple cover image:
    - Draw on paper and photograph, OR
    - Use a simple digital collage / shapes.

### 80–90 min — Quick share

- A few students:
  - Show artwork
  - Play 15–20 seconds of their piece.

## After class / Homework

- Final tweaks to mix and artwork as needed.

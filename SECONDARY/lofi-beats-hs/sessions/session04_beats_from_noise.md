# Session 04 – Beats from Noise

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Lab + short listening  
- **Goals:**
  - Use non-drum sounds as drum voices.
  - Explore rhythm, groove, and pattern from everyday audio.
- **Deliverables:**
  - One short “noise beat” loop.

## Before class (instructor)

- Prepare 1–2 examples of beats made from unconventional sounds.
- Make sure DAW grid/metronome is ready.

## In-class plan

### 0–10 min — Rhythm warm-up

- Clap simple patterns together:
  - Quarter notes, eighth notes, simple syncopation.
- Emphasize feel over perfection.

### 10–30 min — Listening: Beats Without Drums

- Play an example where the “drums” are found sounds.
- Ask:
  - “What do you think the kick/snare/hi-hat are made from?”

### 30–70 min — Build a Noise Beat

- Students:
  - Choose 3–4 slices to act as:
    - Low sound (kick role)
    - Mid sound (snare role)
    - High sound (hi-hat role)
  - Program a **1–2 bar pattern** using only those slices.
- Encourage:
  - Copy/paste to build a 4–8 bar loop.
  - Try one variation per 2 bars.

### 70–85 min — Quick share

- A few students play their noise beats.
- Peers name what they *think* the sounds are vs. what they really are.

### 85–90 min — Exit ticket

- “My favorite ‘fake drum’ sound is…”

## After class / Homework

- Students keep working on their favorite beat if time runs short.

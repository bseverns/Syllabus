# Session 06 – Arranging a Mini-Track

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Lab  
- **Goals:**
  - Introduce basic song form (intro / main / variation / ending).
  - Arrange loops into a 30–60 second piece.
- **Deliverables:**
  - First draft of a short track.

## Before class (instructor)

- Prepare a simple example track that:
  - Starts with one loop
  - Adds layers
  - Ends with something different (drop, filter, mute)

## In-class plan

### 0–15 min — Form mini-lesson

- On the board:
  - Intro → A → A’ → Outro (or similar)
- Listen to a short piece and mark where each section changes.

### 15–70 min — Student arranging lab

- Students:
  - Choose:
    - At least one “dry” loop
    - At least one “roomed” loop
    - At least one “noise beat”
  - Arrange them into a 30–60 second piece:
    - Decide what appears first.
    - Plan at least one change halfway through.

### 70–85 min — Pair sharing

- In pairs:
  - Play each other’s draft.
  - Peers answer:
    - “What did you hear changing?”
    - “Where did you want more or less?”

### 85–90 min — Exit ticket

- “One change I’ll make before the final is…”

## After class / Homework

- Students refine structure based on feedback.

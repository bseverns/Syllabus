## AI at Work: Tools, Judgment, and Professional Practice

### What it is
A 6-week workforce-readiness course that teaches high school students how to use AI tools in professional settings with skill, caution, and confidence.

### Why it matters
Students are entering a world where AI is already present in offices, customer service, communication, administration, and research workflows. Many young people can access these tools, but far fewer know how to use them responsibly, verify outputs, protect privacy, and present polished work.

### What students learn
- how AI tools work at a practical level
- how to prompt for better results
- how to verify and revise outputs
- how to protect privacy and recognize risk
- how to complete workplace-style tasks
- how to build a personal toolkit for future work

### What students leave with
- prompt notebook
- verification checklist
- template pack
- personal AI use policy
- professional folder system
- capstone workflow demonstration

### Why this fits a workforce partner
The course translates AI from hype into habit. It prepares students not just to use tools, but to communicate clearly, work carefully, and build repeatable processes they can carry into internships and early career settings.

# Exit Ticket Prompts

Use one or two at the end of each session.

## Session 1
- Name one place where AI showed up in your day.
- What is one thing you should never upload to an AI tool?
- What surprised you about today’s outputs?

## Session 2
- What did you revise, and why?
- What part of your project feels most like yours?
- What did the AI keep doing that you had to push back on?

## Session 3
- What is one sign that an AI output might be biased or inaccurate?
- What did you verify today?
- What kind of project would need fact-checking before sharing?

## Session 4
- What is one privacy rule you are taking with you?
- What did AI help with in your final project?
- What advice would you give the next group?

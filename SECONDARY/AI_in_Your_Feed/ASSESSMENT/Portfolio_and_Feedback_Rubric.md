# Portfolio and Feedback Rubric

Use this as a feedback tool, not a punishment tool.

## Category 1: Creative Direction
**Look for:**
- a clear idea
- a visible point of view
- evidence of revision
- decisions that feel intentional

**Questions to ask:**
- What was the student trying to make?
- What choices made it feel specific?
- Where did they move past the first generic answer?

## Category 2: Critical Awareness
**Look for:**
- ability to name bias, error, or flattening
- willingness to question outputs
- evidence of verification where needed
- understanding that AI outputs are not automatically true

**Questions to ask:**
- Did the student notice problems in the output?
- Did they explain what felt inaccurate or unfair?
- Did they revise based on that insight?

## Category 3: Privacy and Safety
**Look for:**
- safe tool habits
- no oversharing
- consent-aware decisions
- thoughtful sharing choices

**Questions to ask:**
- Did the student protect personal information?
- Did they avoid uploading sensitive or non-consensual material?
- Can they explain one or two safe-use practices?

## Category 4: Process and Reflection
**Look for:**
- prompt logs, notes, or screenshots
- reflection on what AI helped with
- reflection on what AI failed at
- evidence that the student remained the author

**Questions to ask:**
- Can the student describe their process?
- Can they say what they kept, rejected, or changed?
- Do they understand the difference between tool output and their own intent?

## Simple performance bands

### Emerging
The student is beginning to experiment and can identify at least one strength or risk in the tool.

### Developing
The student is revising work, noticing inaccuracies, and showing basic privacy awareness.

### Strong
The student is making intentional creative choices, checking outputs critically, and using tools with care.

### Distinctive
The student demonstrates authorship, insight, careful revision, and a strong understanding of both creative possibility and ethical risk.

## Suggested final reflection prompts

- What did AI help you do faster or differently?
- What did it get wrong?
- What did you have to fix?
- What would you never upload to an AI tool now?
- What advice would you give the next group?

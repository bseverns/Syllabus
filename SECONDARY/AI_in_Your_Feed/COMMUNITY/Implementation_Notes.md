# Implementation Notes

## Best use case
- 8 to 14 students
- 1 lead facilitator
- 1 assistant if possible
- weekly 90-minute sessions
- computer lab, classroom, library, or youth studio

## Good fit for North Minneapolis settings
This course works especially well in:
- after-school spaces
- youth media labs
- libraries
- nonprofit programs
- summer intensives
- school partnership sites

## Strong implementation choices
- use school-managed accounts where possible
- keep a stable, small tool stack
- print the privacy and bias handouts
- give students a prompt log or process sheet
- end every session with a share-out

## Avoid
- requiring personal logins when avoidable
- making students upload personal photos
- turning the course into pure lecture
- treating tool output as truth
- overloading students with too many platforms

## Recommended culminating moment
A low-pressure showcase where students present:
- what they made
- what AI helped with
- what AI got wrong
- how they protected themselves while using it

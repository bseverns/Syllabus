# Prompt Cards

Use these to get started, then push past them.

## Make it more specific
- Give me 3 versions with different moods.
- Make this sound like a real teen, not a robot.
- Rewrite this with more texture and less generic language.
- Turn this into something fit for a poster, not an essay.
- Make this feel like a game world with rules and stakes.

## Make it more local without oversharing
- Create a scene inspired by a neighborhood basketball court at dusk.
- Design a flyer for a youth showcase with energy, music, and color.
- Imagine a bus ride story where every stop changes the world.
- Create a soundtrack for a community event, fashion drop, or street festival.
- Make a game level inspired by movement through a city, without naming a real person or address.

## Push on the bias
- Show me 3 versions that avoid stereotypes.
- What assumptions are you making in this answer?
- Rewrite this so the people in it feel specific, not generic.
- What details are missing that would make this more fair?
- Give me a version that avoids flattening a neighborhood into one story.

## Verify the facts
- Which part of this answer should be fact-checked?
- What claims here need a source?
- What might be inaccurate or invented?
- Give me a version with uncertainty clearly marked.

## For sound and image work
- Make it rougher, stranger, and less polished.
- Reduce clichés.
- Try a version with more rhythm and contrast.
- Make it feel human-made, not machine-smooth.
- Give me 5 variations that move in different directions.

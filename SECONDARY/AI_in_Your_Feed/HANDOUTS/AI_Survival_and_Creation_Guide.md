# AI Survival and Creation Guide

A quick guide for making with AI without giving away too much of yourself.

## 1. Use AI, but stay in charge
AI can help you brainstorm, remix, sketch, and prototype.
It should not replace your judgment.

Ask:
- Does this sound like me?
- Is this accurate?
- Is this respectful?
- Do I actually want this result?

## 2. Protect your privacy
Do **not** upload:
- your full name
- your address
- your phone number
- passwords
- school IDs
- private family details
- medical information
- legal information
- your face or voice unless the project truly needs it and you have permission

Also do not upload other people’s photos, voices, or private messages without consent.

## 3. Check the output
AI can sound confident and still be wrong.

Check for:
- made-up facts
- stereotypes
- weird “generic” language
- fake sources
- details that seem too neat or too smooth

## 4. Revise like an artist
The first result is rarely the best result.

Try changing:
- tone
- detail level
- audience
- point of view
- medium
- mood
- setting
- constraints

## 5. Watch for bias
Ask:
- Who is centered here?
- Who is missing?
- Who looks flattened or stereotyped?
- Does this output assume one kind of person is “normal”?

## 6. Keep receipts
Save:
- useful prompts
- screenshots
- notes about what changed
- sources you checked
- what you rejected

## 7. Make with care
Do not use AI to:
- impersonate people
- bully or shame people
- generate harm toward classmates or communities
- pretend something is true when you have not checked it

## 8. Good closing question
Before you hit submit, post, or share, ask:

**Did I make this with intention, and did I protect myself while making it?**

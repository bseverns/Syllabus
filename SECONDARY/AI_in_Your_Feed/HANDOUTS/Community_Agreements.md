# Community Agreements

We are here to make, question, and learn.

## Our agreements
1. We protect privacy.
2. We do not upload personal information.
3. We do not use AI to target, embarrass, or impersonate people.
4. We can critique the tool without shutting each other down.
5. We give each other room to revise.
6. We do not confuse speed with truth.
7. We stay authors of our own work.

## What to do when something feels off
Pause and ask:
- Is this accurate?
- Is this respectful?
- Is this safe to upload?
- Does this flatten someone?
- Should I ask for help before continuing?

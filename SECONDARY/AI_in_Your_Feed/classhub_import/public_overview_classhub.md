# AI in Your Feed: Create, Explore, Protect

Course slug: ai_in_your_feed
Grade level: 9th-12th
Session length: 90 minutes
Total sessions: 4
UI Level: secondary

## Course overview

This high school studio course helps students use AI creatively and responsibly while building stronger habits around privacy, bias-checking, and authorship. Instead of treating AI as futuristic hype, the course starts from tools students already encounter in feeds, search, images, music, games, and school platforms.

Students experiment with AI-supported making, question polished but misleading outputs, and finish with a small project plus a practical survival guide for future use.

## Learning goals

By the end of the course, students will be able to:

- identify where AI influences everyday attention and decision-making
- make and revise a small AI-supported creative artifact
- explain where an AI system helped and where it failed
- spot common bias, flattening, misinformation, or overconfidence in outputs
- apply safer privacy and consent habits when using AI tools

## What students make

Students produce:

- prompt logs or output notes
- one small AI-supported project
- one annotated revision or bias-check example
- one reflection on privacy, authorship, or verification

## Course format

Each session includes:

1. opening discussion or share
2. short demo or framing conversation
3. studio build or analysis time
4. reflection and wrap-up

## Tools and materials

- laptops or desktops
- selected low-friction AI tools
- projector
- handouts for privacy, prompting, and reflection
- headphones when audio tools are used

## Assessment

Assessment emphasizes:

- safe tool use
- revision and reflection
- critical awareness
- student ownership over the final work

## Final showcase

Students present a small project and explain what AI contributed, what they changed themselves, and one privacy or bias lesson they are taking forward.

# Session 01: Meet Your AI

Mission: Help students name where AI already shapes their everyday life and begin experimenting with tools while establishing safety norms.
Lesson slug (for course.yaml): s01-meet-your-ai
Slug rule: start with s01- and use lowercase letters, numbers, and dashes only.

Teacher prep
- Test all selected AI tools.
- Prepare one image, one text, and one sound demo.
- Print privacy and community handouts.
- Prepare a low-tech backup plan.

Materials
- Projector
- Laptops or desktops
- `HANDOUTS/Community_Agreements.md`
- `HANDOUTS/AI_Survival_and_Creation_Guide.md`
- Prompt logs or notebooks

Agenda
- 0-15 min: welcome and “where have you seen AI today?” discussion
- 15-25 min: community agreements and safety framing
- 25-45 min: mini-demo across tools
- 45-75 min: hands-on exploration
- 75-90 min: reflection and wrap-up

Checkpoints
- Students identify several places AI already appears in daily life.
- Students save at least one output or screenshot.
- Students can explain one privacy or safety norm.

Common stuck points + fixes
- Students treat AI as magic or novelty only -> return to concrete examples from feeds, search, and school tools.
- Account friction slows the room -> switch to screenshots, shared demos, or paper prompt planning.

Extensions
- Compare two different tools on the same prompt.
- Track how a prompt changes after one revision.

# Session 02: AI in Action: Games and Creativity

Mission: Use AI as a creative partner while reinforcing that students still design the experience and make the final choices.
Lesson slug (for course.yaml): s02-ai-in-action-games-and-creativity
Slug rule: start with s02- and use lowercase letters, numbers, and dashes only.

Teacher prep
- Prepare one small demo project that can be built quickly.
- Decide on project tracks that fit available tools.

Materials
- Laptops or desktops
- Projector
- Headphones if audio tools are used
- `HANDOUTS/Prompt_Cards.md`
- Optional assembly tools such as Scratch, Twine, slides, or p5.js

Agenda
- 0-15 min: check-in and artifact share
- 15-35 min: demo from weak prompt to stronger project draft
- 35-75 min: project-track studio time
- 75-90 min: reflection and close

Checkpoints
- Students revise prompts at least a few times.
- Students create a rough draft of a project.
- Students can name what they changed to make the result feel more like theirs.

Common stuck points + fixes
- Students accept the first output too quickly -> require at least one revision and one rejection.
- Projects stay too vague -> narrow to one scene, one poster, one beat, or one branching moment.

Extensions
- Build a before/after revision comparison.
- Combine outputs from two tools into one project artifact.

# Session 03: Spot the Bias and Verify

Mission: Teach students to inspect AI outputs for bias, flattening, and factual problems rather than trusting polish alone.
Lesson slug (for course.yaml): s03-spot-the-bias-and-verify
Slug rule: start with s03- and use lowercase letters, numbers, and dashes only.

Teacher prep
- Prepare several problematic AI outputs in advance.
- Choose examples that show inaccuracy, stereotype, flattening, or fake confidence.

Materials
- Projector
- Prepared sample outputs
- Student projects from Session 2
- `HANDOUTS/AI_Survival_and_Creation_Guide.md`

Agenda
- 0-15 min: opening discussion on polished-but-wrong outputs
- 15-35 min: pair analysis of prepared examples
- 35-75 min: studio time for revision and verification
- 75-90 min: reflection and discussion

Checkpoints
- Students identify at least one bias or error pattern.
- Students revise one project element with more care.
- Students can explain one verification strategy.

Common stuck points + fixes
- Students speak only in general terms like “it feels weird” -> ask what exact detail feels wrong, missing, or stereotyped.
- Students confuse style preference with factual or ethical concern -> separate “I do not like it” from “this misleads.”

Extensions
- Compare how a prompt changes when stereotypes are removed.
- Annotate one output with verification notes.

# Session 04: Privacy, Safety, and Showoff

Mission: Finish student projects, teach durable privacy habits, and close the course with a short showcase.
Lesson slug (for course.yaml): s04-privacy-safety-and-showoff
Slug rule: start with s04- and use lowercase letters, numbers, and dashes only.

Teacher prep
- Confirm export or screenshot options for final work.
- Decide showcase order.
- Print the student survival guide.

Materials
- Projector
- Laptops or desktops
- `HANDOUTS/AI_Survival_and_Creation_Guide.md`
- Reflection prompts

Agenda
- 0-20 min: privacy and safety mini-lesson
- 20-70 min: final studio time
- 70-85 min: short project showcase
- 85-90 min: final reflection

Checkpoints
- Students complete or nearly complete a final project artifact.
- Students can explain one privacy practice they will use in the future.
- Students can explain what AI did and what they did.

Common stuck points + fixes
- Students try to add too much at the end -> trim scope and finish one strong version.
- Students share work without thinking about consent or exposure -> pause and review classroom vs public sharing.

Extensions
- Turn the final reflection into a mini zine or slide.
- Run a gallery walk with peer notes.

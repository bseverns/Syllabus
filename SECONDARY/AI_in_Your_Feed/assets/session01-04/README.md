# Session 1–4 visual pack

Project these sheets; keep the live text, image, and sound demos tool-specific.

| Sessions | Visual | Use |

## Suggested sequence

- **Session 1:** Ask students to add examples to the “Where is AI already?” map. The sheet is the low-tech fallback, not a replacement for the planned image, text, and sound demonstrations.
- **Session 2:** Read the vague prompt, ask what decisions are missing, then reveal the constrained version.
- **Session 3:** Apply “sourced, specific, certain” to each prepared output. This frame avoids inventing or repeating stereotypes merely to illustrate bias.
- **Session 4:** Read the privacy checklist before students open a tool or prepare public-facing work.

## Alt text

- **Notice and direct:** Everyday AI systems surround a central “shaping choices” circle. A vague poster prompt becomes specific before the student selects, rejects, and edits.
- **Verify and protect:** Three cards ask whether an output is sourced, specific, and appropriately uncertain. A four-step checklist covers private data, personal media, consent, and audience.

# Build Your Block: Advanced Studio

A `PILOT` continuation of [Build Your Block](../../PRIMARY/Build_Your_Block/) for Grades 6–8.

Learners investigate a small public system, choose one interaction platform and one fabrication route, build an interactive prototype, test it with people, and revise from evidence. Cardboard and low-fidelity tests come before machines; the site selects and preflights the technical route.

- **Audience:** Grades 6–8; 12–18 learners
- **Format:** 28 sessions × 105 minutes (49 contact hours)
- **Staffing:** one lead plus one support adult when machine work or 16+ learners are present
- **Screen load:** `S2–S3`
- **Equipment:** `E1` minimum; `E3` with one selected interaction platform and fabrication route
- **Readiness:** `PILOT`

Required artifacts are community/audience research, a design brief, digital model, physical interactive prototype, user-test record, public demonstration, and archive. Machines remain instructor-operated unless the site has separately approved learner procedures. This adaptation does not replace the source course or claim prior delivery.

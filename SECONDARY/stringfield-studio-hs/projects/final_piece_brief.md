# Final Piece Brief

## Title

**String, Gesture, Drawing: Final Studio Piece**

## Goal

Create a short piece that turns gesture into a deliberate composition.

Your final piece should show that you can:

- choose gestures intentionally
- shape a clear beginning, middle, and end
- connect a visual gesture map to a recorded sound result
- explain what was planned, what changed, and what surprised you

## What to submit

Each student or pair submits:

- one final audio piece, 60-120 seconds
- one gesture map drawing or score
- one short artist statement
- optional visuals if your class uses a visual track

## Required features

Your piece must include:

- at least 3 distinct gestures
- at least 1 clear contrast:
  - sparse to dense
  - quiet to loud
  - smooth to rough
  - repetitive to unpredictable
- evidence of revision after feedback

## Artist statement prompts

Write 100-200 words responding to these:

1. What gestures or textures did you focus on?
2. How did your drawing guide the piece?
3. What changed between your first sketch and final version?
4. What part feels most intentional?
5. What part still feels surprising or unstable?

Use `handouts/artist_statement_template.md` if you want a scaffold.

## Presentation format

At the final showing, be ready to:

1. show your gesture map
2. name 2-3 gestures the audience should listen for
3. play the piece
4. explain one choice you revised on purpose

## Suggested work timeline

- **Session 3:** draft gesture map
- **Session 4:** record the first sound sketch
- **Session 5:** add layers or loops
- **Session 6:** refine sound or add visuals
- **Session 7:** revise after peer feedback
- **Session 8:** present

## Revision reminders

- Cut what drags.
- Keep the strongest gesture clear.
- Let contrast do some of the structure work.
- Save versions as you revise.

# Final Piece Rubric

Use this for the Session 8 showing and for final assessment of the course project.

## 1. Gesture vocabulary and experimentation

**Emerging**
- Uses very few gestures or repeats one move without much variation.

**Developing**
- Uses more than one gesture and shows some willingness to experiment.

**Proficient**
- Uses several gestures intentionally and can name them clearly.

**Strong**
- Uses gesture variety with confidence and makes those choices meaningful to the piece.

## 2. Composition and structure

**Emerging**
- Piece feels unfinished or has little sense of arc.

**Developing**
- Piece has a beginning and ending, but the structure is still loose.

**Proficient**
- Piece shows a clear shape with at least one strong contrast or shift.

**Strong**
- Piece has a compelling arc and uses pacing, contrast, and texture with intention.

## 3. Documentation and technical care

**Emerging**
- Files, drawings, or versions are incomplete or hard to follow.

**Developing**
- Most materials are present, but naming or organization is inconsistent.

**Proficient**
- Gesture map, recordings, and final export are clearly labeled and usable.

**Strong**
- Materials are well organized, easy to navigate, and show careful revision over time.

## 4. Reflection and presentation

**Emerging**
- Has trouble explaining choices or describing the process.

**Developing**
- Can name a few choices, but explanations stay general.

**Proficient**
- Explains gesture choices, revisions, and surprises in clear language.

**Strong**
- Presents work with specificity and can connect gesture, drawing, and sound in a thoughtful way.

## Suggested scoring conversion

- **Emerging:** 1
- **Developing:** 2
- **Proficient:** 3
- **Strong:** 4

If you want a 16-point rubric, add the four category scores together.

## What to reward most

Prioritize:

- listening
- revision
- care with files and artifacts
- clear explanation of choices

Do not over-reward expensive gear, advanced software tricks, or polish that hides weak thinking.

# Session 07 – Compose a Short Piece

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Lab + peer feedback  
- **Goals:**
  - Shape sketches into a 60–120 second piece with a clear arc.
  - Encourage intentional use of dynamics and density.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Near-final piece (audio + optional visuals).

## Before class (instructor)

- Decide on a target length (e.g., 60–90 seconds).
- Prepare a simple structure suggestion:
  - Start → build → shift → end.

## In-class plan

### 0–15 min — Structure check-in

- On the board:
  - Ask students to sketch their piece’s shape:
    - Quiet/loud, sparse/dense over time.
- Share 2–3 shapes with the class.

### 15–65 min — Focused work time

- Students:
  - Refine their piece:
    - Adjust where sections change.
    - Ensure at least one clear contrast in the middle.
  - If using visuals, align major changes with visual shifts.

### 65–80 min — Peer feedback pairs

- In pairs:
  - Play each piece.
  - Peers respond:
    - “Where did you feel most engaged?”
    - “Was there a part that felt too long/too short?”

### 80–90 min — Exit ticket

- “One specific change I’ll make before the final showing is…”

## After class / Homework

- Students implement final tweaks before Session 08.

# Session 03 – Sound Drawing: Lines, Dots, and Textures

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Drawing + listening  
- **Goals:**
  - Connect gestures to visual marks.
  - Invent new gestures by drawing first, then playing.
- **Deliverables:**
  - One “gesture map” drawing per student.

## Before class (instructor)

- Prepare paper and drawing tools (markers, pens, etc.).
- Ensure audio playback works for recorded gestures.

## In-class plan

### 0–15 min — Listen and imagine

- Play short clips from the gesture library.
- Students:
  - Close eyes while listening.
  - Imagine what the sound “looks” like.

### 15–40 min — Draw existing gestures

- Students draw abstract marks for several known gestures:
  - Dots, lines, dense scribbles, smooth curves, etc.
- Group share a few examples.

### 40–70 min — Invent new gestures via drawing

- Assignment:
  - Create a **gesture map**: a page of lines/marks that might be performed over 30–60 seconds.
- In pairs:
  - Swap gesture maps and try to “play the drawing” on the string.

### 70–85 min — Discuss surprises

- What was easy/hard about translating drawing → gesture?
- Did any new sounds appear?

### 85–90 min — Exit ticket

- Students label their gesture map with a title.

## After class / Homework

- Optional: refine gesture map or color-code sections for intensity.

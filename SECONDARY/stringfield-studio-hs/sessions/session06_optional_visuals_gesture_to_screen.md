# Session 06 – Optional Visuals: Gesture to Screen

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Code lab (or extra audio time)  
- **Goals (visual track):**
  - Introduce a very simple audio-reactive or key-triggered visual sketch.
  - Let students personalize basic visual parameters.
- **Goals (audio-only track):**
  - Provide extra time to refine recordings and structure.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Either a basic visual sketch or a more polished audio layer.

## Before class (instructor)

- Decide:
  - Are you running a **visuals track**, an **audio-only track**, or both?
- For visuals:
  - Prepare a minimal p5.js or Processing sketch that:
    - Reacts to audio amplitude, **or**
    - Reacts to key presses representing gestures.

## In-class plan (visuals track)

### 0–20 min — Show base sketch

- Demo:
  - How sound or key presses change shapes/lines.
- Point out what parts students can edit:
  - Colors, thickness, shapes, background.

### 20–70 min — Student customization

- Students:
  - Change colors/shapes to match the feeling of their piece.
  - Optionally map different keys to different “gestures” on screen.

### 70–85 min — Quick share

- A few students run their sketch and play part of their audio.

### 85–90 min — Exit ticket

- “One way my visual matches my sound is…”

## In-class plan (audio-only track)

- Use most of the session as:
  - Extended recording time
  - Cleanup of layers
  - Experimentation with subtle effects (reverb, EQ).

## After class / Homework

- Students decide whether their final piece will include visuals or be audio-only.

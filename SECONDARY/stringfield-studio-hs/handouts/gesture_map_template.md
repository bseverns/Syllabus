## Before you draw

Answer these quickly:

- What is the main feeling or texture I want?
- Which 2-3 gestures do I already know I might use?
- Do I want the piece to get denser, thinner, louder, quieter, smoother, or rougher over time?

## Draw the map

Use the page however you want, but include:

- at least 3 visual regions or sections
- symbols, colors, or marks that mean something to you
- a left-to-right or top-to-bottom reading path if time matters

## Performance notes

- Where should the piece begin quietly or simply?
- Where should it change?
- Where should it end?
- Which part do I expect to be hardest to perform?

## After trying it once

- What sounded like I expected?
- What sounded different?
- What should I redraw, relabel, or simplify?

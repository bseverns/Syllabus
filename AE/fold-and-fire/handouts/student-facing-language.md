# Fold & Fire — Student Guide

## What you’re making
- **Vref** creates a midpoint so audio can live on 5V power.
- Op-amp boosts the signal (**drive**).
- Diodes in feedback push back on peaks (**soft clip**).
- Bonus: a Schmitt trigger turns audio into **gates**.

## Outputs
- DRY (buffered)
- DIST (distorted)
- GATE1 (thresholded from DIST)
- GATE2 (optional, filtered)

## Key idea
Distortion is controlled bending. Gates are decisions. This module makes both.

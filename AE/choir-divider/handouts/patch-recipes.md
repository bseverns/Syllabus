# Patch recipes — Choir Divider (AE Modular)

## 1) Downbeat + offbeat
Q0 = downbeat. Q5 = half-time offbeat. Patch to two drums/envelopes.

## 2) Accent mask
PATTERN = OR(Q0, Q7). Use PATTERN to open a VCA only on selected steps.

## 3) 3-step tension
Reset at Q2 (3-step loop). Layer against a 4-step or straight clock source.

## 4) Swing illusion
Use Q2/Q3 as late accents (hats/ghost notes) while Q0 anchors the bar.

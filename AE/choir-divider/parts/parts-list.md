# Parts list (one bench kit per pair)

Core setup:
- 1 AE Modular BRAEDBOARD or instructor-approved 5V breadboard setup
- jumper wires and 4–6 AE-compatible patch leads
- regulated +5V/GND connection approved for the teaching platform
- 1 multimeter per pair or per two pairs

ICs:
- 1 NE555 (optional internal clock)
- 1 CD4017 decade counter
- 1 CD40106 Schmitt inverter (optional)

Resistors:
- 2 × 1k (LED series)
- 1 × 47k (555 timing)
- 2 × 100k (RESET and PATTERN pull-downs)

Capacitors:
- 3 × 100nF (one decoupler at each populated IC)
- 1 × 10nF (555 pin 5 decouple)
- 1 × 10uF electrolytic (slow clock timing; instructor verifies polarity)

Other:
- 2 LEDs
- 1 × 50k potentiometer (rate)
- 6 × 1N4148 or equivalent small-signal diodes for reset/pattern experiments

Spares for the room:
- one known-good, correctly oriented copy of each IC per four teams
- extra LEDs, diodes, jumper wires, and 100nF capacitors
- one instructor-built or instructor-verified reference circuit kept out of student rotation

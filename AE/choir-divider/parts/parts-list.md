# Parts list (bench kit)

ICs:
- NE555 (optional internal clock)
- CD4017 decade counter
- CD40106 Schmitt inverter (optional)

Resistors:
- 1k (LED series)
- 47k (555 timing / pull-ups)
- 100k (pull-downs)

Capacitors:
- 100nF (decoupling at each IC)
- 10nF (555 pin 5 decouple)
- 10uF electrolytic (slow clock timing)

Other:
- LEDs (2 recommended)
- 50k pot (rate)

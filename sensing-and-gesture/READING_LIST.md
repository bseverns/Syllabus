# Reading List – Sensing & Gesture (College)

This list is intentionally larger than any single run of the course will use.  
Each term, choose a subset and sequence it in `READING_SCHEDULE.md`.

---

## 1. Gesture, Scores, and Notation

- Introductory texts on **Labanotation** and movement notation  
- *Fluxus Performance Workbook* (Friedman, Smith, Sawchyn, eds.) – event scores  
- Yvonne Rainer – writings and documentation around *Trio A* and task-based choreography  

---

## 2. Bodies, Disability, and Movement

- Aimi Hamraie – *Building Access: Universal Design and the Politics of Disability*  
- Petra Kuppers – *Disability Culture and Community Performance*  
- Additional disability studies texts that foreground movement norms and alternative vocabularies  

---

## 3. Sensing, Surveillance, and “Smart” Bodies

- Simone Browne – *Dark Matters: On the Surveillance of Blackness*  
- Ruha Benjamin – *Race After Technology: Abolitionist Tools for the New Jim Code*  
- Short articles on surveillance, biometrics, and racialized identification systems  

---

## 4. Embodied Interaction, HCI, and Gesture Interfaces

- Paul Dourish – *Where the Action Is: The Foundations of Embodied Interaction*  
- Lucy Suchman – *Plans and Situated Actions* / *Human–Machine Reconfigurations*  
- Jacob Wobbrock et al. – “User-Defined Gestures for Surface Computing” (CHI 2009)  
- Case studies such as OmniTouch, Kinect-based interaction, or other gesture interfaces  

---

## 5. Practice-Based & Historical References

- *Fluxus Performance Workbook* (as above) and other instruction-based performance anthologies  
- Documentation of gesture-driven installations and performances (your own, or artists you can share)  
- Contemporary examples of consumer gesture-based technologies (game controllers, VR/AR, wearables)

Use these to seed discussions, precedents, and short in-class screenings or score-writing exercises.

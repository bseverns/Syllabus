# Reading Schedule – Sensing & Gesture (College, 14 Weeks)

This is a working map of readings/media to weeks.  
Adjust for your context and student load.

---

## Week 1 – What Is Gesture? What Is Sensing?

**Theme:** Definitions + everyday gesture, low technical load.

**Core**

- Short essay or article on everyday touch/gesture interfaces (tap, swipe, pinch, zoom).  
- A few Fluxus event scores from the *Fluxus Performance Workbook*.

**Optional**

- 1–2 pages from a Labanotation overview to show that movement can be symbolically encoded.

---

## Week 2 – Drawing Gesture

**Theme:** Scores, notation, and diagrams of movement.

**Core**

- Labanotation introduction (article or book excerpt).  
- 1–2 simple scored examples from a source like LabanLab.  
- 5–10 short scores from the *Fluxus Performance Workbook*.

**Optional**

- Brief passage from Yvonne Rainer on task-based choreography.

---

## Week 3 – Bodies as Sensors

**Theme:** Proprioception, disability, and who gets to move/how.

**Core**

- Petra Kuppers – short chapter or excerpt from *Disability Culture and Community Performance*.  
- Aimi Hamraie – excerpt from *Building Access*.

**Optional**

- Any short reflection text on alternative movement vocabularies or disability arts practice.

---

## Week 4 – From Gesture to Data

**Theme:** How movement becomes sensor data and interface commands.

**Core**

- Jacob Wobbrock et al. – “User-Defined Gestures for Surface Computing” (skim for main ideas).  
- Short tutorial/documentation snippet for your chosen hardware (Arduino/micro:bit/Teensy sensor input).

**Optional**

- 3–4 pages from Paul Dourish, *Where the Action Is*, introducing embodied interaction.

---

## Week 5 – Cameras & Pose

**Theme:** Room-level sensing, pose estimation, visual tracking.

**Core**

- Simone Browne – excerpt from *Dark Matters: On the Surveillance of Blackness*.  
- Documentation or tutorial for ml5.js PoseNet or a similar pose-estimation tool.

**Optional**

- Short overview of a case study like OmniTouch or Kinect-based interaction.

---

## Week 6 – Mapping Gesture

**Theme:** Turning sensor streams into interaction logics.

**Core**

- Paul Dourish – focused excerpt from *Where the Action Is*.  
- Revisit Wobbrock or a similar gesture-interface article with attention to mapping.

**Optional**

- Short design essay on mapping continuous sensor data to meaningful ranges and actions.

---

## Week 7 – Gesture, Power, Access

**Theme:** Surveillance, race, disability, misrecognition.

**Core**

- Simone Browne – second excerpt from *Dark Matters*.  
- Ruha Benjamin – excerpt from *Race After Technology*.

**Optional**

- Hamraie – section on disability maker culture or “crip technoscience”.

---

## Week 8 – Prototype 0 Crit

**Theme:** Midterm making, reflection.

**Core**

- No heavy new readings.  
- Ask students to re-read:
  - Their own Gesture Journals and mapping notes, plus  
  - One earlier theoretical text of their choice (bring one quote into crit).

**Optional**

- Short case-study documentation of a gesture-based artwork or performance.

---

## Week 9 – Systems of Gesture

**Theme:** Thinking in systems; plans vs situated action.

**Core**

- Lucy Suchman – excerpt from *Plans and Situated Actions* or *Human–Machine Reconfigurations*.  
- Short systems/cybernetics piece or systems-art essay to tie in diagrams and feedback.

**Optional**

- Brief return to Dourish on coupling and embodied action.

---

## Week 10 – Final Project Proposals

**Theme:** Scoping projects in terms of bodies, spaces, and publics.

**Core**

- Petra Kuppers – excerpt focusing on community performance and shared space.  
- Relevant institutional policy/ethics document (recording, research ethics, accessibility).

**Optional**

- Aimi Hamraie – passage on universal vs situated access to help frame installation decisions.

---

## Week 11 – Build Sprint I: Core Gesture Pipeline

**Theme:** Making; supportive, targeted reading.

**Core**

- Technical reference tailored to each group’s pipeline (sensor docs, PoseNet reference, audio/visual library docs).

**Optional**

- Short HCI/design-pattern piece on debugging interaction or handling misrecognition.

---

## Week 12 – Build Sprint II: Choreography & Interface

**Theme:** Onboarding, legibility, staging interaction.

**Core**

- Fluxus Performance Workbook – 2–3 scores as models for writing participation instructions.  
- Short article or chapter on performance scores and audience participation.

**Optional**

- Short essay on documenting performance or installation work.

---

## Week 13 – Showing / Performance Night

**Theme:** Event, audience, documentation.

**Core**

- No substantial new readings.  
- Ask students to annotate one earlier reading that now feels different after running their work.

**Optional**

- Short text on performance documentation or archival practice.

---

## Week 14 – Deinstallation, Documentation & Final Crit

**Theme:** Reflection and future trajectories.

**Core**

- No new reading; focus on final documentation bundle and individual reflections.

**Optional**

- “Where next?” reading: contemporary article on gesture in AI, robotics, or disability arts, to point beyond the course.

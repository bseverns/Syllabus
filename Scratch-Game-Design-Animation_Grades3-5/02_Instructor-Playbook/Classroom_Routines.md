# Classroom Routines (for Grades 3–5, high-energy groups)

## Signals
- **“Hands off keys”** = palms on lap, eyes forward
- **“Freeze frame”** = stop moving, look at teacher
- **Call/response** options:
  - Teacher: “Game Designers!” Students: “Test it!”
  - Teacher: “Animators!” Students: “Frame by frame!”

## Help system
- **Ask 3 then me:** ask 3 peers before teacher
- **Help desk:** two rotating students sit at a “help station” for 5-minute shifts
- **Stuck protocol:** (1) screenshot with your eyes (describe the bug), (2) point to the script, (3) say what you expected, (4) say what happened

## Movement
- Every sprint break includes:
  - stand up
  - stretch arms
  - look away from screen
  - sit down and save

## Device safety
- Save at each reset.
- Projects titled: `FirstName_GameTitle_WkXX`.

## Playtesting culture
- Feedback sentence starters:
  - “I liked when…”
  - “I got confused when…”
  - “I wish the game would…”

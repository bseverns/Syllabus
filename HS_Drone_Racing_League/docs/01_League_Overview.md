# League Overview & Philosophy

**Goal:** Build disciplined, creative pilots. Students learn to fly clean lines, maintain airframes, operate safely, and contribute to a supportive team culture.

**Progression (overview):**
1. **Weeks 1–4 — Bootcamp:** TRUST + simulator fundamentals + Tiny‑Whoop indoor drills.
2. **Weeks 5–8 — Transfer:** Outdoor LOS, then FPV; course setup; timing; heat etiquette.
3. **Weeks 9–12 — Race Prep:** Pace vs. perfection, passing protocol, pit practice, battery discipline.
4. **Summer — League:** Weekly practice + monthly meets; introduce bracket formats and LiveTime/RotorHazard timing.

**Roles students rotate through:**
- **Pilot** (primary), **Spotter**, **Pit Tech**, **Marshal**, **Timer/Scorer**, **Safety Officer**, **Media**.

**Culture:**
- Calm minds go fast. We coach breathing, visualization, and recovery after crashes.
- “Finish every heat.” Consistency beats hero‑laps.
- “Fix it, fly it.” Maintenance is training.

# Team Management, Code of Conduct, and Inclusion

**Code of conduct (draft):**
- Respect teammates, volunteers, and venues.
- No flying over people; spotters required; follow frequency board.
- Help others fix their gear; we all launch together.

**Roles rotation:** Pilot → Spotter → Marshal → Pit Tech → Timer → Media.

**Performance rubric (pilot):** see `rubrics/pilot_progression.csv`.

**Mentorship:** Pair new pilots with seniors (shadowing for build, setup, and first FPV).

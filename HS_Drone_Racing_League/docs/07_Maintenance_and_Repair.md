# Maintenance & Repair (Make it part of practice)

**Daily:** Prop swap, motor screws check, antenna check, VTX mount, battery straps.

**Weekly:** Frame inspection, arm bolts with threadlocker, USB ports, conformal where needed.

**Firmware & tuning:**
- Betaflight Blackbox: https://betaflight.com/docs/development/Blackbox and explainer: https://oscarliang.com/blackbox/
- BLHeli_32 manual: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bitdump/BLHeli/master/BLHeli_32%20ARM/BLHeli_32%20manual%20ARM%20Rev32.x.pdf
- Betaflight modes & turtle mode: https://betaflight.com/docs/development/Modes

**Soldering basics:**
- 350–400 °C, flux, pre‑tin leads & pads, strain relief with zip‑ties/UV glue. Practice on scrap PCBs.

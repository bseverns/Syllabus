# Equipment Intake & Triage — Unknown/Legacy FPV Gear

**Goal:** Identify what works today, what can be bridged, and the minimum purchases that unlock training immediately.

## 1) Radios
- Note **firmware & protocol**: EdgeTX/OpenTX? Internal module (e.g., FrSky ACCST D16, DSMX) vs. **ELRS 2.4/900** module?
- USB test in simulator. If gimbals are noisy, prioritize new radio for coaches.
- **Bridge plan:** Keep legacy FrSky/DSMX quads flying via multiprotocol modules while standardizing new builds around **ELRS 2.4**.

## 2) Goggles / Video
- Determine analog (5.8 GHz) vs **digital** (DJI/Walksnail/HDZero). Inspect receivers/antennas.
- Indoor racing favors **analog or HDZero** for ultra‑low latency and easy timing integration; use what you have safely.

## 3) Airframes
- Inventory by class: **65–75 mm Tiny‑Whoop (1S/2S)**, **3–5″**.
- Check FC/ESC stack, firmware (Betaflight/iNav), VTX brand, **pit mode** availability.
- Label quads with **pilot name + VTX channel**.

## 4) Power & Charging
- Count LiPos by cell count and connector type (BT2.0/PH2.0/XT30/XT60).
- Inspect for puffing; segregate questionable packs; create a **quarantine box**.
- Verify chargers, balance leads, and power supplies. Post storage‑voltage SOPs.

## 5) Minimum coach kit (to de‑risk Day 1)
- 2× Tiny‑Whoop trainer bundles (quad + radio + goggles) that are **known‑good**.
- 1× field soldering setup (iron, flux, solder, heat‑shrink) + smoke stopper.
- Extra props/strap/antenna kits, LiPo bags, and two smart chargers.

## 6) Standardization roadmap
- Move pilots to **ELRS 2.4** radios/receivers as funding allows.
- Keep a small **compatibility cache** (FrSky/DSMX) for legacy airframes.
- Maintain a **VTX table** pack and frequency board; teach pit‑mode rituals.

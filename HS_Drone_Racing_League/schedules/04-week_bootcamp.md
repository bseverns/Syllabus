# 4‑Week Bootcamp (Launch Plan)

**Week 1**
- Admin: TRUST overview & enrollment; safety briefing; LiPo talk
- Sim: Controller setup; boxes & lines drills
- Field (indoors): LOS hover, figure‑8s through whoop gates

**Week 2**
- Sim: Ladders + time trials (record PBs)
- Field: First FPV with spotters; very slow gates; arm/disarm rituals
- Shop: Props, motor screws, basic soldering demo

**Week 3**
- Sim: Gate party (target 50 clean)
- Field: Course flow; recovery/turtle mode; passing protocol
- Ops: Frequency board; pit etiquette; battery charging roles

**Week 4**
- Mini‑meet: Qualifying + mains (2‑3 packs each)
- Debrief: PBs, consistency %, parts wear check
- Parents Q&A and summer plan

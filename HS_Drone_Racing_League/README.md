# High School Drone Racing League — Starter Kit

This folder is a ready-to-run starter kit for launching and coaching a high‑school FPV drone racing program. It’s organized for quick onboarding, safe operations, and progressive pilot training from zero to race‑ready.

**How to use**
1) Start with `docs/00_Safety_and_Law.md` and `docs/01_League_Overview.md`.
2) Use `schedules/04-week_bootcamp.md` for the first month, then loop `schedules/summer_extension.md`.
3) Share `docs/03_Sim_Training.md` with students to begin flying *today*.
4) Print `checklists/` and bring to the field.
5) Customize `forms/` for your school and district.

> This kit favors sub‑250 g indoor/Tiny‑Whoop training first, then outdoor 3–5″ racing craft. It assumes EdgeTX-compatible radios, Betaflight‑based quads (or HDZero/DJI video as available), and MultiGP‑style track norms.

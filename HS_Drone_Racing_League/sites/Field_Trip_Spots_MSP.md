# Field Trip & Partnership Spots — Minneapolis–Saint Paul

> Always contact hosts in advance, confirm site rules, and bring proof of insurance/permits where required.

## Home Base
- **PCYC / PYC Arts & Technology** — 2210 Oliver Ave N, Minneapolis, MN 55411. Indoor Tiny‑Whoop practice; family/community showcases.

## FRIA & Club Fields (outdoors)
- **Grassfield RC Club (FRIA)** — 8901 101st Ave N, Brooklyn Park, MN 55445 (close to North Mpls). AMA‑chartered; FRIA status published. Great for LOS, FPV demos, and guest days.
- **Twin Cities Radio Controllers (TCRC) — Model Air Park** — 18266 W Sioux Vista Dr, Jordan, MN 55352. Large AMA field, frequent events.
- **St Paul Modelers RC Club (SPMRC)** — Ham Lake, MN. Active club; check site for meeting/field info.
- **Anoka County Radio Control (ACRC) (FRIA)** — Paved runway; check site for directions & guest policy.

*Tip:* Use the **FAA FRIA map** to verify current FRIA boundaries and discover additional sites.

## University / Education
- **University of Minnesota — Alphadrone (student FPV team)** — Contact AEM Dept. for tours or joint practice sessions.

## Community / League
- **MultiGP (MN chapter: “The Mighty Drones”)** — Join the group to find race days, practice meets, and scrimmages. Coordinate youth‑friendly heats.

## Parks & Permits (Minneapolis)
- **MPRB Drone Use Permit** — Required for flights in Minneapolis parks; submit 10+ days in advance.
- **MPRB Filming/Photography Permits** — Required for media projects in parks.

## Indoor Alternatives
- Community center gyms, school cafeterias, unused field houses. Indoors is **not** in the NAS; still obtain facility permission and follow safety code.

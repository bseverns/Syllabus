# Week 3 – First Oscillators

## At a glance

- Length: 2–3 hours  
- Theme: Breadboarding simple tone/noise circuits.

## Goals

By the end of this week, students should:

- Deepen their understanding of this week's theme.  
- Connect at least one reading or listening example to their own instrument ideas.  
- Produce one small artifact (study, sketch, or build step) that feeds into Prototype 0 or the final instrument.

## Suggested readings / media

- Beginner electronics primer (voltage, current, resistance).
- Excerpt or handout on simple oscillators (555, CMOS, etc.).

## Before class (prep)

- Assign readings/listening and ask students to bring 3–5 bullet-point notes or questions.  
- Prepare any demos, tools, or example instruments you want to show.  
- Check that lab stations, tools, and parts are ready for the planned activities.

## In-class plan

Use this section to sketch your timings and activities. A typical pattern might be:

- Short warm-up listening or show-and-tell  
- Discussion of readings/listening, guided by student questions  
- Bench or studio time (building, testing, sketching, rehearsing)  
- Short in-progress shares or mini-crits

You might also include:

- Safety refreshers when new tools or techniques are introduced  
- Quick, timed constraints (e.g., “10 minutes to make a sound from this object”) to keep energy up

## After class / Homework

- Continue or extend the in-class build or study.  
- Short written or sketched response connecting the week's readings/listening to their own instrument work.  
- Instrument & Rig Journal updates, focusing on this week's theme.

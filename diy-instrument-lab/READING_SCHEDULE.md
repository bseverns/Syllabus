# Reading Schedule – DIY Instrument Lab (College, 14 Weeks)

Use this as a template and adjust to fit your actual selections and student reading load.

---

## Week 1 – Listening to Instruments

**Theme:** What counts as an instrument? Who decides?

**Possible readings:**

- Short introductory text or excerpt on the history of electronic instruments or noise.  
- 1–2 short interviews with artists discussing their self-built instruments.  

---

## Week 2 – Sound from Objects

**Theme:** Contact mics, surface sound, found objects.

**Possible readings:**

- Excerpt from Nick Collins, *Handmade Electronic Music*, chapter on contact microphones and found sound.  
- Short sound art case study using contact mics or amplified objects.

---

## Week 3 – First Oscillators

**Theme:** Simple audio circuits.

**Possible readings:**

- Collins or similar primer on simple oscillators (555, CMOS, etc.).  
- Short electronics handout on voltage, current, resistance, and safety.

---

## Week 4 – Noise, Feedback, and Glitch

**Theme:** Embracing and framing instability.

**Possible readings:**

- Kim Cascone – “The Aesthetics of Failure” (or excerpt).  
- Short text or interview with an artist who uses feedback and circuits-as-instruments.

---

## Week 5 – Interfaces & Enclosures

**Theme:** How instruments meet hands and bodies.

**Possible readings:**

- Short design or HCI text on affordances and embodied interaction.  
- Case study of an unusual controller (accessibility-focused, game controller hack, etc.).

---

## Week 6 – Sensors & Gestures

**Theme:** Mapping gesture to sound.

**Possible readings:**

- Short HCI/interaction design article on gesture and mapping.  
- Documentation/tutorial excerpt on using sensors (FSR, LDR, etc.) in a simple audio or control context.

---

## Week 7 – Microcontrollers & MIDI

**Theme:** Bridging hardware and software instruments.

**Possible readings:**

- Introductory Arduino (or similar) guide to reading analog controls and sending MIDI.  
- Short case study of a microcontroller-based controller used in performance.

---

## Week 8 – Prototype 0 Crit

**Theme:** Reflection on first instruments.

**Reading task:**

- Ask students to revisit one earlier conceptual reading (Cascone, Rodgers, Oliveros, etc.) and bring a quote that now resonates differently after having built something.

---

## Week 9 – Effects & Processing

**Theme:** Routing and shaping sound.

**Possible readings:**

- Short text on signal flow in studios / live rigs.  
- Case study of a performer whose rig is heavily effects- or routing-based.

---

## Week 10 – Ensembles, Networks, and Shared Control

**Theme:** Group instruments and collective play.

**Possible readings:**

- Short reading on collective improvisation or ensemble instrument design.  
- Interview or documentation for a group who plays only self-built instruments.

---

## Week 11 – Final Build Sprint I

**Theme:** Stabilizing builds.

**Reading task:**

- Technical references as needed (datasheets, microcontroller docs).  
- Optional short reflection text on repair, maintenance, or lutherie as practice.

---

## Week 12 – Final Build Sprint II

**Theme:** Enclosures and staging.

**Possible readings:**

- Short text on instrument aesthetics and stage presence, or on installation design.  
- Optional piece on ergonomics and access.

---

## Week 13 – Performance / Installation Night

**Theme:** Showing the work.

**Reading task:**

- No new reading; ask students to bring notes on how their playing or audience interaction differed from their expectations.

---

## Week 14 – Final Crit & Documentation

**Theme:** Reflection and futures.

**Possible readings:**

- Optional “where next?” reading: a recent article or interview on DIY instruments, hacking, or post-digital sound practices.  
- Students re-read one favorite text from the semester and use it explicitly in their final reflections.

# Session 01 – Lo-Fi & Listening to Rooms

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Listening lab + discussion  
- **Goals:**
  - Introduce “lo-fi” as an aesthetic, not just “bad quality.”
  - Help students notice room tone and everyday sound.
  - Set expectations and norms for the workshop.
- **Deliverables:**
  - A class list of local sounds to hunt for.
  - Students leave with a simple recording homework prompt.

## Before class (instructor)

- Pick 3–5 short listening examples:
  - “Clean” production vs. noisy/lo-fi recordings.
  - Ideally 1–2 examples that include clear room tone / crowd sound.
- Test audio playback in the room.
- Decide where you’ll collect homework files (Drive, LMS, USB, etc.).

## In-class plan

### 0–10 min — Welcome & framing

- Quick round of names + favorite sound (not favorite song).  
- Overview of the course:
  - We’ll use **found sound** and **rooms** as instruments.
  - No prior music experience required.

### 10–30 min — Listening: Clean vs. Lo-Fi

- Play 2–3 contrasting tracks:
  - Ask students to jot what they hear:
    - Background noise?
    - Tape hiss / vinyl crackle?
    - Room / crowd sounds?
- Group discussion:
  - “What makes this feel clean?”
  - “What makes this feel messy or intimate?”

### 30–45 min — What is a Room Sound?

- Short explanation of:
  - Room tone
  - Reverb / echo
  - Noise as texture
- 60-second silent listening:
  - Students sit quietly and write down every sound they can identify in the room.

### 45–70 min — Sound Hunt Brainstorm

- On the board, list:
  - School-specific sounds (buses, hallways, doors, bells, sports, etc.)
  - Home / neighborhood sounds (traffic, cooking, pets, etc.)
- Students star 3–5 sounds they might realistically record this week.

### 70–85 min — Homework briefing

- Demonstrate a quick phone or laptop recording:
  - Emphasize “short is fine” (1–5 seconds).
- Homework prompt:
  - Record **3–5 short sounds** from your life.
  - Aim for **at least one indoor** and **one outdoor** recording.
  - Save them as: `firstname_sound01`, etc.

### 85–90 min — Exit ticket

- One sentence on a sticky or in the LMS:
  - “One sound I want to capture this week is…”

## After class / Homework

- **Students:** Record 3–5 short sounds and have them ready for import next session.  
- **Instructor:** Prepare a folder structure (per-student, or class shared).

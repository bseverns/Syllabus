# Session 08 – Listening Party & Reflection

## At a glance

- **Length:** 90 minutes  
- **Mode:** Group share + discussion  
- **Goals:**
  - Share finished (or near-finished) tracks.
  - Reflect on listening habits and everyday sound.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Final track and artwork presented.
  - Short written reflection.

## Before class (instructor)

- Gather all audio files and cover images into a playlist/folder.
- Test playback setup (projector, speakers, etc.).

## In-class plan

### 0–10 min — Setup

- Explain format:
  - Each piece gets ~2–3 minutes:
    - Show cover
    - Play 30–45 seconds
    - 1–2 comments from peers.

### 10–65 min — Listening party

- Play each student’s work.
- Prompt peers:
  - “Name one sound you noticed.”
  - “Where did you think the room was affecting the sound?”

### 65–80 min — Reflection writing

- Students respond in writing:
  - “How did this class change the way you listen?”
  - “What is one sound from your daily life you’ll never hear the same way again?”
  - Optional: what they’d do with more time/gear.

### 80–90 min — Closing circle

- Quick go-round:
  - “One thing you’re proud of from your piece or someone else’s.”

## After class / Homework

- Optional: share playlist with students/families per school policy.

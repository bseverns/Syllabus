# Diffuser Tops — Variants

Four printable options sized to your pedestal cavity. Measure **cavity_w**, **cavity_d**, and **lip_depth** from your printed pedestal (or keep defaults if you used the provided SCAD).

- `diffuser_square_siton.scad` — rests inside the top rebate with small clearance.
- `diffuser_square_snapin.scad` — slightly oversized with micro barbs for a light press-fit.
- `diffuser_round_siton.scad` — round plate (good with circular vases), sits in rebate.
- `diffuser_round_snapin.scad` — round with micro barbs; gentler snap/press.

> Tip: print in **natural/white PLA** at 0.24–0.28 mm layer height and **100% concentric infill** for even diffusion.

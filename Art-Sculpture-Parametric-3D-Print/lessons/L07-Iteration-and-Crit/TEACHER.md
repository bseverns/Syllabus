# Teacher Guide — L07 Iteration and Crit

## Block A — Concept & Demo
- Introduce the art/design idea of the day; show 2–3 references.
- Live demo: parametric technique in OpenSCAD (and optional p5.js for 2D → SVG).

## Block B — Studio
- Circulate with the **crit funnel**: ask about intent → rules → settings → artifact.
- Encourage small, fast prints during exploration; reserve quality modes for finals.

## Prep & Materials
- Pre-slice a small sample for the day's technique.
- Have `activities/parametric-templates` open on station machines.
- Maintain a visible wall timeline of works‑in‑progress.

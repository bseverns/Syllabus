# Student Brief — L01 Parametric Thinking

## Block A (Learn)
- Capture **3 rules** you will use today (variables or constraints).
- Duplicate the demo and push it one step further (change a variable, mapping, or transformation).

## Block B (Make)
- Produce 1–3 study prints (draft mode). Photograph and log settings in your process doc.
- Add a 2–3 sentence reflection: What rule created the most interesting effect?

## Deliverables
- Parametric source (SCAD or p5.js) + STL.
- One photo of each study print, labeled with settings.
- Exit ticket: short response to the prompt.

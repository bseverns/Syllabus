# Rubric — Final Artwork (100 pts)
- **Concept & Intent (25):** Clear artistic idea and parameter rules.
- **Craft & Print Quality (20):** Clean surfaces; intentional layer/extrusion effects.
- **Material & Light (20):** Effective use of thickness/infill/orientation for visual impact.
- **Iteration Evidence (20):** Process log with studies, parameters, and photos.
- **Presentation (15):** Stable display, considered scale, wall label with succinct statement.
